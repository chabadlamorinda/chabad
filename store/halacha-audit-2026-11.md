# Halacha audit of the practical-law guides (Nov 13, 2026)

Every guide in the Shabbat and holidays, kosher, life cycle, mourning, prayer and home categories (475 guides) was read by an automated proofreader and its claims were checked against Chabad.org (or the Shulchan Aruch HaRav, Rambam or Mishnah as presented there). Unsupported or contested claims were corrected or softened to "ask the Rabbi". This is a proofreading check, NOT a halachic ruling: the Rabbi's review is still needed. Each edit below was made in English, Hebrew and Russian.
Guides checked: 448. Guides corrected: 153.
