# Questions for the Rabbi from the 200 new guides (round 10)

Each question comes from a reviewer reading the guides against Chabad.org. Answer in the chat and Claude updates the guide. Until you answer, each guide says "ask the Rabbi" or leaves the point out.


## From review 1
1. **Girls and young children lighting** (who-lights): is there a Chabad custom on girls lighting their own menorah? The guide now says "ask the Rabbi". Recommendation: keep it until the Rabbi gives the custom.
2. **The Rebbe's view on straight-arm menorahs**: Chabad.org does present it, citing Rashi and Maimonides' drawing. I added one sentence to the menorah-campaign-history FAQ. Recommendation: approve. The kosher-menorah guide could carry the same sentence.
3. **No fasting, no eulogies and no Tachanun on Chanukah** (is-work): this is standard halacha (Shulchan Aruch 670, 683). Recommendation: approve as written.
4. **Lighting after midnight with everyone asleep** (missed-lighting): Chabad.org says to light without a blessing if two or three people cannot be woken. The guide says this is a view and to ask the Rabbi. Recommendation: approve. The Rabbi may state his ruling instead.
5. **A guide on menorah fire safety** does not exist. Recommendation: write it as a practical, non-medical guide if the Rabbi wants one.
6. **Burning leftovers "in a fireproof dish outdoors or in a fireplace"** is a practical suggestion, not from Chabad.org. Recommendation: approve. It is only a way to carry out the Chabad.org custom of burning them.
7. **Menorah campaign "firsts"** (Manhattan 1973, Philadelphia 1974, San Francisco 1975): the guide says "among the first". Recommendation: approve. The Kremlin 1991 item was rightly left out.
8. **Chana and her seven sons for families**: Chabad.org tells it as true and the guide tells it gently. Recommendation: publish.
9. **The Hasmonean crown**: the guide gives Chabad.org's criticism (Ramban) and adds "some defend them, ask the Rabbi". Recommendation: approve.
10. **Second Temple dates**: no calendar years are given. Recommendation: keep it that way.
11. **Electric menorah in a hospital** (menorahs-in-dark-times): the guide says "ask the Rabbi". Recommendation: keep it, unless the Rabbi wants a standard answer, for example "light an electric menorah without a blessing and fulfil the mitzvah through a family member at home".
12. **Joshua ben Gamla's schools**: Chabad.org places them in Shlomtzion's era, while historians date him later. Recommendation: keep it, since it follows Chabad.org, or drop the sentence if the Rabbi prefers.

## From review 3

7. Grandmothers' honors. I replaced "an aliyah for a grandmother" and "passing the Torah down the generations" with "a blessing or a few words at the meal; ask the Rabbi". Recommendation: say whether you offer any other honor for grandmothers (for example a speech, or reading a blessing at the meal).
8. Bat Mitzvah Club. It is stated only from programs.json ("girls join the Bat Mitzvah Club"). Please confirm it is running this year.
9. Visits. The guides say the Rabbi and Rebbetzin visit the sick at home, in hospital and in care facilities, with no visit time promised. Recommendation: keep.
10. Outside this batch. The existing Hebrew guide learn/chanukah-menorah.md has בִּזְמַן הַזֶּה (should be בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה). The existing Hebrew guides learn/shehecheyanu.md and learn/blessings.md have לַזְּמַן הַזֶּה (Chabad nusach: לִזְמַן הַזֶּה). I did not touch them because they are not in my assignment. Recommendation: fix them in a follow-up.
