## English

**App name (max 30):** My Pushka · Chabad Lamorinda

**Short description (max 80):**
Drop coins daily, watch your pushka fill, then give to Chabad of Lamorinda.

**Full description:**
My Pushka is a digital tzedakah box from Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, California.

Make giving a daily habit:
• Drop a coin into your pushka any time, and watch it fill with gold as you get closer to your goal.
• Build a weekday streak and get gentle reminders, including before Shabbat candle lighting.
• When it's full, empty it to Chabad of Lamorinda in one tap. Your card is saved securely with Stripe and charged automatically. No forms, no redirects.

Give your way:
• Choose from our funds: General Fund, Chai Club, Jewish Preschool Scholarships, Bay Kosher Market, Building Campaign, Holiday Programs, Synagogue, End of Year Campaign, Children Events and Pledge.
• One-time or monthly gifts, with a dedication or note, and an optional processing-fee add-on.
• Support the new Center for Jewish Life, sponsor a Kiddush or holiday dinner, or give by Zelle, Venmo, check, stock or donor advised fund.
• Receipts are emailed automatically. Chabad of Lamorinda is a 501(c)(3), Tax ID 47-4642750.

Inspiration along the way:
• 40 segulos, prayers and customs in English and Hebrew, with a Give button on each one.
• Today's Tehillim, the Traveler's Prayer, blessing your children, and the Rebbe's teachings.
• A Maaser calculator, candle-lighting times, the Hebrew date, and multiple pushkas for the whole family.

Fully available in English and Hebrew (עברית), with right-to-left layout.

Your privacy matters: no ads, no tracking, and we never see your card number.
