# Look-over checklist for jewishlamorinda.com

Do this on your phone first, then on a computer. Take your time. For anything odd, send Claude a screenshot and say what you expected. Nothing here changes the old chabadoflamorinda.com site.

## 1. First look (5 minutes)
- [ ] Open https://jewishlamorinda.com. Does the top of the page show the right banner (Chanukah menorah lighting)? Is the text readable on your phone?
- [ ] Tap the language buttons: English, Hebrew, Russian. Does each page switch fully (menus, buttons, banners)?
- [ ] Scroll the home page. Are the phone number, address and Shabbat times right?
- [ ] Open the menu. Does every item open a page (nothing blank or "not found")?

## 2. Shabbat and this week
- [ ] /shabbat: are Kabbalat Shabbat, Shabbat morning and Kiddush times right? (Candle lighting is worked out by itself.)
- [ ] /this-week: are the parsha, Shabbat times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, and events right?
- [ ] /parsha: this week's portion with the Rebbe's teaching. Read one story. Does it sound right to you?

## 3. Events and RSVP
- [ ] Open the Chanukah menorah lighting event. Check date, time, place, picture.
- [ ] Do a test RSVP with your own name and email. Did you get an email? Did one reach the Rabbi area (rabbi desk, RSVPs)?
- [ ] Tap "Print a flyer". Does it print nicely?
- [ ] Add to calendar: does it land in your phone calendar with the right time?

## 4. Programs
- [ ] /programs: Preschool, Hebrew School and the rest are listed. CKids Afterschool and Summer Camp are not listed (not running this year).
- [ ] Open Preschool and Hebrew School. Are the days, times and prices right? Does the sign-up form open?

## 5. Giving (do not finish a real payment)
- [ ] /donate: open it, pick an amount, go as far as Stripe's page, then go back. Does it show Chabad of Lamorinda?
- [ ] /sponsor-a-kiddush and /chai-club open and read well.
- [ ] /legacy: read it. Open the guide "Sample Bequest Language" and try "Write your own wording".

## 6. Reaching you
- [ ] On /learn/mezuzah, tap Call, Text and WhatsApp. Do they open your phone with the right number and message?
- [ ] Send a test message through "Talk to the Rabbi". Did the email arrive?
- [ ] Ask the chat bubble two questions (for example "When is Shabbat service?" and "How do I put up a mezuzah?"). Are the answers right?

## 7. The Learn library
- [ ] /learn shows subjects, not one huge list. Open one subject. Are the guides in a sensible order?
- [ ] Search for a topic you know. Does it find it?
- [ ] Open three guides you know well. Does anything say something you would not say?

## 8. Accounts and the Rabbi area
- [ ] Sign in at /account with your email (a code is emailed). Add a yahrzeit as a test.
- [ ] Open https://jewishlamorinda.com/admin with your password. Is everything there (RSVPs, requests, weekly email, finances)?
- [ ] Weekly email: Rabbi area, Website, Weekly email, "Send me a test". Check Shabbat times and the joke.

## 9. Before you say "go ahead"
- [ ] The old site chabadoflamorinda.com still works (it is untouched).
- [ ] Google Analytics and Search Console set up (so you can see visitors from day one).
- [ ] Google Business Profile link changed to jewishlamorinda.com.
- [ ] Optional last step, only when you decide: point chabadoflamorinda.com to the new site (Claude walks you through it; old addresses already map to new pages).
