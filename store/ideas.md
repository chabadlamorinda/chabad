# 30 ideas to keep people coming back and giving small amounts

Each idea is built to do one of three jobs: **bring them back every day**, **remind them at the right moment**, or **make a small gift feel meaningful**.
Status: ✅ built now · ⏭ ready to build next (say the word) · 🧩 needs something from you (content, a decision or a native app).

## Daily habit
1. ✅ **Seven-day coin strip.** Seven glowing dots show which days you gave. Missing a day is visible, so you want to fill it.
2. ✅ **Daily auto-coin.** Choose an amount ($1.80 and up) and the pushka fills itself every day, including days you were away. You decide when to empty it. (Settings)
3. ✅ **Thought for today.** A daily quote from the Rebbe on the home screen with a one-tap "drop a coin in response".
4. ✅ **Drop with an intention.** Health, family, parnassah, safe travels, good news, thank you Hashem, refuah. The reason shows in your history. People give more often when the coin means something.
5. ✅ **Weekday streak that Shabbat never breaks.** (Already there, now with milestone badges.)
6. ⏭ **Streak rescue.** If you are about to lose a streak, one reminder that evening: "Drop a coin to keep your 12-day streak."
7. ⏭ **Morning blessing coin.** An optional 7:30 am notification with the day's Tehillim and a coin button.
8. ⏭ **Weekly summary.** Sunday notification: "You gave $14.40 in 8 coins this week."

## Reminders that fit real life
9. ✅ **Before Shabbat.** On by default; turn off any time.
10. ✅ **Yahrzeit reminders** a month, a week and a day before. Editable.
11. ✅ **Suggested reminders:** good morning coin, today's Tehillim, evening gratitude, Friday coin with the kids, Rosh Chodesh, Chai day (the 18th).
12. ✅ **Coming up.** The next Jewish occasion within 30 days (Rosh Hashanah, Erev Yom Kippur, Sukkos, Chanukah, Tu BiShvat, Purim, Pesach, Shavuos, Tisha B'Av) with the matching tradition and a one-tap gift.
13. ⏭ **Birthdays and anniversaries.** Add a name and date; a week before, "Give tzedakah in honor of Rachel's birthday."
14. ⏭ **Travel coin.** Before a trip, a reminder for the traditional coin for a safe journey.
15. ⏭ **Erev Rosh Chodesh and fast days** as optional reminders.

## Recognition and fun
16. ✅ **Achievements.** 11 badges (first coin, Chai coins, a full week, Chai streak, pushka emptied, monthly giver, Chai Club, remembering) with your totals.
17. ✅ **Chai Club honor board** with Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum medals, in the app and on your website.
18. ⏭ **Seasonal pushka skins.** A menorah glow in Chanukah, a Purim mask, a sukkah in Sukkos. A small visual treat that changes with the calendar.
19. ⏭ **Sound packs.** Classic clink, soft chime, or a niggun hum when the pushka is emptied.
20. ⏭ **Level-up celebration.** A short animation when someone crosses into a higher Chai Club level.

## Community
21. ⏭ **Community counter.** "This week our community dropped 1,248 coins" on the home screen. Seeing others give makes people join in.
22. ⏭ **Matching days.** You announce "a donor will match every coin today"; the app shows a banner and sends a notification.
23. ⏭ **Family pushka.** One pushka several family members can drop coins into, with each person's name on the coins.
24. ⏭ **Kids' mode.** Big coin buttons, stickers, and a parent approval before any charge.
25. ⏭ **Sponsor my streak.** A friend pledges $1 for every day of your streak.

## Meaningful small gifts
26. ⏭ **Cause of the month.** A featured cause (a family in need, a school fund) with a progress bar; coins can be aimed at it.
27. ⏭ **Impact notes.** You write a short thank-you ("your coins fed 40 families this Shabbos"); it appears in the app and as a notification.
28. ⏭ **Gift in honor with a card.** Send an email card to the person honored (birthday, recovery, simcha).
29. ⏭ **Yearly giving statement.** A tidy summary of gifts by year for tax time, downloadable.
30. 🧩 **Home-screen widget and lock-screen coin.** A one-tap coin from the phone's home screen. Needs the native Android and iPhone apps.

## Notes
- Small gifts are collected in the pushka and charged once, because card fees make charging $1.80 on its own wasteful. The app already offers "cover the processing fee".
- Items marked ⏭ that send notifications need the database and always-on server from `store/rabbi-setup.md`.
