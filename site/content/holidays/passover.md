Passover celebrates our Exodus from Egypt and the birth of the Jewish people. At the **Seder** we tell the story, eat matzah and bitter herbs, drink four cups of wine, and ask the Four Questions.

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
- **Community Passover Seder**: reserve your place
- **Seder-to-go kits** for your home Seder
- Order handmade **shmurah matzah**
- **Sell your chametz** through the Rabbi
- **Model Matzah Bakery** for children

## Getting ready
Clean the home of chametz (leavened foods), search for chametz the night before Passover, and burn it the next morning.
