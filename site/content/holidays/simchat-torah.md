---
title: Shemini Atzeret & Simchat Torah
hebrew: שמחת תורה
description: Dance with the Torah! Simchat Torah hakafot for the whole family at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette.
---
Right after Sukkot come **Shemini Atzeret** and **Simchat Torah**, when we finish reading the Torah and start again from the beginning. We celebrate with **hakafot**: joyful dancing with the Torah scrolls, with flags and treats for the children.

Every Jew is equal on Simchat Torah: we dance with the Torah closed, because its joy belongs to everyone, whatever they know. Join us!
