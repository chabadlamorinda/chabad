On Shavuot we celebrate receiving the Torah at Mount Sinai. Every man, woman and child is encouraged to come to synagogue to hear the **Ten Commandments**, just as the whole Jewish people stood at Sinai. It is customary to eat dairy foods.

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
Hear the Ten Commandments, then celebrate at our **ice cream and cheesecake party**. Bring the kids!
