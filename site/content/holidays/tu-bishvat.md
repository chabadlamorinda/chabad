Tu BiShvat, the 15th of Shevat, is the New Year for Trees. We eat fruits, especially the seven fruits of the Land of Israel (wheat, barley, grapes, figs, pomegranates, olives and dates), and remember that each person is like a tree, growing and giving fruit. Join our Tu BiShvat family celebration.
