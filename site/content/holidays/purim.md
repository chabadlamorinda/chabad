Purim celebrates the rescue of the Jewish people in ancient Persia, told in the Book of Esther. It is the most joyful day of the year.

## The four mitzvahs of Purim
1. Hear the **Megillah** read, at night and again in the day
2. Send **mishloach manot**: a gift of two ready-to-eat foods to a friend
3. Give **matanot la'evyonim**: gifts to at least two people in need
4. Enjoy a festive **Purim meal**

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
Megillah readings, a Purim party for the whole community and a kids' hamantashen bake. Costumes encouraged!
