Lag BaOmer, the 33rd day of the Omer, celebrates the life of the great sage Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and the end of a plague among Rabbi Akiva's students. It is a day of bonfires, parades, outings and joy. Join our Lag BaOmer BBQ in Lafayette.
