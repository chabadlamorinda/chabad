Sukkot celebrates G-d's protection of our ancestors in the desert. For seven days we eat in the **sukkah**, a hut with a roof of branches, and each day (except Shabbat) we shake the **lulav and etrog**, the Four Kinds.

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
- Community Sukkot holiday dinner in the sukkah
- Sushi in the Sukkah for families, and Scotch and Steaks in the Sukkah for the Men's Club
- Order your own **lulav and etrog** set
- Come shake the lulav at our sukkah any day of the holiday
