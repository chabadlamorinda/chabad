Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement, is the holiest day of the year: a 25-hour fast of prayer, reflection and return, when every Jew's soul shines. It begins with **Kol Nidre** at sunset and ends with the **Neilah** prayer and a final blast of the shofar.

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
Yom Kippur services are held at the **Lafayette Park Hotel**, with a warm, understandable service and the Rabbi's explanations. **Yizkor**, the memorial prayer for loved ones, is said on Yom Kippur day; add names to our Yizkor booklet. A break-fast follows Neilah.

## Before the fast
Eat a festive meal, light candles before sunset, and bless your children. Many people give charity and ask forgiveness from friends and family.
