Chanukah, the Festival of Lights, celebrates the victory of the few Maccabees over the mighty Greek army and the miracle of the oil that burned for eight days in the Holy Temple. Each night we add a candle, until all eight shine.

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
- **Grand menorah lighting** and community Chanukah celebration, with latkes, donuts and music
- **Make your own menorah** for kids
- **Be a Chanukah lamplighter**: sponsor a night of the community menorah

## Lighting at home
Light after nightfall (on Friday, before Shabbat candles; on Saturday night, after Havdalah). Place the menorah in a window or doorway to share the light. Say the blessings, light from left to right, and enjoy foods fried in oil.
