Rosh Hashanah is the Jewish New Year, the birthday of humanity, when we crown G-d as King and ask for a sweet new year. Its central mitzvah is hearing the **shofar** on both days (except on Shabbat).

## With Chabad of Lamorinda
- **Rosh Hashanah services** with explanations and page announcements, so everyone can follow
- **Shofar and Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir**
- **Community Rosh Hashanah dinner** in Lafayette
- **Dot cakes and more** for the kids

No membership and no tickets. Reservations help us save you a seat.

## At home
Light holiday candles, say Kiddush, dip apple in honey and eat a round challah. Wish everyone *Shanah Tovah u'Metukah*: a good and sweet year.
