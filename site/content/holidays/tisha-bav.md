title: Tisha B'Av
hebrew: תשעה באב
description: Tisha B'Av, the fast of the 9th of Av, with Chabad of Lamorinda.
---
Tisha B'Av, the 9th of Av, is a day of fasting and mourning for the destruction of both Holy Temples in Jerusalem and other tragedies in Jewish history. We read the Book of Lamentations (Eicha) and look forward to the coming of Moshiach, when this day will become a day of celebration.
