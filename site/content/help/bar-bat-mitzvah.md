A bar or bat mitzvah is a milestone your child will remember for life. At Chabad of Lamorinda, Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski help families in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and nearby towns prepare for a bar or bat mitzvah that is joyful, meaningful and personal. We welcome every family, whatever your background, and adults who never had one are welcome too. Call **925-384-7242** to start planning.

## When is the bar or bat mitzvah?

A boy becomes bar mitzvah at **13**, and a girl becomes bat mitzvah at **12**, according to the Hebrew calendar. The date is based on the Hebrew birthday, which may be different from the English one. Use our [bar and bat mitzvah date calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah), or ask the Rabbi to confirm.

Becoming bar or bat mitzvah happens automatically on that date. The celebration is how we mark it.

## Step by step

1. **Call the Rabbi** about a year before, or earlier. We will talk about your family, your child and your hopes.
2. **Confirm the date** using the Hebrew calendar.
3. **Start preparing.** Lessons are tailored to your child. See our [bar and bat mitzvah program](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah).
4. **Plan the celebration**, whether a Torah reading in synagogue, a family gathering or a party.

## Preparation for boys

- **Tefillin.** Learning to put on tefillin, a mitzvah a boy begins at his bar mitzvah. The Rabbi can help you get a kosher pair and teach your son to wrap them.
- **Torah reading.** Being called to the Torah for an aliyah, and if he wishes, reading from the Torah or the Haftorah.
- **Learning.** Understanding what it means to be a Jewish man, and a short speech on the Torah portion.

## Preparation for girls

- **Learning** about the mitzvot of a Jewish woman, such as lighting Shabbat candles, and Jewish role models.
- **A meaningful project**, such as a mitzvah or charity project, or a study of a Jewish topic.
- **A celebration** with a talk by the bat mitzvah girl, in a setting that is right for your family.

The Rebbetzin often works closely with bat mitzvah girls.

## For adults who never had one

It is never too late. Many adults, men and women, celebrate a bar or bat mitzvah later in life. For a man, it can be as simple as putting on tefillin and being called to the Torah. The Rabbi will be honored to help you mark this moment.

Children in our [Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) build a strong foundation in Hebrew reading and Jewish knowledge, which makes bar and bat mitzvah preparation easier and more meaningful.

## Common questions

### How do I find my child's bar or bat mitzvah date?
It is based on the Hebrew birthday. Use our [date calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah) or call the Rabbi to confirm.

### Do we need to be members or observant?
No. Chabad of Lamorinda welcomes every Jewish family. We meet you where you are.

### How long does preparation take?
It depends on your child. Many families begin about a year ahead, but we can work with shorter timelines. Call us to talk it through.

### Can an adult have a bar or bat mitzvah?
Yes. It is never too late, and many adults find it deeply moving.

### Where can the celebration take place?
We can help you plan a Torah reading in synagogue and a celebration that fits your family. Call 925-384-7242 to talk about options.
