If you, your parent or a neighbor is alone, unwell, getting older or having a hard time, Chabad of Lamorinda is the place to turn. Every Jew in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda is welcome, no membership needed, and nobody has to carry a hard time alone.

## Ask for help

Whatever you need, we will do our best to give it, to anyone who needs it. Tell us what is going on. It is private: only the Rabbi and the Rebbetzin see it. You can ask for yourself or for someone else.

- A visit at home, in the hospital or in a care home
- A phone call or friendly company
- A ride to an appointment or to services
- A Shabbat or holiday meal or package
- Help with food or groceries
- Financial help
- A prayer or a blessing

<p class="btns"><a class="btn gold" href="/forms/need-help">Ask for help</a> <a class="btn ghost" href="tel:9253847242">Call 925-384-7242</a></p>

If you would rather talk first, call the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin (925-384-7241). In an emergency, call 911.

## Are you a senior?

You do not need to be a member, and you do not need to be able to come to us. We would be glad to visit, to call, to bring Shabbat to you, and to connect you with people who care. See also [Jewish seniors in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-seniors-in-lamorinda) and [Jewish life in senior living](/learn/jewish-life-in-senior-living-lamorinda).

## Someone sick or in the hospital

Visiting the sick, bikur cholim, is a great mitzvah. The Rabbi and Rebbetzin visit at home, in the hospital and in care facilities. Add a name to the [Tehillim prayer list](/tehillim), and read [when someone is sick](/help/someone-is-sick).

## Financial help

We offer whatever is needed, to whoever needs it. Hard times can happen to anyone. If you are struggling, whether it is food, a bill, tuition or an emergency, tell us. Nothing is too small to ask about, and it stays between you and us.

## Volunteer

Visit a senior, call someone who is alone, drive to an appointment or deliver a Shabbat package. An hour makes a difference. You can also add a gift to the Chesed Fund when you sign up.

## Sponsor visits and help for those in need

Your gift to the Chesed Fund goes to visiting seniors and the sick and to helping families in need. Give any amount, once or monthly, or in honor or in memory of someone. Sponsors can choose to sponsor a visit, a Shabbat package, a ride or a family's need.

## Common questions

### Where can a senior in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda get help?

Call Chabad of Lamorinda at 925-384-7242 or [ask for help online](/forms/need-help). We can arrange a visit, a call, a ride or a Shabbat meal. You do not need to be a member.

### Who visits the sick in the hospital in Lamorinda?

The Rabbi and Rebbetzin visit at home, in the hospital and in care facilities. Call 925-384-7242 or [ask for a visit](/forms/need-help).

### Can Chabad help with financial need?

Tell us what you need at [the help form](/forms/need-help) or by phone, 925-384-7242. It is private, and nothing is too small to ask about.

### How can I volunteer to visit seniors or the sick?

[Sign up to volunteer](/forms/volunteer) and tick visiting seniors and the sick, phone calls or driving.

### How can I sponsor visits for those in need?

Give to the [Chesed Fund](/donate?fund=chesed#give), once or monthly, in any amount, in honor or in memory of someone.
