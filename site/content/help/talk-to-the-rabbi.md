Sometimes you just need to talk to a Rabbi. Whatever is on your mind, a question about Judaism, a decision you are facing, a hard time, a joyful milestone, or something you have never told anyone, Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski of Chabad of Lamorinda are here for you. Call **925-384-7242**, email info@chabadoflamorinda.com, or reach out through [/contact](/contact). Our conversations are confidential, and no question is too small.

## How to reach us

- **Phone:** 925-384-7242. If we cannot answer, leave a message and we will call you back.
- **Email:** info@chabadoflamorinda.com
- **Online:** [/contact](/contact)
- **In person:** Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, CA 94549. Please call ahead to set a time.

We serve Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, and nearby Pleasant Hill and Martinez. If you live farther away, call anyway. We will help however we can.

**In an emergency, call 911 first.** The Rabbi is not a substitute for emergency services or for professional medical, legal or mental health care.

## What you can talk to the Rabbi about

Truly anything. People reach out to us about:

- **Life events:** a [new baby](/help/new-baby), a [bar or bat mitzvah](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah), a [wedding](/help/getting-married).
- **Illness and loss:** [someone who is sick](/help/someone-is-sick), [end of life](/help/end-of-life), [a death in the family](/help/someone-passed-away), Kaddish and yahrzeit.
- **Relationships:** [marriage and family](/help/shalom-bayit), [disputes](/help/disputes).
- **Hard times:** [grief, loneliness, addiction or crisis](/help/grief-and-hard-times).
- **Jewish questions:** [Am I Jewish?](/help/jewish-identity), kosher, Shabbat, prayer, holidays, mezuzahs and tefillin.
- **Big questions:** faith, G-d, meaning, doubt and purpose.

## What to expect

- **A warm welcome.** It does not matter how much you know or how observant you are. Jews of every background, and interfaith families, are welcome.
- **No judgment.** The Rabbi will listen first.
- **Confidentiality.** What you share stays private, unless someone's safety is at risk.
- **Practical help.** Whether you need an answer, a plan, a prayer or a referral, we will help you find the next step.
- **Honest Jewish guidance.** We share the traditional Jewish view, warmly and clearly. For questions about your specific situation, the Rabbi will guide you personally.

## Why talk to a Rabbi?

The Mishnah teaches, "Make for yourself a Rav," a teacher you can turn to. Having a Rabbi in your life means having someone who knows you, cares about you, and can help you bring Jewish wisdom to whatever you are going through. That is what we want to be for you.

## Common questions

### Do I need to be a member to talk to the Rabbi?
No. Anyone can reach out, any time. There is no charge to talk to the Rabbi.

### Is my question too small?
No question is too small. If it matters to you, it matters to us.

### Can I talk to the Rebbetzin instead?
Yes. Many people, especially women, prefer to speak with Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski. Just ask when you call.

### Is it confidential?
Yes. Your conversation is private, except where someone's safety is at risk.

### What if I am not religious?
That is completely fine. The Rabbi is here for every Jew, and for anyone with a question about Judaism.
