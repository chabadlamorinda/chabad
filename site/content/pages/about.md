Chabad of Lamorinda is a home for every Jew in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, and for families from nearby Pleasant Hill, Martinez, Walnut Creek and all of Contra Costa County. People of every background come to learn about their roots, celebrate Shabbat and the holidays, and find a warm, caring community that feels like one big family.

Our services are traditional, in a casual and joyful atmosphere, with a bilingual prayer book. Children are always welcome. There is no membership and no dues: everyone belongs.

## Our Rabbi and Rebbetzin
**Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski** is a warm, caring and energetic teacher who brings Torah to life in a modern, relevant way. **Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski** directs Lamorinda Jewish Preschool and Lamorinda Hebrew School. She holds a Master's degree in Education and has more than ten years of experience in Jewish education.

Rabbi Yisroel and Tzipora are emissaries (shluchim) of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, of righteous memory.

## Our mission
To serve the Jewish communities of Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda with an open door for strengthening Jewish identity, through Torah classes, Shabbat and holiday celebrations, family and social programs, education for children of every age, and help in times of joy and of need. We follow the Chabad-Lubavitch way of joy, scholarship and unconditional love for every Jew.
