---
title: Privacy
description: How Chabad of Lamorinda handles your information.
---
We collect only what you choose to give us: your name, email, phone and address, your family's names and dates if you add them, yahrzeits, and your event RSVPs. We use it to run our programs, send the reminders you ask for, and stay in touch. We never sell or share your information.

Yahrzeits you save are private to you unless you tick "Share with the Rabbi". Card payments are handled by Stripe; card numbers never reach our server.

You can update or delete your account at any time from [My account](/account), or by emailing info@chabadoflamorinda.com.
