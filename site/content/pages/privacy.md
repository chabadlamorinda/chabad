---
title: Privacy
description: How Chabad of Lamorinda handles your information.
---
We collect only what you choose to give us: your name, email, phone and address, your family's names and dates if you add them, yahrzeits, and your event RSVPs. We use it to run our programs, send the reminders you ask for, and stay in touch. We never sell or share your information.

Yahrzeits you save are private to you unless you tick "Share with the Rabbi". Card payments are handled by Stripe; card numbers never reach our server. In a fundraising campaign, the donor wall shows only the name, amount and words you chose to show (or nothing, if you give anonymously). Ambassadors' email and phone, and pledges' contact details, are seen only by the Rabbi. We keep a record of gifts (name, contact details, amount and date) to send receipts and year-end tax letters; only the Rabbi sees it.

Like most websites, we note which pages of our site are visited and which website or link brought people here (for example Google, Facebook or a flyer's QR code), using an anonymous id saved in your browser (a first-party cookie); visit history is kept for up to 3 years. We do not keep IP addresses ourselves. We also use Google Analytics, which counts visits (pages read, kind of device, general area) so we can see whether our programs and events reach people; we do not send Google your name or email, and Google Analytics is switched off for browsers that ask not to be tracked. If you leave us your name and email (for example when you RSVP, fill in a form or write to the Rabbi), we connect your visits to your name, so the Rabbi can understand what interests you and offer you the right help, classes and opportunities. We do not track browsers that ask not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track). To have your visit history deleted, email info@chabadoflamorinda.com. We also count the words people search for on our site (without names or ids), so we know what to write next.

For each page we also note how long it was read, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the time zone your browser reports (which shows only a general area); this detail is kept for up to 13 months. It lets the Rabbi see how many people are on the website at the moment and which pages help people most. When someone reads our pages about a loss or mourning, an illness or a hard time, or a new baby, the Rabbi receives a private note about that visit (which pages, for how long), so he is ready to help if you reach out; it shows a name only if you have already left your details with us, and it is never shared.

When you type your name, email or phone into one of our forms (and the amount on the giving page), it is saved as you type, even before you press Send, so that if you are interrupted the Rabbi can follow up once to see whether he can help. Every such form says so right under it. Nothing else you type (for example a message, a question or names for prayers) is saved until you send it. Details from a form that was not sent are deleted after 90 days. None of this happens in browsers that ask not to be tracked.

Our weekly email goes only to people who signed up with their email address; each new subscriber gets a welcome message with a link to unsubscribe in one click, and anyone who unsubscribed must confirm from their own inbox before they can sign up again. It notes whether each email was opened and which links were clicked, so we can make it more useful; every email has a link to unsubscribe in one click and to choose its language.

When you are signed in, we note which of our guides, holiday pages, programs and events you look at, so the Rabbi can invite your family to things that fit you. Allergies and dietary needs you add are seen only by our staff. You can turn suggestions off at any time in My account ("Suggest events and classes that fit my family").

You can update or delete your account at any time from [My account](/account), or by emailing info@chabadoflamorinda.com.
