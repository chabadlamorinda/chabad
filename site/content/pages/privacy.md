---
title: Privacy
description: How Chabad of Lamorinda handles your information.
---
We collect only what you choose to give us: your name, email, phone and address, your family's names and dates if you add them, yahrzeits, and your event RSVPs. We use it to run our programs, send the reminders you ask for, and stay in touch. We never sell or share your information.

Yahrzeits you save are private to you unless you tick "Share with the Rabbi". Card payments are handled by Stripe; card numbers never reach our server.

Like most websites, we note which pages of our site are visited, using an anonymous id saved in your browser (a first-party cookie); visit history is kept for up to 3 years. We do not keep IP addresses and do not use outside tracking companies. If you leave us your name and email (for example when you RSVP, fill in a form or write to the Rabbi), we connect your visits to your name, so the Rabbi can understand what interests you and offer you the right help, classes and opportunities. We do not track browsers that ask not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track). To have your visit history deleted, email info@chabadoflamorinda.com.

When you are signed in, we note which of our guides, holiday pages, programs and events you look at, so the Rabbi can invite your family to things that fit you. Allergies and dietary needs you add are seen only by our staff. You can turn suggestions off at any time in My account ("Suggest events and classes that fit my family").

You can update or delete your account at any time from [My account](/account), or by emailing info@chabadoflamorinda.com.
