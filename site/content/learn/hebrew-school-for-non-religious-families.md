Your family does not need to be religious to send your child to Hebrew school. At Chabad of Lamorinda, children come from every kind of Jewish home: families who keep Shabbat and families who never have, families with two Jewish parents and families with one, families who know a lot and families just starting. Every Jewish child is welcome, with no membership and no judgment.

## "But we're not religious"

Many parents in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda say this when they first call. They worry that their child will feel out of place, or that the school will expect things from them that they do not do at home.

Here is the truth: Hebrew school is for your child, and your child belongs. Chabad teaches that every Jew has a Jewish soul that is precious, whatever they practice. The Torah is called "the inheritance of the congregation of Jacob": it belongs to every Jewish child by birth, not by how observant the family is. See [not religious? You are welcome](/learn/not-religious-welcome).

## What your child will gain

- **Knowing who they are.** Children who know the stories, letters and holidays carry their Jewish identity with confidence.
- **Friends.** Other Jewish children who share their holidays and heritage.
- **Skills.** Hebrew reading with Aleph Champ, the blessings, and how to follow a prayer book.
- **Values.** Kindness, honesty, respect and tzedakah, through stories and doing.
- **A choice for later.** A child who knows Judaism can choose it as an adult. A child who never learned has nothing to choose from.

See [the benefits of Torah study for children](/learn/benefits-of-torah-study-for-children).

## What is expected of you

Nothing you cannot do. No one checks what you eat or what you do on Saturday. Teachers welcome every family as it is. If your child comes home excited to light Shabbat candles or say a blessing, you may find yourself joining in, and many parents enjoy that. But there is no pressure.

## If one parent is not Jewish

Families with one Jewish parent often ask whether their child can attend. The answer depends on the child's Jewish status, which in Jewish law follows the mother. This is a private, caring conversation, not a test. Call the Rebbetzin at 925-384-7241 or ask the Rabbi, and you will get a warm, honest answer.

## When your child asks questions you can't answer

That is a good sign. Say, "Great question. Let's find out together," and ask the teacher or look it up. Your child learns that Judaism is something you can explore at any age. Our guide to [answering "Why are we Jewish?"](/learn/answering-kids-why-are-we-jewish) can help.

## Lamorinda Hebrew School

Lamorinda Hebrew School meets on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, for children ages 3 to 13. Lessons are fun and hands-on, with games and creative projects. Tuition is $600 per child per year, including books and supplies; no membership is needed, and no child is turned away for lack of funds. See [the program](/programs/hebrew-school).

## Common questions

### Do we have to be religious to send our child to Hebrew school?
No. Lamorinda Hebrew School welcomes every Jewish child, whatever the family's level of observance, with no membership required.

### Will my child feel out of place?
Children from many kinds of homes learn together, and the teachers make every child feel at home. Lessons start from where each child is.

### Will the school try to make our family religious?
No. The school teaches your child about Judaism with warmth and joy. What your family does with it is your choice.

### My spouse is not Jewish. Can our child attend?
Call Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241 to talk about your family privately. Every family is treated with respect.

### Can we afford it?
Tuition is $600 per child per year with books and supplies, and no child is turned away for lack of funds. Ask about a payment plan or help on the [registration form](/forms/hebrew-school).
