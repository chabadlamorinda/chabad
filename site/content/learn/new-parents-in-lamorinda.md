Mazal Tov! Whether you are expecting or just came home from the hospital, Chabad of Lamorinda would love to celebrate with your family. Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski help new parents in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and nearby Pleasant Hill and Martinez with every step, from a prayer during pregnancy to a bris, a baby naming and a first preschool. There is no membership required. Call **925-384-7242**.

## While you are expecting

Many mothers say chapters of Tehillim (Psalms) during pregnancy for an easy, healthy birth, and Psalm 20 especially when labor begins. You will find the chapters on our [Tehillim page](/tehillim). Our guide to [Jewish pregnancy](/learn/jewish-pregnancy) has more customs and blessings.

## A baby boy: the bris

The bris milah takes place on the **eighth day** of the baby's life, as long as the baby is healthy. Call the Rabbi soon after the birth. He will help work out the correct date (a baby born after sunset counts from the next day), arrange a qualified mohel, and plan the honors, the blessings and a festive meal. If your son is a firstborn, ask the Rabbi whether a **pidyon haben** applies on the 31st day. See [new baby](/help/new-baby) and our [brit milah guide](/learn/brit-milah).

## A baby girl: the naming

A baby girl receives her Hebrew name when her father, or another family member, is called up to the Torah, usually at the first Torah reading after the birth, on a Monday, Thursday or Shabbat. Call us to arrange it, and celebrate by [sponsoring a Kiddush](/sponsor) for the whole community. See our [baby naming guide](/learn/baby-naming).

## Choosing a Hebrew name

A Hebrew name is the name of the soul. Many families name after a relative who has passed away, or after a figure from the Torah. The Rabbi is happy to help you find a meaningful name. See [Hebrew names](/learn/hebrew-names).

## The first weeks

- It is a custom to place a card with Psalm 121 (Shir Hamaalot) in the baby's room.
- Many families give charity in honor of the new baby.
- Say the chapters of thanks for a healthy baby and mother on the [Tehillim page](/tehillim).
- Rest. Let the community help with meals. Just ask.

## Your baby's Hebrew birthday

Every child has a Hebrew birthday as well as an English one. Find it with the [Hebrew birthday calculator](/tools/hebrew-birthday), and save your family in [your account](/account) so you can RSVP to events in one tap. Years from now, the same Hebrew date sets the bar or bat mitzvah.

## Building a Jewish home

Small things make a home Jewish: a kosher [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) on the door, Shabbat candles on Friday, a blessing for your children at the Shabbat table, and Jewish songs at bedtime. Ask the Rabbi for help with mezuzahs on the [mezuzah form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin), and read [Jewish home in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-home-in-lamorinda).

## Lamorinda Jewish Preschool

When you are ready, [Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool) in Lafayette welcomes children from **12 months to 4 years**, in two classes: 12 to 24 months and 2 to 4 years. It is warm and play-based, inspired by Reggio Emilia and guided by Jewish values. Hours are Monday to Thursday 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM and Friday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Tuition is $17,000 for the school year, or 10 monthly payments of $1,700, and need-based scholarships are available. Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski directs the school. [Schedule a tour](/forms/preschool).

For summer, [Lamorinda Jewish Summer Camp](/programs/summer-camp) is for ages 2 to 4. At age 3, children can join the early childhood track of [Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) on Sundays. See also [choosing a Jewish preschool](/learn/choosing-a-jewish-preschool).

## Common questions

### Who can I call for a bris in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda?
Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 soon after the birth. He helps set the date, arranges a qualified mohel and plans the ceremony.

### How do we name a baby girl in Lamorinda?
At the Torah reading at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, usually the first one after the birth. Call us to arrange it.

### Is there a Jewish preschool for babies near Lafayette?
Yes. Lamorinda Jewish Preschool has a class for 12 to 24 months and a class for 2 to 4 years. [Schedule a tour](/forms/preschool) or call 925-384-7242.

### We are an interfaith family. Can we have a bris or naming?
Please call the Rabbi. He welcomes every family and will talk with you about your situation warmly and personally.
