Christianity is a world religion based on the life, teachings, death and resurrection of Jesus of Nazareth, whom Christians believe is the Son of G-d and the savior of humanity. With about 2.3 billion followers, it is the largest religion in the world, and most of the neighbors and coworkers we meet in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda who are religious are Christian.

This guide explains Christianity the way Christians themselves would recognize it. It is meant to inform, not to argue.

## Where Christianity began

Christianity began in the first century in the Land of Israel, then part of the Roman Empire. Jesus was born a Jew, taught in Jewish villages, and his first followers were Jews. Within a few decades his message spread to non-Jews across the Roman world, and over the following centuries Christianity grew into a separate religion. In the fourth century it became widely accepted in the Roman Empire, and from there it spread across Europe, the Americas, Africa and Asia.

## What Christians believe

Christians hold a set of core beliefs, though different churches explain them in different ways:

- **One G-d in three persons.** The Trinity is the belief that there is one G-d who exists as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
- **The incarnation.** Christians believe that G-d became human in Jesus, who is both fully divine and fully human.
- **Death and resurrection.** Christians believe Jesus was crucified, died, and rose from the dead on the third day.
- **Salvation.** Christians believe that through Jesus, people are forgiven their sins and offered eternal life with G-d. Churches differ on how this works, for example on the roles of faith, grace and good deeds.
- **The Second Coming.** Christians believe Jesus will return at the end of days to judge the living and the dead.

Many churches summarize their faith in a creed, such as the Nicene Creed (formed in the fourth century) and the Apostles' Creed.

## The Christian Bible

The Christian Bible has two parts. The Old Testament is the Hebrew Bible (the Jewish Tanach), which Christians read as the first part of their scripture. The New Testament is a collection of writings about Jesus and the early church. Read more in [the New Testament, explained](/learn/the-new-testament-overview).

## The three main branches

Christianity is usually divided into three large groups:

1. **Roman Catholic.** The largest branch, led by the Pope in Rome, with a worldwide structure of priests and bishops.
2. **Eastern Orthodox.** Churches mainly in Eastern Europe, Russia, Greece and the Middle East, each led by its own patriarch or bishops, with deep roots in the early church. Many Russian-speaking neighbors belong to this tradition.
3. **Protestant.** Hundreds of denominations that grew out of the Reformation of the 1500s, such as Lutheran, Baptist, Methodist, Presbyterian and many others.

For more detail, see [Christian denominations explained](/learn/christian-denominations).

## A Jewish note

Jews do not share Christian beliefs. Judaism teaches that G-d is one and absolutely indivisible, and that G-d does not take human form (see the [thirteen principles of faith](/learn/thirteen-principles-of-faith)). Jewish law also forbids Jews to take part in the religious rites of other faiths. At the same time, Judaism teaches that righteous people of every nation have a share in the World to Come, and that non-Jews are guided by the [seven Noahide laws](/learn/noahide-laws). Judaism asks us to treat everyone with kindness and respect. For any personal question, ask the Rabbi.

## Common questions

### What is Christianity in simple terms?
Christianity is the religion of those who follow Jesus of Nazareth, believing that he is the Son of G-d who died and rose again to save humanity. It has about 2.3 billion followers worldwide.

### How many Christians are there in the world?
There are about 2.3 billion Christians, roughly three in ten people on earth, according to the Pew Research Center. It is the largest religion in the world.

### What is the Trinity?
The Trinity is the Christian belief in one G-d in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Most Christian churches hold this belief, though they describe it in different words. Judaism does not accept it, as Jewish faith holds that G-d is one and indivisible.

### What are the main branches of Christianity?
The three main branches are Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox and Protestant. Each has many traditions and communities of its own.

### Was Jesus Jewish?
Yes. Jesus was born to a Jewish family in the Land of Israel in the first century, and his first followers were Jews. Christianity became a separate religion over the following centuries.

### Do Jews and Christians believe in the same G-d?
Both believe in the G-d of Abraham who created the world, and Christians read the Hebrew Bible as scripture. They differ deeply on who G-d is and how He relates to humanity. Judaism does not accept the Trinity or the incarnation. For a deeper answer, ask the Rabbi.
