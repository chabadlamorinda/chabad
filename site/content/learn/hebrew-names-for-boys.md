Hebrew names for boys come from the patriarchs and the twelve tribes, the prophets and kings, the sages, and Yiddish. Popular choices include Avraham (אברהם), Yitzchak (יצחק), Yaakov (יעקב), David (דויד), Moshe (משה) and Daniel (דניאל). A Jewish boy is traditionally named at his bris, on the eighth day.

The Hebrew spellings and meanings follow Chabad.org's list of popular Jewish boy names, retold in our own words. Meanings of ancient names are sometimes debated.

## Patriarchs and the tribes

- **Avraham** (אברהם): exalted father.
- **Yitzchak** (יצחק): he rejoiced, or laughter.
- **Yaakov** (יעקב): heel.
- **Reuven** (ראובן): see, a son.
- **Shimon** (שמעון): G-d heard.
- **Levi** (לוי): attached.
- **Yehudah** (יהודה): thanking G-d.
- **Dan** (דן): to judge.
- **Naftali** (נפתלי): wrestled or bonded.
- **Gad** (גד): fortune.
- **Asher** (אשר): happy one.
- **Yissachar** (יששכר): reward.
- **Zevulun** (זבולון): abode.
- **Yosef** (יוסף): may G-d add.
- **Binyamin** (בנימין): son of the right hand.

## Leaders, prophets and kings

- **Moshe** (משה): to withdraw.
- **Aharon** (אהרון): light, teaching.
- **Yehoshua** (יהושע): G-d will save.
- **Shmuel** (שמואל): G-d heard.
- **David** (דויד): friend or beloved.
- **Shlomo** (שלמה): G-d gives peace.
- **Eliyahu** (אליהו): my G-d is the L-rd.
- **Yeshayahu** (ישעיהו): G-d saves.
- **Yirmiyahu** (ירמיהו): exalted G-d.
- **Daniel** (דניאל): G-d is my judge.
- **Ezra** (עזרא): help.
- **Noach** (נח): rest.

## Names of light, strength and G-d's gifts

- **Meir** (מאיר): to give light.
- **Uri** (אורי): my light.
- **Noam** (נועם): pleasantness.
- **Eitan** (איתן): mighty.
- **Boaz** (בועז): strength within.
- **Natan** (נתן): he gave.
- **Netanel** (נתנאל): G-d has given.
- **Refael** (רפאל): G-d has healed.
- **Michael** (מיכאל): who is like G-d?
- **Gavriel** (גבריאל): G-d's man.
- **Baruch** (ברוך): blessed.
- **Menachem** (מנחם): comforter.
- **Chayim** (חיים): life.
- **Shalom** (שלום): peace.

## Sages and Yiddish classics

- **Akiva** (עקיבא): heel.
- **Shneur** (שניאור): two lights.
- **Tzvi** (צבי): deer.
- **Mendel** (מענדל): comforter.
- **Zelig** (זעליג): happy one.
- **Zundel** (זונדל): sonny boy.
- **Lieber** (ליבער): beloved.
- **Shraga** (שרגא): lamp.
- **Feivish** (פייויש): light.

## How to choose

Chabad.org says parents may honor a departed relative, mark a special event, or pick any Jewish name they find beautiful. Many Jewish boys also carry a secular name. Customs differ between communities, so ask the Rabbi if you wonder about naming after relatives. See [Hebrew names](/learn/hebrew-names) and [Jewish baby names](/learn/jewish-baby-names).

## Common questions

### What are good Hebrew names for boys?
Avraham, Yitzchak, Yaakov, David, Moshe, Daniel, Meir, Noam and Eitan are popular, with many more listed above.

### What Hebrew boy names mean light?
Meir (מאיר) means to give light, Uri (אורי) means my light, and Shneur (שניאור) means two lights.

### When is a Jewish boy named?
At his bris, on the eighth day, according to Chabad.org.

### Can we name a baby after a relative?
Yes, parents often name after departed loved ones. Ask the Rabbi about specific cases.

### Do Hebrew names need to be biblical?
No. Chabad.org says any Jewish name you find beautiful may be chosen, including Yiddish names loved by many families.
