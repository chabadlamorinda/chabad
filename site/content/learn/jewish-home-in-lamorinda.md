Whether you just moved into a house in Lafayette, an apartment in Orinda or a new home in Moraga, Pleasant Hill or Martinez, a Jewish home is built one step at a time. A mezuzah on the door, Shabbat candles on Friday, a kosher dish or two, some good Jewish books on the shelf. Chabad of Lamorinda, right in Lafayette, is here to help with each step, at your own pace. No membership is needed.

## Mezuzahs: buy, check and put up

The [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) marks a Jewish home and, as Jewish tradition teaches, brings G-d's protection to everyone inside. Through our [mezuzah form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin) we can:

- **Help you buy kosher mezuzahs**, handwritten by a scribe, not printed
- **Have your mezuzahs checked** by a scribe, since parchment can fade or crack
- **Come to your home** to help put them up with the blessing

Moving in is the perfect time. Tell us how many doors you have and we will take it from there. Many people also have their mezuzahs checked when someone in the family is unwell, or in the month of Elul before Rosh Hashanah. Dedicating a new home with a small gathering is a lovely custom too: read about [chanukat habayit](/learn/chanukat-habayit).

## A kosher kitchen

Thinking about keeping kosher, or taking one more step? The Rabbi will happily help you kosher your kitchen, step by step, at your own pace. Call 925-384-7242 to talk it through, and start with our guides to [kosher basics](/learn/kosher) and the [kosher kitchen](/learn/kosher-kitchen). Questions about immersing new dishes are answered in our guide to [tevilat kelim](/learn/tevilat-kelim); ask the Rabbi about your specific dishes.

For the food itself, [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda, makes keeping kosher in the East Bay easy: kosher meat and poultry, groceries, holiday foods and Judaica, ordered online and delivered.

## Shabbat candles

Lighting candles on Friday before sunset brings the peace of Shabbat into your home. Our [Shabbat candles guide](/learn/shabbat-candles) has the blessing and instructions, and this week's candle lighting time for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda is on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat). Candlesticks, Kiddush cups, menorahs and more are available through [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher).

## Jewish books

A Jewish home needs Jewish books: a prayer book, a Chumash (the Five Books of Moses) with the weekly Torah portion, a book of Tehillim, and something for the children. Our guide to the [Jewish home library](/learn/jewish-home-library) suggests where to start. The Rabbi is glad to recommend books for your family's level and interests. Call 925-384-7242.
