## Passover in one minute

Passover (Pesach, פֶּסַח) celebrates our Exodus from Egypt and the birth of the Jewish people. At the Seder (סֵדֶר, "order") we tell the story, eat matzah and bitter herbs, drink four cups of wine and ask the Four Questions. Read the basics in [Passover for beginners](/learn/passover-for-beginners). The dates and candle lighting times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are on the [Jewish holidays page](/jewish-holidays).

## Your Passover checklist, in order

1. **Order shmurah matzah.** Shmurah (שְׁמוּרָה, "guarded") matzah is watched from the wheat harvest through baking. Chabad of Lamorinda offers handmade shmurah matzah on the [matzah form](/forms/matzah), and we will confirm prices and pickup. Learn more in [matzah](/learn/matzah).
2. **Clean out your chametz.** Chametz (חָמֵץ) is leavened food. Read [chametz](/learn/chametz) for how to prepare.
3. **Sell your chametz.** A Jew may not own chametz during Passover, so you authorize the Rabbi to sell what you keep. Put it in a closed cabinet or room; it is bought back for you after the holiday. See [selling your chametz](/learn/selling-your-chametz) and use the [online form](/forms/sell-chametz).
4. **Stock kosher-for-Passover food.** See [kosher for Passover](/learn/kosher-for-passover). Holiday foods can also be delivered by [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher).
5. **Reserve your Seder** and decide who you will invite.
