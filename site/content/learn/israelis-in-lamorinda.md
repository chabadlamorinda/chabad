Living in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda, far from family in Israel, it is easy to miss the feeling of Shabbat on a Friday afternoon, the holidays in the street, and simply speaking Hebrew with friends. At Chabad of Lamorinda, Israelis and Hebrew speakers find a warm Jewish home just a few minutes away, at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette. There is no membership and no dues. You are simply welcome.

## A Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal

Every **Tuesday from 8:30 to 9:30 PM**, Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski teaches a Torah class in Hebrew, and it comes with a meal. It is a relaxed evening of learning, conversation and good food, in your own language. No background is needed, and questions are always welcome. See all the weekly classes on the [adult education page](/programs/adult-education).

Over the years the class list has also included a Tanya class in Hebrew and a women's class in Hebrew. Ask the Rabbi what is running now, or ask him to set up one-on-one learning in Hebrew on any topic you choose.

## The website in Hebrew

This whole website is available in Hebrew. Tap **עב** at the top of any page to switch. Shabbat times, events, holidays, the help pages and the forms all appear in Hebrew, so you can share them easily with family and friends. You can also [create a free account](/account) and RSVP for the whole family in one tap.

## Shabbat in Lafayette

Shabbat here feels like home. **Kabbalat Shabbat** is on Friday evening at {kabbalat-shabbat} (the time may change with the season), and Shabbat morning services are on **Saturday from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM**, with the Torah reading, a short talk, children taking part, and then a hot Kiddush lunch. Candle lighting times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).

Want a real Friday night meal with songs and a festive table? The Rabbi and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski and their family host Shabbat dinners any week of the year. [Let us know you are coming](/forms/shabbat-meal), and bring the kids.

## Programs for your children

Many Israeli families want their children to grow up with Hebrew and with Jewish tradition, even far from Israel. Here is what we offer in Lafayette:

- **[Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool)** for ages 12 months to 4 years, directed by Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski.
- **[CKids Afterschool](/programs/ckids-afterschool)** for ages 5 to 13, weekdays 3 to 6 PM, with homework help, sports, STEAM, **modern Hebrew** and Jewish heritage, plus a hot meal.
- **[Lamorinda Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school)** on Sundays 10 AM to 12 PM, for ages 3 to 13.
- **[Kids and teen events](/programs/kids-and-teens)** around the holidays, and [bar and bat mitzvah](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah) preparation.

## The holidays together

From [Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah) with shofar and Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir, to [Yom Kippur](/holidays/yom-kippur) at the Lafayette Park Hotel, [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah), [Purim](/holidays/purim) and the community [Passover](/holidays/passover) Seder, the whole year is celebrated together. See what is coming up on the [events calendar](/events).

## Praying for Israel

In hard times for Israel, many people want to do something from here. Our [Tehillim page](/tehillim) has the chapters said for the safety of Israel and the Jewish people, and you are welcome to pray with us at Shabbat services.

## Common questions

### Is there a Torah class in Hebrew in Lafayette?
Yes. Every Tuesday from 8:30 to 9:30 PM at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, with a meal. Everyone is welcome, whatever your level.

### Is there a Hebrew-speaking community in Lamorinda?
The Tuesday class in Hebrew, Shabbat and the holiday celebrations are the best places to meet people. Call 925-384-7242 and the Rabbi will be glad to help you connect.

### Where can my kids learn Hebrew near Moraga or Orinda?
CKids Afterschool in Lafayette includes modern Hebrew, and the Sunday Hebrew School teaches Hebrew reading. Both are a short drive from Moraga and Orinda.

### Do I need to be religious to come?
Not at all. Israelis of every background come to Chabad of Lamorinda. Come as you are.
