"Am Yisrael Chai" (עַם יִשְׂרָאֵל חַי) means "The Jewish people lives." It is a declaration that the Jewish people endures, in body and in spirit, through every challenge.

## What the words mean

*Am* is "people," *Yisrael* is Israel, the Jewish people, and *chai* means "alive." Chabad.org describes the phrase as the everlasting statement that the Jewish nation lives on and prevails despite the hardships it faces. In the Jewish view, our endurance has two sides: we survive physically, and we remain strong spiritually.

The word *chai* is also the number 18, the value of its two Hebrew letters. That is why we give gifts in multiples of 18. See [the Chai Club](/chai-club).

## A living people and a living Torah

The Rebbe taught that the Jewish people lives because the Torah is a "living Torah," from the living G-d. Its commandments are not museum pieces: they are meant to be lived, in every generation. Chabad.org shares this teaching in an article written by a rabbi in France, who describes how preparing for Shabbat, buying kosher food, sending children to Jewish schools and attending synagogue are all ways Jewish life continues. For more, read [Jewish pride](/learn/jewish-pride).

## How Jewish survival looks through history

Our people has faced exile, persecution and attempts at destruction, and still carries the Torah to each new generation. Why have we endured? Our tradition answers: through Torah, mitzvahs, family and community. You can read the long story in [Jewish history in brief](/learn/jewish-history-overview).

## What to do in hard times

When the news is heavy, Judaism does not tell us to only worry. It gives us things to do:

1. **Light Shabbat candles.** Women and girls light before sunset on Friday; see [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles). Join us for Friday night: Kabbalat Shabbat this week is at {kabbalat-shabbat}.
2. **Say Tehillim (Psalms).** See [Tehillim](/learn/tehillim) for how to begin.
3. **Do one more mitzvah.** Give charity, put on tefillin, hang a mezuzah, invite a guest.
4. **Stand together.** Call a neighbor and attend a gathering. Unity strengthens us.
5. **Respond to hate with pride.** Read [Facing antisemitism](/learn/facing-antisemitism).

## Common questions

### What does "Am Yisrael Chai" mean?
It means "The Jewish people lives." It is a statement of survival and hope.

### How do you say Am Yisrael Chai in Hebrew?
It is written עם ישראל חי and pronounced "Am Yis-ra-EL KHAI," with the "ch" like the sound in "Bach."

### What is the Jewish response to hard times?
Jewish tradition teaches to turn to prayer, Tehillim, charity, Shabbat and acts of kindness, and to stay united. Ask the Rabbi for personal guidance.

### What is a "living Torah"?
The Rebbe taught that the Torah comes from the living G-d and is meant to be lived each day, not only studied.

### Why does chai matter?
Chai means "life," and its letters equal 18, which is why Jews give charity in multiples of 18.
