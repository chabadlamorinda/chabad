Candle lighting time is the moment to light Shabbat candles, and it is about 18 minutes before sunset on Friday. Shabbat begins at sunset, so once the sun has set it is too late to light, and Shabbat ends the next night after dark, with Havdalah.

For Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, the exact times for this week and the whole year are on [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times), worked out automatically for each town. This guide explains what the words on that page mean, so you can plan your Friday with confidence.

## What "candle lighting" means

Candle lighting is the mitzvah that welcomes Shabbat, traditionally done by the woman of the house, and by the man of the house if no woman is present. Chabad.org explains that you light about 18 minutes before sunset. Why 18 minutes? The custom is to "provide a margin for error." Sunset is the deadline, and the 18 minutes protect you from lighting too late.

Once the candles are lit, and you have said the blessing, Shabbat has begun for you. You can read more about how in [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles).

## Sunset, and why it matters

Sunset is when Shabbat actually starts. That is why candle lighting is tied to it: it is a few minutes earlier, and never later. According to Chabad.org, if you are running late, you may still light until shortly before sunset. After sunset, it is forbidden.

The earliest time to light is *plag haminchah*, 1¼ halachic hours before sunset. Some families light earlier than 18 minutes before sunset, especially in the summer; see [early Shabbat in summer](/learn/early-shabbat-in-summer). Ask the Rabbi what you should do in your household.

## When Shabbat ends: Havdalah

Shabbat ends on Saturday night after nightfall, when it is dark enough that stars are visible. This is some time after sunset, and the exact time is on the Shabbat times page. We mark the end with [Havdalah](/learn/havdalah), a short ceremony with wine, a candle and fragrant spices. You will see the time listed for each town, so you do not need to work it out.

## Why every town has its own page

Sunset happens at slightly different times in different places, depending on how far east or west you are. That is why there is a page for each town: [Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda](/jewish-lafayette), as well as nearby towns such as [Pleasant Hill](/jewish-pleasant-hill) and [Martinez](/jewish-martinez). When you use [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times), choose the town where you will light.

If you are traveling, say, to San Francisco or the Peninsula, the time is different there. Ask the Rabbi, or check the page for your destination.

## How the times move through the year

Candle lighting moves a little every week:

- **In winter** the sun sets early, and Shabbat can begin in the afternoon. This is why working families plan their Fridays carefully; see [Shabbat in winter](/learn/shabbat-in-winter).
- **In summer** sunset is late, so Shabbat begins late in the evening, and many families light early; see [early Shabbat in summer](/learn/early-shabbat-in-summer).
- **In between,** the time changes a few minutes each week.

You do not need to keep the numbers in your head. The easiest habit is to look at [this week's times](/this-week) on Monday.

## Common questions

### What time is candle lighting in Lafayette?

Candle lighting in Lafayette is about 18 minutes before sunset on Friday, and the exact time for this week is on [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times).

### Are candle lighting times in Orinda and Moraga different from Lafayette?

They can differ slightly, because sunset depends on location. Each town has its own page, so choose yours.

### Why do we light candles 18 minutes before sunset?

Chabad.org explains that it is a margin for error, so that you do not light after Shabbat has begun.

### What is the latest I can light candles?

Chabad.org teaches that you may light until shortly before sunset if you are running late. After sunset, it is forbidden.

### When does Shabbat end in Contra Costa County?

Shabbat ends on Saturday night after dark, when stars are visible, and the exact Havdalah time for each town is on the [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times) page.

### What if I miss candle lighting?

Chabad.org says that if you missed it through carelessness, you add one candle in the weeks that follow, and if it was beyond your control, nothing is added. Ask the Rabbi about your situation.
