Jewish women in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are busy: careers, kids, carpools, parents to care for. Sometimes what we need most is an evening out with friends, something creative in our hands, and a little inspiration to take home. That is what the **Jewish Women's Circle** at Chabad of Lamorinda is all about.

## The Jewish Women's Circle

The [Jewish Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle) brings women together for creative evenings, challah bakes, workshops and inspiration with friends. Every woman is welcome, whatever her background, age or level of observance. You do not need to know anyone, and you do not need to be a member of anything.

Past evenings have included:

- The **Mega Challah Bake**, "Loaves of Love"
- **Sip & Paint** and **Sip & Color Analysis**
- **Latkes & Latte** at Chanukah
- A **ceramic Seder plate workshop** and Seder plate painting before Passover
- A **pre-Shavuot cheese board workshop**
- **Shabbat in an Hour**, before Rosh Hashanah
- **Be-you-tiful, inside and out**
- A **DIY menorah** workshop with resin and roses
- A live concert with Meleha

Creative, warm and full of good company: that is the spirit of every evening. To hear about the next one, [join our weekly email](/forms/newsletter) and tick "Women's Circle," or call 925-384-7242.

## Shabbat candles

Lighting Shabbat candles on Friday before sunset is a special mitzvah given to Jewish women, and it brings light and peace into the home. If you have never lit, or have not in years, this week is a beautiful time to start. Our guide to [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles) has the blessing and step-by-step instructions, and the exact candle lighting time for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda is on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat) every week. Need candlesticks? You can find them through [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda.

## Bake challah

Few things fill a home with Shabbat like the smell of fresh challah. Learn how in our [challah guide](/learn/challah), including the special mitzvah of separating challah. And if you love to bake, you can [volunteer](/forms/volunteer) to bake challah for the community.

## Someone to talk to

Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski directs Lamorinda Jewish Preschool and Lamorinda Hebrew School, and she is always glad to talk woman to woman. Many women prefer to speak with the Rebbetzin about marriage and family, a new baby, preparing for a wedding and the mikvah, a hard time, or a question about Jewish life. Conversations are private. See [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi) and [marriage and family](/help/shalom-bayit).

## More ways to connect

- **Learn.** Weekly classes on Sunday evening, Tuesday evening (in Hebrew, with a meal) and Wednesday at 1 PM (Tanya) are open to women and men. See [adult education](/programs/adult-education). Over the years there has also been a women's class in Hebrew; ask what is running now.
- **Shabbat together.** Come for Saturday services and the hot Kiddush lunch, or [join the Rabbi's family for Shabbat dinner](/forms/shabbat-meal).
- **For your daughters.** Girls preparing for bat mitzvah join the **Bat Mitzvah Club**. See [bar and bat mitzvah](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah).
- **Give back.** Help at holiday events, bake challah, or visit seniors and the sick. See [volunteer](/forms/volunteer).

## Common questions

### Is there a Jewish women's group in Lafayette?
Yes. The Jewish Women's Circle of Chabad of Lamorinda meets for creative evenings, challah bakes and workshops. [Join the email list](/forms/newsletter) to hear about the next one.

### Do I have to be religious to join the Women's Circle?
No. Women of every background come from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and nearby. Come as you are and bring a friend.

### When do I light Shabbat candles in Lamorinda?
Eighteen minutes before sunset on Friday. Check this week's exact time on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).

### Can I talk to the Rebbetzin instead of the Rabbi?
Of course. Call 925-384-7241 and ask for Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski.
