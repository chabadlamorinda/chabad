To leave money to a synagogue in your will, you ask your estate attorney to add a gift (a "bequest") naming the organization, with its name, address and tax ID; for us the name is simply "Chabad of Lamorinda." Chabad of Lamorinda accepts bequests, including a whole estate, and the gift supports Jewish education and Jewish life in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda for the next generation.

## The three kinds of bequest

There are three simple ways to give through a will or trust:

- **A set dollar amount**, for example a sum you choose.
- **A specific asset**, such as a particular account or property.
- **A percentage of your estate.**

The [legacy page](/legacy) of Chabad of Lamorinda also describes a gift of what remains after your loved ones are provided for, and an entire estate can be left to Chabad of Lamorinda: the limit of a fifth on giving applies during life, not at death. Which kind fits you is a question for your attorney. A set amount is easy to plan around. A percentage rises and falls along with the estate, so it stays in proportion to what your family receives.

## Why a bequest is easy to start

A gift in a will costs nothing now, leaves your cash flow untouched while you are alive, and can be changed later if your family's situation changes. You keep everything during your lifetime. You are simply writing down what you would like to happen. And a legacy gift counts as maaser.

The Talmud tells of an old man planting a carob tree he would never eat from. He said he found a world full of carob trees planted by those who came before him, so he plants for his grandchildren. ## Common questions

### How do I leave money to a synagogue in my will?
Ask your estate attorney to add a bequest that names the synagogue, with its name, address and tax ID; for us, "Chabad of Lamorinda." The bequest can be a set amount, a specific asset, a percentage or your entire estate.

### Can I leave a bequest to Chabad of Lamorinda?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts legacy gifts, including a bequest in a will or trust. To arrange one, contact Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241.

### What details does my attorney need?
The name to use is Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, CA 94549, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, tax ID 47-4642750. The same details are on the [legacy page](/legacy), and sample wording is in [Sample Bequest Language for Your Attorney](/learn/sample-bequest-language-for-attorneys).

### Can I change my will later?
A gift in a will can be changed if your circumstances change. Your attorney can explain how.

### Do I have to tell the Rabbi?
No. But if you do, the Rabbi can thank you and make sure your gift is used as you wish. Everything stays confidential.

### Should my will also follow Jewish law?
That is a separate and important question, and many families ask the Rabbi about it. See [A Jewish Will](/learn/halachic-will).
