Mazal tov! A new daughter is a great blessing. Simchat bat means "joy of a daughter," and it is the name many families use for the celebration of a baby girl. The heart of it is simple: she receives her Hebrew name at the Torah reading, and family and friends share the joy.

## Naming at the Torah reading

A Jewish girl is traditionally named in the synagogue when the Torah is read. Her father is called up for an aliyah, and after his portion a special prayer, a [Mi Sheberach](/learn/mi-sheberach), is said for the mother and the baby, and the baby's Hebrew name is announced for the first time.

- **When:** the Torah is read on Shabbat morning, Monday and Thursday mornings, Rosh Chodesh and holidays. Chabad custom is to name a girl as soon as possible, at the first Torah reading after she is born.
- **Who needs to be there:** the father. The mother and baby are often still at home recovering, and that is fine.
- **If the father cannot be there**, the Rabbi can arrange the naming another way.

Read more in our guide to [baby naming](/learn/baby-naming).

## Celebrating

Many families celebrate with a kiddush after the naming, or a gathering at home once mother and baby are ready. There is no fixed ceremony for a girl like the brit milah for a boy, so customs vary widely. Sephardic families have a beautiful tradition called **zeved habat**. Some families say a few words about the name, share why they chose it, or give tzedakah in the baby's honor.

Whatever you choose, the joy is the main thing. You can [sponsor a kiddush](/learn/sponsoring-a-kiddush) to celebrate with the community.

## Choosing a Hebrew name

Jewish tradition teaches that parents receive a spark of prophecy when they name a child. Many families:

- **Name after a beloved relative**, often one who has passed away (an Ashkenazic custom), or one who is living (a Sephardic custom).
- **Choose a name from the Torah or Jewish history**, such as Sarah, Rivka, Rachel, Leah, Miriam, Esther or Chana.
- **Look for a meaning** they hope she will grow into.

See [Jewish baby names](/learn/jewish-baby-names) and [Hebrew names](/learn/hebrew-names) for ideas. The Rabbi is happy to talk it through and help with the Hebrew spelling.

## Common questions

### When is a Jewish baby girl named?
At a Torah reading, when her father receives an aliyah. Chabad custom is to do it at the first Torah reading after she is born.

### Does the mother need to be at the naming?
No. The father is called to the Torah; the mother and baby are often still recovering at home.

### Is there a set ceremony for a simchat bat?
No. The naming at the Torah is the key part. Families celebrate in many different ways, such as a kiddush or a gathering at home.

### Can a girl be named later?
Yes. If she was not named soon after birth, she can be named at any time. Call the Rabbi to arrange it.
