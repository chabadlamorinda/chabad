You moved to Lamorinda for the hills, the schools or the commute, or maybe you grew up here and came back. Either way, if you are Jewish and in your 20s or 30s, single or a couple, Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette is a place to find Shabbat, good conversation and friends. There is no membership and no dues. Just come.

We are at 3477 Golden Gate Way, close to downtown Lafayette and the Lafayette BART station, a few minutes from Orinda on Highway 24 and about ten minutes from Moraga.

## Friday night: Shabbat dinner

After a long week, there is nothing like a Shabbat table. Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski and their family host Shabbat dinners any week of the year: a traditional Friday night meal with Jewish songs, warm conversation and a festive table. Come alone or bring a friend. RSVP through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) or at 925-384-7242.

The same form can match you with a local host, or, once you are settled, let you host guests yourself. Shabbat starts with candle lighting; this week's times are on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat). New to it all? Read [Shabbat meals](/learn/shabbat-meals).

## Learning after work

Our weekly [classes for adults](/programs/adult-education) are in the evening, so they fit a working schedule:

- **Sunday, 9:00 to 10:00 PM:** weekly Torah class in English.
- **Tuesday, 8:30 to 9:30 PM:** Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal. Israelis and Hebrew speakers will feel right at home.

Every level is welcome, from your very first class to Talmud and Kabbalah. Busy schedule? Ask the Rabbi to set up one-on-one study at a time that works for you. See [Torah classes in Lamorinda](/learn/torah-classes-in-lamorinda).

## Before work

Commuting through Orinda or Lafayette BART? The weekday morning minyan meets Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM (Sunday at 8:00 AM). It is a great time to put on tefillin with the Rabbi. Want your own pair? Use the [mezuzah and tefillin form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin).

## Men's Club and Women's Circle

- The [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club) brings men together for Bagels, Lox & Tefillin, Scotch & Steaks in the Sukkah, learning and a l'chaim.
- The [Jewish Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle) hosts creative evenings. Past evenings have included Sip & Paint, Latkes & Latte and the Mega Challah Bake.

To hear about the next one, [join our weekly email](/forms/newsletter).

## Holidays with friends

The holidays are the best time to meet people. Hear the shofar at the [Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah) Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir, eat in the sukkah on [Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot), dance with the Torah on [Simchat Torah](/holidays/simchat-torah), come in costume to the [Purim](/holidays/purim) party and join the community [Passover](/holidays/passover) Seder. Dates are on the [events calendar](/events).

## Dating and getting married

Hoping to meet the right person? Read our guide to [Jewish dating](/learn/jewish-dating), and see the chapters of [Tehillim](/tehillim) customarily said when hoping to find a soulmate. Engaged? Mazal Tov! The Rabbi helps couples plan a Jewish wedding from the first meeting to the chuppah. See [getting married](/help/getting-married).

## Give back

[Volunteer](/forms/volunteer) an hour or a season: holiday events, setting up the sukkah, baking challah, Kiddush setup, visiting seniors, tutoring or photography. Or join the [Chai Club](/programs/chai-club), monthly partners who keep Jewish life in Lamorinda going with no dues.

## Common questions

### Where can young Jewish professionals go for Shabbat in Lafayette?
To Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way. The Rabbi's family hosts Shabbat dinner any week of the year. [RSVP here](/forms/shabbat-meal).

### Are there evening Jewish classes in Lamorinda?
Yes. A Torah class in English on Sunday at 9:00 PM and a Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal, on Tuesday at 8:30 PM, both in Lafayette.

### I am single and new to Orinda. How can I meet other Jewish people?
Come to Shabbat dinner, a class or a holiday celebration, and join the weekly email. Call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242 and he will gladly introduce you.

### Can I volunteer with the Jewish community in Lafayette?
Yes. Sign up on the [volunteer form](/forms/volunteer) and choose what you would like to help with.
