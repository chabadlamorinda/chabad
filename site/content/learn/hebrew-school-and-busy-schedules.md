Families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are busy. Between school, homework, sports, music and friends, adding Hebrew school can feel like a lot. The good news: Lamorinda Hebrew School is just two hours a week, on Sunday mornings from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and those two hours give your child something nothing else in the week can.

## Why it is worth a place in the week

Your child's week is full of things that build skills. Hebrew school builds something deeper: knowing who they are. It gives a Jewish identity, Hebrew reading, friends who share their heritage and the values of the Torah. The Midrash says that at Sinai our children were the guarantors of the Torah; Jewish learning is not one more activity, it is the root of the others. See [our children are our guarantors](/learn/children-are-our-guarantors).

## Practical tips for a busy family

**Make Sunday a family rhythm.** When Hebrew school is simply "what we do on Sunday morning", like brushing teeth, there is no weekly debate.

**Plan the year early.** Put Hebrew school on the family calendar at the start of the year, together with the Jewish holidays. See [Jewish holidays and Lamorinda school calendars](/learn/jewish-holidays-and-lamorinda-school-calendars).

**Talk to coaches early.** If a sports schedule clashes, many families find that a word with the coach helps. Our guide to [Shabbat and youth sports](/learn/shabbat-and-youth-sports) has ideas for these conversations.

**Carpool.** Families from Moraga and Orinda often share rides to Lafayette. Ask other parents.

**Keep it light at home.** Five minutes a few times a week, like reading a Hebrew word, saying a blessing or asking about the parsha at dinner, is enough to keep learning going. See [Jewish learning at home with kids](/learn/jewish-learning-at-home-with-kids).

**Let your child see it matters.** Children take their cue from parents. If you ask with interest what they learned, they will value it too.

## Hebrew school and public school

Lamorinda Hebrew School is open to children from every school in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, public or private. It complements their regular school: a different alphabet, different stories and a different community. Learning a second alphabet and discussing Torah stories also exercises the mind. Above all, children who are the only Jewish child in their class gain friends who understand them.

## When a week goes wrong

Some weeks a child is sick, there is a family event, or the schedule falls apart. That happens. With Aleph Champ, each child reads at their own level, so a missed Sunday does not leave them behind. Just come back next week.

## Lamorinda Hebrew School

Lamorinda Hebrew School meets on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, for children ages 3 to 13. Tuition is $600 per child per year, including books and supplies; no membership is needed, and no child is turned away for lack of funds. ### Will Hebrew school overload my child?
Two joyful, hands-on hours a week, with games and creative projects, usually feel like a break, not a burden. Children come for the friends as much as for the learning.

### What if we miss a Sunday?
