A Jewish legacy gift is a gift to a Jewish cause that you arrange during your lifetime and that takes effect later, most often through a will, a living trust or a beneficiary form. It is a way to keep your values and your care for others alive after you, and to give the next generation what you were given: a Jewish education.

## What a legacy gift is

You do not give anything away today. You write down your wishes so that, in time, part of what you leave helps a cause you believe in. Standard guidance describes several ways this can be done:

- **In a will or living trust:** a specific dollar amount, a particular asset, or a percentage of the estate.
- **As a beneficiary of a retirement account,** such as an IRA or 401(k), usually by filling in a form with the institution that holds the account.
- **As a beneficiary of life insurance, an annuity, or a donor-advised fund.**

Chabad of Lamorinda accepts all of these, and also accepts donor-advised fund grants and gifts of stock now, during your lifetime. Chabad of Lamorinda has a Fidelity brokerage account for gifts of stock; call the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin and we will give you the transfer details. A legacy gift counts as maaser, and so does a donor-advised fund grant. An entire estate can be left to Chabad of Lamorinda: the limit of a fifth on giving applies during life, not at death. Standard guidance notes that a bequest in a will can be changed if your family's situation changes, and that it does not touch your cash flow while you are alive. It also urges people to work with an experienced estate attorney and to talk with family and a financial advisor. This guide is not legal or tax advice; please ask your attorney and your tax advisor.

## The Jewish meaning of leaving something behind

Judaism has always cared about what we hand on. Standard guidance presents a planned gift as a contribution to the continuity of the Jewish people and to keeping Torah teachings alive for the next generations. Tzedakah (צְדָקָה) means "justice," and a gift in a will is one more way of doing what is right, even when we are no longer here to do it ourselves. See [Tzedakah](/learn/tzedakah).

For Chabad of Lamorinda, a legacy gift is education for future generations: Jewish education for children and families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, the preschool, the Hebrew school and every Shabbat table. It is how Judaism stays alive here after we are gone.

A gift can also carry a name. Many people choose to dedicate it in memory of a parent or spouse, and Chabad of Lamorinda gladly offers dedication opportunities, such as dedicating a room or a memorial plaque in the synagogue; consult the Rabbi. See [Giving in Someone's Honor or Memory](/learn/giving-in-someones-honor).

## Values and possessions together

A legacy is more than money. An ethical will is a letter that passes on values, memories and life lessons to your children. Standard guidance shares a moving example in which a mother, who says her possessions would fit in a few lines, leaves her children a sense of belonging, faith and identity, and her love and blessing. See [An Ethical Will](/learn/ethical-will).

If you would like your estate to follow Jewish law for your heirs, that is a separate and important question. See [A Will That Follows Jewish Law](/learn/halachic-will) and ask the Rabbi.

## Common questions

### What is a legacy gift?
It is a gift you arrange now that takes effect later, usually through a will, a living trust or a beneficiary form. You keep everything during your lifetime.

### Can I leave a gift to Chabad of Lamorinda in my will?
Yes. The [legacy page](/legacy) describes ways to do this, including a will, a trust, a retirement account, life insurance, a donor-advised fund or stock. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts donor-advised fund grants and gifts of stock, and your gift goes to Jewish education for the next generation. Talk to your attorney, and call Rabbi Labkowski at 925-384-7242.

### What is an ethical will?
It is a letter that passes on your values, memories and blessings. See [An Ethical Will](/learn/ethical-will). It is a separate thing from a legal will.

### Can I change my mind later?
A bequest in a will can be changed, as Standard guidance notes. Your attorney can explain how.

### Does a legacy gift have tax benefits?
That depends on your situation. Ask your tax advisor and attorney. This guide does not give tax or legal advice.

### Is there a minimum amount?
Leaving a legacy is not about the size of the gift. It is about saying that what you cared about will continue.
