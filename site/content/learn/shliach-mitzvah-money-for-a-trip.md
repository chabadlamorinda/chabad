Shliach mitzvah money is tzedakah that a friend gives you to give away when you arrive at your destination, so that you travel as a "shliach mitzvah," a messenger on a mitzvah mission. The reason for the custom: the Sages teach that a messenger on a mitzvah is not harmed, so people try to have a traveler appointed in this way.

## What "shliach mitzvah" means

A *shliach mitzvah* (שְׁלִיחַ מִצְוָה) is someone sent to do a mitzvah. Rabbi Eliezer Wenger explains that the Sages teach that a *sheliach mitzvah* is not harmed. This is why the custom developed: a traveler is given money to distribute as tzedakah at the destination. It is a tradition about trust and protection on the road, not a guarantee. For anything that concerns you personally, [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi).

## The custom, step by step

Here is how the practice works:

1. **Someone gives the traveler money** to distribute as tzedakah when he or she reaches the destination.
2. **Some people also give extra** for tzedakah to be given on the return trip, so the traveler is a shliach mitzvah in both directions.
3. **It may be advisable to say** that the traveler may exchange the money for other money or for foreign currency, as needed on the trip.
4. **The traveler gives the money** to people in need at the destination.

## If no one gives you the money

A traveler can also set aside money of his or her own and say that upon arrival it will be given to tzedakah, *bli neder*, without making a binding vow. It is suggested to say that the gift is in memory of Rabbi Meir Ba'al Haneis. You can read about him in [Rabbi Meir Baal HaNess](/learn/rabbi-meir-baal-haness).

## Tzedakah before leaving

A traveler should also give tzedakah before setting out. A verse in Psalms (85:14), "Righteousness [*tzedek*] goes before him," is read as hinting that giving comes before the journey. *Tzedek* shares a root with *tzedakah* (צְדָקָה), "justice."

## Where this fits with the travelers' prayer

Giving tzedakah is a separate custom from the travelers' prayer, [Tefilat HaDerech](/learn/tefilat-haderech). That guide explains who says the prayer and when. ## Common questions

### What is shliach mitzvah money?
It is money that a friend gives a traveler to give to tzedakah at the destination. The Sages teach that a messenger on a mitzvah is not harmed, so the traveler is appointed as a "shliach mitzvah."

### Is shliach mitzvah money a guarantee of a safe trip?
No. It is a tradition, based on the Sages' teaching, and it is considered an advisable practice. For any personal question, call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242.

### Do I have to give the money away at the destination?
Yes, that is the point: the traveler distributes it as tzedakah when he or she arrives. The traveler may exchange the money for other money or currency if needed.

### What if nobody gives me money to take?
You can set aside your own money and say, without making a vow (bli neder), that you will give it to tzedakah when you arrive.

### Should I also give tzedakah before leaving?
Yes. A traveler should give tzedakah before setting out, based on Psalms 85:14.

### Who gives the money to the traveler?
Friends and family appoint the traveler by handing over money to distribute at the destination. Ask the Rabbi if you have questions about your own trip.
