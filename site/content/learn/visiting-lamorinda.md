Coming to Lamorinda to visit your children and grandchildren, for a wedding, a graduation at Saint Mary's College, or a business trip? Welcome! Chabad of Lamorinda, at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, is happy to be your Jewish home away from home. There is no membership and no dues, and visitors are always welcome.

## Getting here

We are close to downtown Lafayette and the **Lafayette BART station**. From Orinda it is a few minutes east on Highway 24, or one stop on BART. From Moraga it is about ten minutes up Moraga Road or St. Mary's Road. Pleasant Hill is about 15 minutes away and Martinez about 20. See [our area](/our-area) and the town pages for [Lafayette](/jewish-lafayette), [Moraga](/jewish-moraga) and [Orinda](/jewish-orinda).

## Shabbat while you are here

- **Candle lighting:** this week's times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat). Candle lighting is 18 minutes before sunset, and Shabbat ends at nightfall. For daily prayer times, see [zmanim](/tools/zmanim).
- **Friday night:** Kabbalat Shabbat at 6:30 PM (the time may change with the season, so check the Shabbat page).
- **Shabbat dinner:** the Rabbi's family hosts a traditional Friday night meal any week of the year. Visitors are our guests. Please [let us know you are coming](/forms/shabbat-meal) so we can set a place, or ask to be hosted by a local family.
- **Saturday morning:** services 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, with Torah reading and a short talk, followed by a hot Kiddush lunch. The prayer book is bilingual and the Rabbi announces the pages.

More in [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda).

## Weekday minyan and Kaddish

The morning minyan meets Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM and Sunday at 8:00 AM. Saying Kaddish or observing a yahrzeit while you travel? Let us know before you come and we will make sure there is a minyan. If you cannot be there, we can arrange for Kaddish to be said on your behalf at [Kaddish](/kaddish). Need tefillin? The Rabbi is happy to help you put them on.

## Kosher food

Kosher meat, poultry, groceries and holiday foods can be ordered online and delivered from [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda. For current kosher options in the area, call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242. Keeping kosher on the road? See [kosher when traveling](/learn/kosher-when-traveling).

## Visiting for a holiday

Many families come to Lamorinda for the holidays. Visitors are welcome at all of them:

- [Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah) services, and Shofar and Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir.
- [Yom Kippur](/holidays/yom-kippur) services at the Lafayette Park Hotel, from Kol Nidre to Neilah and a break-fast.
- High Holiday services need no tickets; [reserve a seat](/forms/high-holidays) so we can prepare.
- [Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot): shake the lulav at our sukkah any day of the holiday.
- [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah) menorah lighting, [Purim](/holidays/purim) Megillah readings and the community [Passover](/holidays/passover) Seder.

Dates are on the [holidays page](/holidays) and the [events calendar](/events).

## If a loved one here is ill

Visiting a parent in a hospital or care facility nearby? Rabbi Labkowski visits at home, in hospitals, care facilities and hospice, across Lamorinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez. Call 925-384-7242, and we will add their name to our [Tehillim prayer list](/tehillim). See [when someone is sick](/help/someone-is-sick).

## Before you travel home

Say the Traveler's Prayer as you set out. See [Tefilat HaDerech](/learn/tefilat-haderech). And if your family lives here, introduce them to us: the Rabbi and Rebbetzin would love to meet them.

## Common questions

### Where can a Jewish visitor go for Shabbat in Lafayette?
Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way. Kabbalat Shabbat on Friday evening, Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family, and Saturday services at 10:00 AM with Kiddush lunch.

### Can I say Kaddish while visiting Lamorinda?
Yes. Join the minyan Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM or Sunday at 8:00 AM, and let us know ahead so we can make sure there is a minyan.

### Where can I get kosher food near Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda?
Order from [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher) for delivery, and call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242 for current kosher options in the area.

### Do visitors need tickets for High Holiday services in Lafayette?
No. Services are open to everyone with no tickets or membership. [Reserving](/forms/high-holidays) helps us save you a seat.
