Welcome to Lamorinda! Moving to Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda means a new home, new neighbors and new routines. It also means a warm Jewish community waiting to meet you. Chabad of Lamorinda, at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, is a home for every Jew in the three towns and nearby Pleasant Hill and Martinez. There is no membership and no dues: everyone belongs.

## Where your town fits

- **Lafayette:** we are right in town, close to downtown and the Lafayette BART station. See [Jewish Lafayette](/jewish-lafayette).
- **Moraga:** Moraga has no synagogue of its own, so Chabad of Lamorinda is about ten minutes away up Moraga Road or St. Mary's Road. See [Jewish Moraga](/jewish-moraga).
- **Orinda:** a few minutes east on Highway 24, or one stop on BART. Commuting through Orinda BART? The weekday morning minyan and evening classes are a short ride away. See [Jewish Orinda](/jewish-orinda).
- **Pleasant Hill and Martinez:** about 15 and 20 minutes away. See [Pleasant Hill](/jewish-pleasant-hill) and [Martinez](/jewish-martinez).

## Your first month, step by step

1. **Come for Shabbat.** Saturday at 10 AM for services and a hot Kiddush lunch, or a Friday night dinner with the Rabbi's family. [RSVP for a Shabbat meal](/forms/shabbat-meal). More in [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda).
2. **Create your free account.** Save your family once and RSVP to anything in one tap at [my account](/account).
3. **Put up a mezuzah.** A new home is the perfect time. We help you buy kosher mezuzahs and put them up with the blessing. Use the [mezuzah form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin), and read about [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) and a [chanukat habayit](/learn/chanukat-habayit), the Jewish housewarming.
4. **Find a place for your kids.** [Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool) for ages 12 months to 4 years, [Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) on Sundays for ages 3 to 13, and [CKids afterschool](/programs/ckids-afterschool) on weekdays. See [Jewish kids in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-kids-in-lamorinda).
5. **Stock your kitchen.** Kosher meat, groceries and Judaica are delivered by [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda. The Rabbi will gladly help you kosher a new kitchen. See [kosher in Lamorinda](/learn/kosher-in-lamorinda).
6. **Meet people.** The [Jewish Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle), the [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club), [weekly classes](/programs/adult-education) and holiday celebrations are the easiest way to make friends.

## Speaking Hebrew?

Israeli and Hebrew-speaking families will feel at home at the **Tuesday Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal**, 8:30 PM. See [Torah classes in Lamorinda](/learn/torah-classes-in-lamorinda).

## Stay in the loop

Get Shabbat times, events and a short Torah thought once a week with our [weekly email](/forms/newsletter), and check the [events calendar](/events) and [holidays](/holidays).

## When life happens

New baby, bar mitzvah, a wedding, or a hard time? The Rabbi and Rebbetzin are here for every life event. Start at [help](/help) or [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi).

## Give back

Once you are settled, consider the [Chai Club](/programs/chai-club), monthly partners who keep Jewish life here going with no dues, or [volunteer](/forms/volunteer) an hour or a season.

## Common questions

### Is there a synagogue in Moraga?
Moraga has no synagogue of its own. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, about ten minutes away, is the Jewish home for Moraga families.

### Is there a synagogue in Orinda?
Orinda families come to Chabad of Lamorinda in downtown Lafayette, a few minutes east on Highway 24 or one stop on BART. Call the Rabbi for anything else you are looking for.

### Do I need to join or pay dues?
No. There is no membership and no dues at Chabad of Lamorinda. Everyone is welcome at every service, class and event.

### How do I meet the Rabbi?
Come for Shabbat, or call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242. You can also [send a note](/contact). We would love to welcome you personally.
