Kislev is the third month of the Jewish year counting from Rosh Hashanah, and it usually falls in November and December. The nights are at their longest, and yet Kislev is the month of light: it holds Chanukah and several Chassidic days of joy.

## Days to know in Kislev

- **Rosh Chodesh Kislev**, the first of the month. In 1977 the Lubavitcher Rebbe returned to his office on this day after a serious heart attack, and Chassidim celebrate it as a day of thanks.
- **10 Kislev.** The release from prison of the second Chabad Rebbe, Rabbi DovBer, the Mitteler Rebbe, in 1826.
- **19 Kislev, [Yud Tes Kislev](/learn/yud-tes-kislev).** The release of the Alter Rebbe, the founder of Chabad, in 1798: the "Rosh Hashanah of Chassidus."
- **25 Kislev.** The first day of **Chanukah**. The first candle is lit the evening before, and Chanukah continues into the month of Tevet.

## A month of miracles

Kislev's holidays all celebrate light overcoming darkness: a few Maccabees defeating a great army, a small jar of oil burning for eight days, and Rebbes freed from prison to keep teaching. The lesson is that even when things seem dark, a little light, added every day, changes everything.

## A month of dreams

The Torah portions read in Kislev tell of Joseph's dreams, and the commentators connect Kislev with sleep and dreams. Our sages compare the time of exile to a dream, and Kislev's miracles remind us that the dream will end with a bright awakening, the coming of Moshiach.

## Making the most of Kislev

- **Add light.** Give an extra coin to tzedakah each weekday, light Shabbat candles on time, and learn something new.
- **Prepare for Chanukah.** Check your menorah, buy candles or oil, and invite friends to light with you. See [how to light the menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah).
- **Join a farbrengen** on Yud Tes Kislev.

## Common questions

### How do I know when Kislev starts?
The Hebrew calendar follows the moon, so Kislev begins on a different date each year. Our [Jewish holidays](/holidays) page lists this year's dates.

### Is Kislev 29 or 30 days long?
It can be either. That is why the date of Chanukah's last days and Rosh Chodesh Tevet changes slightly from year to year.

### What is the mazal (sign) of Kislev?
The sign of Kislev is the bow (Sagittarius), which the commentators connect with the rainbow, a sign of G-d's promise and kindness.
