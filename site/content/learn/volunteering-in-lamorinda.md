Some of the best Jewish moments in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda happen because someone gave an hour: the sukkah that went up on time, the challah on the table, the visit that brightened a lonely afternoon. At Chabad of Lamorinda you can help build Jewish life here, **an hour or a season at a time**. No experience and no membership needed, just a willing heart.

## Ways to help

When you [sign up as a volunteer](/forms/volunteer), you can tell us what you would enjoy:

- **Holiday events.** Help set up, welcome guests and serve at celebrations like the [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah) menorah lighting, the [Purim](/holidays/purim) party, the [Passover](/holidays/passover) Seder and the [Shavuot](/holidays/shavuot) ice cream party.
- **Setting up the sukkah.** Before [Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot), many hands make the sukkah go up quickly.
- **Baking challah.** Bake for Shabbat and holidays. Our [challah guide](/learn/challah) has everything you need.
- **Kiddush setup.** Help prepare the hot Kiddush lunch after Shabbat morning services on Saturday.
- **Visiting seniors and the sick.** Bikur cholim, visiting the sick, is one of the greatest mitzvahs. A short, cheerful visit can mean the world.
- **Driving.** A ride to Shabbat, a holiday or an appointment can make all the difference for someone who cannot drive.
- **Teaching or tutoring.** Share what you know with children or adults.
- **Photography.** Capture our events so the whole community can enjoy them.
- **Office help.** The many behind-the-scenes tasks that keep a community running.

Tell us when you are available, and we will match you with what fits your schedule.

## Open your Shabbat table

One of the most beautiful ways to help is to host. New families, students from Saint Mary's College and Diablo Valley College, and people who are on their own often look for a Shabbat meal. If you would like to host guests at your home, fill in the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) and choose "Host guests for a Shabbat meal." We will make the match. Read about the mitzvah of [welcoming guests](/learn/hachnasat-orchim).

## For teens and families

Volunteering together is a wonderful way to teach children kindness. Families can help at holiday events, bake challah together, or bring a smile to seniors. For a teen preparing for a bar or bat mitzvah, a mitzvah or charity project is a meaningful part of the journey. See [planning a bar or bat mitzvah](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah).

## Why it matters

The Torah teaches that the world stands on three things: Torah, prayer and acts of kindness. [Chesed](/learn/chesed), kindness, is how a group of neighbors becomes a community that cares for one another in times of joy, sorrow and need. At Chabad of Lamorinda there is no membership and no dues, so every volunteer hour goes straight into making Jewish life possible for everyone in Lamorinda.

## Common questions

### Where can I volunteer in the Jewish community in Lafayette?
At Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way. Help with holidays, the sukkah, challah, the Kiddush, visiting seniors, driving, tutoring, photography or the office. [Sign up here](/forms/volunteer).

### Can my kids volunteer with me?
Yes. Many tasks, like baking challah and helping at holiday events, are perfect for families. Mention your children when you sign up.

### I live in Moraga or Orinda and only have a little time. Can I still help?
Absolutely. Even one hour around a holiday makes a difference. Tell us when you are free and we will find the right fit.

### Can I host a Shabbat meal for newcomers or students?
Yes. Use the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) and choose to host guests. We will match you with guests.
