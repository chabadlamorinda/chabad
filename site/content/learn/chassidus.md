Chassidus (also spelled Chassidism or Hasidism) is a movement and a body of teachings that reveal the inner, soulful side of Judaism. Founded by the Baal Shem Tov in the 18th century, it teaches that G-d is present everywhere and that every Jew can serve Him with joy, love and sincerity.

## What does Chassidus mean?

The word comes from chessed, meaning kindness or devotion. A chassid is someone who goes beyond the minimum, serving G-d with heart and soul. Chassidus is both a way of life and a deep philosophy built on the teachings of [Kabbalah](/learn/kabbalah).

## How it began

Rabbi Israel Baal Shem Tov (1698 to 1760) lived in Eastern Europe at a time when many Jews were poor, persecuted and discouraged. Torah scholarship had become the domain of a learned few, and simple Jews often felt distant.

The Baal Shem Tov taught that:

- **G-d is everywhere,** in every detail of life, guiding even the smallest events.
- **Every Jew is precious,** whether a great scholar or a simple laborer. Sincere prayer and a heartfelt "Amen" are deeply valued.
- **Joy is essential.** Serving G-d with happiness is a key part of Judaism, not an extra.
- **Love every Jew.** Ahavat Yisrael, love for a fellow Jew, is a foundation of Torah.

His student, the Maggid of Mezeritch, spread these teachings through a group of great disciples who led communities across Eastern Europe.

## Core ideas of Chassidus

**Divine providence (hashgacha pratit).** Nothing happens by chance. Every leaf that falls is part of G-d's plan.

**Constant creation.** G-d does not create the world once and step away. He creates it anew every moment.

**Elevating the physical.** Everyday activities like eating, working and talking can become holy when done with the right intention.

**Inner work.** It is not enough to do mitzvot mechanically. Chassidus encourages refining one's character and connecting with the inner meaning of prayer and Torah.

**Joy and song.** Melodies (niggunim) and gatherings (farbrengens) help lift the soul.

## Chabad Chassidus

Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, a student of the Maggid, founded [Chabad](/learn/chabad). Chabad stands for Chochmah, Binah, Daat (wisdom, understanding, knowledge), emphasizing that the mind should understand G-dliness deeply so the heart can feel it. His book, the [Tanya](/learn/tanya), is the foundation of Chabad Chassidus. His successors, leading to [the Rebbe](/learn/the-rebbe), Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, explained these teachings in thousands of works.

## Common questions

### Are all Chassidim the same?

No. There are many Chassidic groups, such as Chabad, Satmar, Belz and Ger, each with its own Rebbe, customs and emphasis. They share the core teachings of the Baal Shem Tov.

### Do I have to be Chassidic to learn Chassidus?

Not at all. Chassidic teachings are for every Jew, and many people from all backgrounds find them inspiring and practical.

### What is a farbrengen?

A farbrengen is a Chassidic gathering with stories, songs, Torah thoughts and a l'chaim. It is a warm way to grow together as a community.

### Why do Chassidim dress differently?

Many Chassidim keep traditional dress as a way of preserving their heritage and modesty. The heart of Chassidus, though, is inner devotion and love of fellow Jews.
