The twelve Torah passages (the "12 pesukim") are twelve short verses and teachings chosen by the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, to sum up the essence of Judaism, and he suggested that every Jewish child learn them by heart. Parents in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda can teach one a week.

## What are the 12 pesukim?

A passuk (פסוק) is a verse. According to Chabad.org, the Rebbe selected these twelve from across Jewish literature as a foundation for a child's whole life. They are short enough for a young child and deep enough for an adult.

## The twelve, one line each

1. **Torah Tziva (Deuteronomy 33:4).** The Torah is the inheritance of every Jew, not only scholars.
2. **Shema Yisrael (Deuteronomy 6:4).** G-d is one and is everywhere, creating and sustaining the world. See the [Shema](/learn/shema).
3. **B'chol Dor Vador (Pesachim 116b).** Each person, each day, should see themselves as having left Egypt, breaking free of the pull toward wrongdoing.
4. **Kol Yisrael (Sanhedrin 90a).** Every Jew has a share in the World to Come, and G-d cherishes each one like a gardener tending a plant.
5. **Ki Karov (Deuteronomy 30:14).** The Torah is close to you, in speech, feeling and action.
6. **V'hineh Hashem (Tanya, chapter 41).** G-d is aware of each person, which inspires awe.
7. **Bereishit (Genesis 1:1).** The world was created for Torah, so even ordinary acts like eating can be holy.
8. **V'shinantam (Deuteronomy 6:7).** Teach Torah to your children and keep it in daily speech.
9. **Yagati (Job).** Real success comes through effort.
10. **V'ahavta (Leviticus 19:18).** Love your fellow as yourself.
11. **Vezeh Kol HaAdam (Tanya, chapter 33).** We are here to make our homes and the world a dwelling place for G-d through mitzvot.
12. **Yismach (Tanya, chapter 33).** Every Jew should rejoice in this mission.

## How to teach one a week

- **At bedtime.** Say the verse in Hebrew, then in English, then ask "What do you think this means?"
- **In the car.** Repeat the verse together on the way to school or to Hebrew school. Short rides are perfect for memory.
- **At Shabbat dinner.** Let a child "teach" the week's verse to the family, then add it to the [Torah thought at the table](/learn/dvar-torah-at-the-shabbat-table).
- **Songs and videos.** Chabad.org has a children's series of videos on the twelve passages. Songs help young children remember.

Take it slowly. One verse a week finishes in about three months, and you can start over again.

## Why it matters

A child who knows these twelve verses holds a short summary of what Judaism asks and promises: that every Jew belongs, that G-d is near, that love and learning come first. For families who live far from large Jewish communities, this is a gift that travels. ## Common questions

### What are the 12 pesukim?
They are twelve Torah verses and teachings chosen by the Lubavitcher Rebbe to sum up the essence of Judaism for children.

### Who chose the twelve Torah passages?
The Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, selected them and suggested that every child learn them by heart.

### At what age can a child learn them?
Young children can begin with the first verses, such as the Shema. Go at your child's own pace.

### Where can I find videos or songs for the 12 pesukim?
Chabad.org has children's videos and articles on them. Call us at 925-384-7242 for more help.

### Do all twelve come from the Torah?
Most come from the Torah, but a few are from the Talmud and the Tanya, as the list shows.
