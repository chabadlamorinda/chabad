"Be among the students of Aaron: love peace and pursue peace" (Ethics of the Fathers 1:12). Aaron, Moses' brother, would go to two people who had quarreled, and tell each one how much the other regretted it, until they embraced. Making peace is one of the greatest mitzvahs.

## Asking forgiveness

Yom Kippur atones for wrongs between a person and G-d. For wrongs between people, we must first ask the person we hurt to forgive us. That is why many people call friends and family before Yom Kippur, but any day is the right day.

How to do it well:

1. **Be specific.** "I'm sorry I spoke harshly to you at dinner on Friday."
2. **Don't explain it away.** No "but you...".
3. **Make it right** if there was a loss: pay back, fix, correct what you said to others.
4. **Commit to change.** True teshuvah means not repeating it.

Jewish law says that if the person refuses to forgive, ask sincerely up to three times. After that, you have done your part.

## Forgiving others

Maimonides writes that a person should be easy to appease and hard to anger, and when asked to forgive, should forgive with a whole heart. Many say each night before sleep: "I forgive anyone who angered or hurt me," a short prayer in the bedtime [Shema](/learn/bedtime-shema).

Forgiving does not mean pretending nothing happened, or putting yourself in harm's way again. It means letting go of the anger, so it no longer controls you.

## Forgiving yourself

After sincere [teshuvah](/learn/teshuvah), G-d forgives completely. Carrying guilt forever does not help anyone; changing does.

## Making peace between others

Be a "student of Aaron": encourage friends who quarreled to talk, speak well of each one to the other, and never carry gossip between them.

## Common questions

### What if the person I hurt has passed away?
Jewish law describes going to their grave with ten people to ask forgiveness. Giving tzedakah and saying Kaddish in their memory also helps. Ask the Rabbi.

### Do I have to forgive someone who isn't sorry?
Forgiveness is asked of us when the person sincerely asks. Still, letting go of anger is good for your own soul.

### Is it okay to say a small untruth to make peace?
The sages teach that for the sake of peace, one may adjust words, as Aaron did. Hurtful lies are never allowed.
