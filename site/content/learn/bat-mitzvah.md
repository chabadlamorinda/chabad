A bat mitzvah is the moment a Jewish girl turns 12 and becomes responsible for keeping the mitzvot (commandments). It happens automatically on her 12th Hebrew birthday; a celebration honors this milestone, but she becomes a bat mitzvah with or without one.

## What does bat mitzvah mean?

Bat mitzvah means "daughter of the commandment." From this age a girl is considered a Jewish adult in religious terms, personally responsible for her actions and her connection to G-d.

## Why 12 and not 13?

Jewish tradition teaches that girls mature earlier than boys, both physically and spiritually. The Talmud says that women were given an extra measure of binah, intuitive understanding. So a girl reaches the age of mitzvot a year before a boy.

## When is the bat mitzvah?

It falls on her 12th birthday on the Hebrew calendar. Use our [bar and bat mitzvah calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah) to find the date. Remember that a Jewish day begins at sunset, so an evening birth counts as the next Hebrew date.

## How to prepare

A meaningful bat mitzvah focuses on who she is becoming. Preparation often includes:

1. **Learning about mitzvot** that are special to Jewish women and girls, such as lighting [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles), separating [challah](/learn/challah), and caring for a Jewish home.
2. **Studying inspiring Jewish women,** from Sarah and Rachel to Esther and Devorah. See our guide on [Jewish women](/learn/jewish-women).
3. **Preparing a talk** (a d'var Torah) on a topic she cares about.
4. **Taking on a mitzvah project,** such as volunteering, tzedakah, or visiting the elderly.
5. **Choosing a personal mitzvah** to grow in, such as daily prayer or giving charity regularly.

Learn more on our [bar and bat mitzvah program](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah) page.

## Ways to celebrate

Every family celebrates differently. Popular ideas include:

- A festive meal or party where she shares her d'var Torah.
- A challah bake with friends and family.
- A women's or girls' gathering with blessings and inspiration.
- A trip, project or gift that has lasting Jewish meaning, such as her own candlesticks.

In Chabad communities, the bat mitzvah celebration usually takes place outside the synagogue service, often as a dinner or event where the girl speaks. This lets her shine in a setting that celebrates her uniquely.

## Common questions

### Can an adult woman have a bat mitzvah?

Absolutely. Every Jewish woman became bat mitzvah at 12, but many choose to celebrate later in life with study, a speech and a gathering. It is a lovely way to reconnect.

### Does she need to read from the Torah?

In traditional practice, a bat mitzvah celebration focuses on learning, a speech and mitzvot rather than a Torah reading. What makes it meaningful is her growth and commitment, not a performance.

### When should we start planning?

Many families begin six months to a year ahead. That leaves time for learning, a mitzvah project and planning the celebration without stress.

### What is a good bat mitzvah gift?

Meaningful gifts include Shabbat candlesticks, a siddur (prayer book), Jewish books, a tzedakah box, or a gift in her honor to a cause she cares about.
