The longing for a child can be one of life's deepest pains. Many of our great matriarchs, Sarah, Rebecca, Rachel and Chana, waited years for a child. Their stories are part of the Torah because they speak to every couple who waits and prays.

## You are not alone

Infertility is far more common than people talk about. It is not a punishment and not anyone's fault. It is a time to support each other, and to let others support you.

## Prayer and customs

- **Chana's prayer.** Chana poured out her heart to G-d in the Tabernacle, and her son Samuel was born. Her story is read in the synagogue on Rosh Hashanah. Speak to G-d in your own words, any time.
- **Tehillim.** Many say Psalms each day, especially Psalm 113, which praises G-d who makes "the childless woman a joyful mother of children." See our [Tehillim](/learn/tehillim) guide.
- **Mitzvahs as a merit.** Many couples take on a mitzvah, such as checking their [mezuzahs](/learn/mezuzah), Shabbat candles or tzedakah.
- **The [mikvah](/learn/mikvah)** and the laws of family purity are a foundation of Jewish married life and a source of blessing.
- **Asking for a blessing.** Many write to the Lubavitcher Rebbe's resting place (the Ohel) to ask for a blessing.

## Medical treatment

Judaism encourages couples to seek medical help. Fertility treatment, including IVF, is used by many observant couples. Some procedures raise questions in Jewish law, for example about supervision in the lab, donor eggs or sperm, and the timing of treatment with the mikvah. **Speak with a rabbi before starting treatment**, so every step is done in the right way. There are organizations that provide this guidance and lab supervision, and the Rabbi can connect you.

## For friends and family

- Don't ask "When are you having kids?" You never know what someone is going through.
- Include couples without children warmly in holidays and Shabbat meals.
- Offer to pray for them, quietly, by their Hebrew names.

## Common questions

### Is IVF allowed in Jewish law?
Many rabbinic authorities permit IVF with proper guidance and supervision. The details matter, so ask the Rabbi before you begin.

### Can I add a name to a prayer list?
Yes. You can add names to your private prayer list in your [account](/account), or ask the Rabbi to pray for you.

### What about adoption?
Adoption is a beautiful mitzvah. See our guide to [Jewish adoption](/learn/jewish-adoption).
