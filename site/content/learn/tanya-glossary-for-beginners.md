The Tanya's key words are mostly Hebrew terms for the soul and the inner struggle: beinoni (the ordinary person who strives), tzaddik (the righteous), nefesh habahamit (the animal soul), nefesh Elokit (the divine soul) and kelipah (a layer that conceals G-dliness). Knowing a few of them makes the first chapters much easier.

Below are the words you will meet, in plain English, each as Chabad.org's Tanya pages explain it. Keep this page open while you learn. For the book itself, see [what the Tanya is](/learn/tanya) and [how to start learning Tanya](/learn/how-to-start-learning-tanya).

## The book and its parts

- **Tanya:** the book's popular name, from its first word.
- **Likkutei Amarim:** "Collected Teachings," the modest title the author gave Part One. It is also called the "Book of the Intermediates" (**Sefer shel Beinonim**).
- **The five parts:** Likkutei Amarim (53 chapters); **Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah**, the "Portal of Unity and Belief"; **Iggeret HaTeshuvah**, the "Epistle of Repentance" ([teshuvah](/learn/teshuvah)); **Iggeret HaKodesh**, the "Sacred Epistle"; and **Kuntres Acharon**, the "Latest Treatise."
- **The Alter Rebbe:** the author, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi ([who he was](/learn/the-alter-rebbe)).

## The soul

- **Nefesh Elokit:** the divine soul, "a part of G-d above" ([part of G-d above](/learn/part-of-g-d-above)).
- **Nefesh habahamit:** the animal soul, the natural self ([the two souls](/learn/the-two-souls-explained); [not evil](/learn/animal-soul-not-evil)).
- **Garments:** the three ways the soul expresses itself: thought, speech and action ([three garments](/learn/three-garments-of-the-soul)).
- **Chochmah, Binah, Daat:** the intellectual faculties of the soul: wisdom, understanding and knowledge ([Sefirot](/learn/sefirot)).
- **Middot:** the emotions, such as love and fear of G-d.
- **Love and awe (ahavah and yirah):** Chapter 12 speaks of a hidden, natural love and fear of G-d in the heart. See [loving G-d](/learn/loving-g-d) and [fear of G-d](/learn/fear-of-g-d).

## Kinds of people

- **Tzaddik:** a righteous person whose inner desires have been transformed.
- **Rasha:** "wicked." Tanya uses the word strictly: Chapter 12 says that someone who willingly indulges a wrong thought is called a rasha at that moment.
- **Beinoni:** the "intermediate" person, who feels the desire but never lets it direct thought, speech or action ([the beinoni](/learn/the-beinoni)).

## Layers and levels

- **Kelipah:** literally a "shell," a layer that conceals G-dliness. Chapter 1 says the animal soul comes from kelipah.
- **Sitra achra:** "the other side," a name Chapter 1 gives alongside kelipah.
- **Kelipat nogah:** a mixed realm of good and evil, which contains good. Tanya teaches that physical things from this realm can be raised up through a mitzvah (see [the animal soul](/learn/animal-soul-not-evil) and [eating as a mitzvah](/learn/eating-as-a-mitzvah)).
- **Ein Sof:** "the Infinite" (literally "without end"), a name for G-d; Tanya speaks of the light of the Ein Sof.
- **Tzimtzum:** the "compression" of G-d's wisdom so that people can grasp it, as Chapter 4 describes it.

## Ideas of the struggle

- **Itkafya** (also spelled iskafya): the constant effort to subdue the animal soul's desires ([itkafya](/learn/itkafya-self-discipline)).
- **Mind over heart:** the principle that the mind can guide the heart ([mind over heart](/learn/mind-over-heart-tanya)).
- **Mitzvah:** a commandment, and a way to connect to G-d ([what is a mitzvah](/learn/what-is-a-mitzvah)).
- **A dwelling in the lowest world:** the Sages' teaching, quoted in Chapter 37, that G-d desired an abode in the lower realms, our physical world.
- **Chitas:** the daily study of Chumash, Tehillim and Tanya ([daily Tanya](/learn/daily-tanya-cycle)).

## Common questions

### What does beinoni mean in Tanya?
Beinoni means the "intermediate person," one who does not sin in thought, speech or action even though the pull of the wrong desire is still felt.

### What is nefesh habahamit?
It is the animal soul: the natural self, with its drives and desires. See [is the animal soul bad?](/learn/animal-soul-not-evil).

### What is a kelipah?
A kelipah is a "shell," a layer that hides G-dliness. Tanya says the animal soul comes from kelipah, and kelipat nogah contains good that can be raised up.

### What do chochmah, binah and daat mean?
They are the soul's intellectual faculties: wisdom, understanding and knowledge.

### What is Sefer shel Beinonim?
The "Book of the Intermediates," another name for the Tanya's first part, because the beinoni is its main subject.
