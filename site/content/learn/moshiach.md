Moshiach (the Messiah) is a future leader from the family of King David who, according to Jewish belief, will bring the Jewish people back to the Land of Israel, rebuild the Holy Temple in Jerusalem, and usher in an era of peace and knowledge of G-d for the whole world. Belief in Moshiach is one of the 13 principles of Jewish faith listed by Maimonides.

## What does Moshiach mean?

Moshiach means "anointed one." In ancient Israel, kings and high priests were anointed with oil when they took office. Moshiach will be a human being, a great leader and scholar descended from King David, not a divine being.

## What will Moshiach do?

According to Maimonides, Moshiach will:

1. **Inspire all Jews** to follow the Torah and mitzvot.
2. **Fight the battles of G-d,** protecting the Jewish people.
3. **Rebuild the Holy Temple** (Beit Hamikdash) in Jerusalem.
4. **Gather the Jewish exiles** from around the world back to the Land of Israel.

When these are accomplished, it will be clear that he is Moshiach.

## What will the Messianic era be like?

The prophets describe a world transformed:

- **Peace:** "Nation will not lift up sword against nation, nor will they learn war anymore" (Isaiah 2:4).
- **No more hunger, jealousy or strife.** Maimonides writes that good things will be plentiful.
- **Knowledge of G-d:** "The earth will be filled with the knowledge of G-d as the waters cover the sea" (Isaiah 11:9).
- **The resurrection of the dead,** which will come in the later stage of the Messianic era.

Maimonides emphasizes that the world will continue to run naturally. The change will be in people's hearts and minds, as everyone recognizes G-d's goodness.

## How do we bring Moshiach?

Jewish tradition teaches that each person can help bring the redemption. Every mitzvah adds light to the world, and at some point the light will tip the balance. Maimonides writes that a single good deed could be the one that brings redemption for the whole world.

[The Rebbe](/learn/the-rebbe) placed great emphasis on this, encouraging people to:

- Add in acts of goodness and kindness.
- Learn about Moshiach and the redemption.
- Live with the expectation that Moshiach can come any day.

## Moshiach in daily life

Jews pray for Moshiach three times a day in the Amidah prayer. Many traditional Jews say each day: "I believe with complete faith in the coming of Moshiach, and even if he delays, I will wait for him every day."

## Common questions

### Is the Jewish Moshiach the same as the Christian Messiah?

No. In Jewish belief, Moshiach is a human leader who will bring about the specific changes described above, such as world peace, the ingathering of exiles and the rebuilding of the Temple. Since these have not yet happened, Jews believe Moshiach has not yet come.

### When will Moshiach come?

No one knows the exact time. The Talmud mentions that the redemption can come in its destined time or sooner, if we merit it. That is why every good deed matters.

### Why is Moshiach important to Chabad?

Chabad teaches that bringing the world to its perfected state is the purpose of creation. The Rebbe encouraged people to make the world ready for Moshiach through Torah, mitzvot and kindness.

### What can I do today?

Do one more mitzvah. Give [tzedakah](/learn/tzedakah), light Shabbat candles, help someone in need, or learn something new about Judaism.
