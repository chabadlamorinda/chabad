Looking for a Hebrew school in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda that your kids will actually look forward to? **Lamorinda Hebrew School** at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette offers hands-on, fun Jewish learning once a week, on Sundays, for just two hours. It is open to every Jewish child, with no synagogue membership or affiliation required.

## The basics

- **When:** Sundays, 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Who:** children ages 3 to 13
- **Where:** Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette
- **Tuition:** $600 per child, including books and supplies. A payment plan is available, and no child will be turned away for lack of funds.
- **Membership:** none required

## Three tracks, one school

**Early Childhood (ages 3 to 5).** Stories, songs, crafts and holiday celebrations. Little ones discover the joy of being Jewish through their hands and their hearts.

**Hebrew School (ages 5 to 12).** Hebrew reading with the **Aleph Champ** program, where children move up level by level at their own pace. Students learn the holidays and traditions, and build real Jewish pride.

**Bar/Bat Mitzvah (ages 12 to 13).** Personal preparation for the big day. See [bar and bat mitzvah in Lamorinda](/learn/bar-bat-mitzvah-in-lamorinda).

## The teachers

Lamorinda Hebrew School is directed by **Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski**, who holds a Master's degree in Education. Classes are taught by Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski, Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski, **Morah Shira Gold** and **Morah Rachel Schwartz**: warm, knowledgeable teachers who love helping children learn and grow.

## Why Sunday Hebrew school works for Lamorinda families

Weekdays in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are full of school, sports and activities. Two hours on Sunday morning gives your child a steady, joyful Jewish anchor without overloading the week. Children from Acalanes, Campolindo and Miramonte, and from every elementary and middle school in the three towns, learn side by side.

The Hebrew reading and Jewish knowledge they build here make bar and bat mitzvah preparation easier and more meaningful later on.

## Common questions

### Is there a Hebrew school in Moraga or Orinda?
Lamorinda Hebrew School in Lafayette serves Moraga and Orinda families. It is about ten minutes from Moraga and a few minutes from Orinda on Highway 24.

### Do we have to join a synagogue?
No. Lamorinda Hebrew School is open to all, with no membership or affiliation required.

### How much does Hebrew school cost?
Tuition is $600 per child, including books and supplies. A payment plan is available, and no child is turned away for lack of funds. Check the box for a payment plan or scholarship on the [registration form](/forms/hebrew-school).

### Can my child join in the middle of the year?
Call the Rebbetzin at 925-384-7242 to talk about your child and find the right place to start.

### My child has never learned Hebrew. Is that okay?
Yes. With Aleph Champ, every child starts at their own level and moves forward at their own pace.
