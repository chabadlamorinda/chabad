A child can start Hebrew school at any age. A three-year-old begins with stories, songs and crafts; a seven-year-old begins with the first Hebrew letters; an eleven-year-old can start too, and still reach a meaningful Bar or Bat Mitzvah. At Lamorinda Hebrew School, every child begins at their own level and moves forward at their own pace.

## Starting young: ages 3 to 5

Jewish tradition says a child's Torah learning begins as soon as they can speak, with the verse "Torah tzivah lanu Moshe" and the Shema. Many families mark the start of a boy's learning at his [upsherin](/learn/upsherin), his first haircut at three, often with honey on the Hebrew letters so that learning is sweet.

In the Early Childhood track (ages 3 to 5), children discover Judaism through stories, songs, crafts and holiday celebrations. They do not sit at desks for long; they sing, touch, make and play. That early joy becomes a love of learning.

## Starting in the middle: ages 5 to 12

Children join the Hebrew School track (ages 5 to 12) at every point along the way. Some come with a little Hebrew, some with none. That is fine. With **Aleph Champ**, Hebrew reading is taught level by level, so a child who starts at eight or nine begins at the first level and moves up as quickly as they can. No one waits for the class, and no one is left behind. See [Aleph Champ](/learn/aleph-champ-hebrew-reading).

Alongside reading, children learn prayer and the siddur, the weekly Torah portion, Israel, Jewish history, Shabbat and the holidays, and Jewish values, through games and creative projects.

## Starting late: ages 11 to 13

If your child is close to Bar or Bat Mitzvah age and has not had Jewish education, take a breath: it is not too late. The Bar/Bat Mitzvah track (ages 12 to 13) offers personal preparation, and girls can join the Bat Mitzvah Club. Read [preparing for a Bar or Bat Mitzvah without years of Hebrew school](/learn/bar-mitzvah-prep-without-hebrew-school) and [from Hebrew school to Bar and Bat Mitzvah](/learn/hebrew-school-to-bar-bat-mitzvah).

## Helping a new child feel at home

- Tell your child that everyone in Hebrew school started at the beginning once.
- Celebrate the first letters and the first Aleph Champ level.
- Bring a friend from school or the neighborhood.
- Ask with interest what they learned each Sunday.

## Common questions

### What is the best age to start Hebrew school?
The earlier the better, because children absorb songs, stories and letters easily, but any age is a good age to start. Lamorinda Hebrew School welcomes children from 3 to 13.

### Is my 10-year-old too old to start Hebrew school?
No. With Aleph Champ, your child starts at the first level and moves up at their own pace, without being compared to anyone.

### Can my child join in the middle of the year?

### My child is almost 12. Is there time to prepare for a Bar or Bat Mitzvah?
Yes. The Bar/Bat Mitzvah track gives personal preparation. Call 925-384-7242 to talk about your child.

### Does my child need to know any Hebrew first?
No. Every child begins at their own level, including the very first letters.
