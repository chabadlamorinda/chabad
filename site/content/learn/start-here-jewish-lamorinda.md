Welcome. If you are new to Jewish life, or coming back after a long time, you are in the right place, and you do not need to know anything yet. Your first step is simple: **come for Shabbat**, or call 925-384-7242 and say hello. Everything else on this page is a map you can use whenever you are ready.

## Your first step

Chabad of Lamorinda is at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, and welcomes Jews from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez. There is no membership and no dues. Start with one of these:

- Come to Saturday services at 10:00 AM and stay for a hot Kiddush lunch. See [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda).
- Ask for a place at a Friday night meal with the Rabbi's family through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal).
- Nervous about walking in? Read [first time at synagogue](/learn/first-time-at-synagogue) and [you do not have to be religious](/learn/not-religious-welcome).
- Just moved here? Read [moving to Lamorinda](/learn/moving-to-lamorinda).

## Common questions

### How do I get involved in Jewish life in Lafayette?
Come to Shabbat services on Saturday at 10 AM, join a class or event, or call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242. No membership is needed.

### Where can I find a Jewish community in Moraga or Orinda?
Moraga and Orinda families come to Chabad of Lamorinda at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, a few minutes away. See [Jewish Moraga](/jewish-moraga) and [Jewish Orinda](/jewish-orinda).

### I do not know any Hebrew. Can I still come?
Yes. Services have a prayer book with English, and the Rabbi explains as we go.

### Do I have to be a member or pay?
No. Everyone is welcome at every service, class and event.

### How can I learn about Judaism as an adult?
Join a Torah class, ask the Rabbi for a one-on-one study partner, or start with the guides above.
