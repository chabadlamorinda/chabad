Aaron (Aharon, אַהֲרֹן) was the older brother of Moses and Miriam, the first High Priest (Kohen Gadol) and the ancestor of all the Kohanim. He is remembered above all as the man who loved peace and pursued peace: the Mishnah teaches, "Be of the disciples of Aaron, loving peace and pursuing peace."

## Brother and partner of Moses

When Moses was sent to free the Jewish people, Aaron went with him and spoke to Pharaoh as his partner. [Moses](/learn/moses) was the younger brother, and Chabad.org highlights that Aaron accepted his brother's leadership with joy and without a trace of jealousy. The Torah says Aaron would rejoice in his heart when he saw Moses.

## The first High Priest

Aaron served in the Tabernacle throughout most of the forty years in the desert. His duties included the daily offerings and, on Yom Kippur, entering the Holy of Holies to offer the incense. His descendants, the Kohanim, carry on the tradition today, including blessing the people with the [priestly blessing](/learn/birkat-kohanim). See also [Kohen, Levi, Yisrael](/learn/kohen-levi-yisrael).

## The great peacemaker

Our Sages tell how Aaron made peace. When two people quarreled, he would go to one and say, "My friend is sorry and wants to make peace." Then he went to the other and said the same. When they met, each was ready to forgive, because each believed the other wanted peace. He also strengthened families: he made peace between husband and wife, so that, as our tradition says, many children were named Aaron. And when he saw someone doing wrong, he treated him with respect and warmth, so the person felt ashamed to disappoint such a friend and changed his ways.

## The Golden Calf

When Moses was delayed on Mount Sinai, the people demanded a substitute leader and brought their gold. Aaron tried to stall for time, hoping Moses would return, and a calf emerged from the fire. How to understand Aaron's part in this is discussed in Torah commentaries, and he was forgiven. Ask the Rabbi if you want to dig deeper.

## His death

Aaron died near the end of the forty years on Mount Hor, with his son Elazar, who received the priestly garments. The Torah says the whole house of Israel cried for thirty days. He died peacefully, and the people loved him.

## How to be a disciple of Aaron

1. **Be the first to reach out** after an argument.
2. **Say the kind true thing** about the other person to the one who is angry.
3. **Praise, do not shame.** Help people be proud of their best self.
4. **Keep peace at home.** [Forgiveness and making peace](/learn/forgiveness-and-making-peace) begin with those closest to us.

## Common questions

### Who was Aaron in the Bible?
Aaron was Moses's older brother, the first High Priest and the father of the Kohanim.

### Why is Aaron known for peace?
Our Sages tell that he would bring quarreling people and couples together by telling each side that the other wanted peace.

### What does "love peace and pursue peace" mean?
It is Hillel's teaching in the Mishnah (Pirkei Avot) to follow Aaron: not only to want peace but to actively pursue it.

### Was Aaron responsible for the Golden Calf?
The commentaries discuss his role at length; he tried to delay the people, and he was forgiven. Ask the Rabbi for details.

### Who are the Kohanim today?
They are the male descendants of Aaron through their fathers, who bless the congregation with the priestly blessing.
