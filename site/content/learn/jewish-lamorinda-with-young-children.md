Raising Jewish children in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda can be joyful, and you do not have to do it alone. From the first months to a bar or bat mitzvah, Chabad of Lamorinda has a program for each stage, and the whole family is welcome with no membership. Here is the path, step by step.

## Baby and toddler

If you are expecting or have a new baby, start with [new parents in Lamorinda](/learn/new-parents-in-lamorinda). For toddlers, see [holidays with toddlers](/learn/holidays-with-toddlers).

The [Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool), led by Tzipora Labkowski, welcomes children from 12 months to 4 years, Monday to Thursday 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM and Friday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. It is play-based and inspired by Reggio Emilia. Read [choosing a Jewish preschool](/learn/choosing-a-jewish-preschool), then [schedule a tour](/forms/preschool). The [summer camp](/programs/summer-camp) is for ages 2 to 4.

## Ages 5 to 13

- **Hebrew School.** On Sundays from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, for ages 3 to 13, with tracks for early childhood, ages 5 to 12, and bar and bat mitzvah preparation. See [Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) and [Hebrew school in Lamorinda](/learn/hebrew-school-in-lamorinda).
- **CKids afterschool.** Weekdays from 3:00 to 6:00 PM for ages 5 to 13, with homework help, sports, STEAM and Hebrew. See [CKids](/programs/ckids-afterschool).
- **Bar and Bat Mitzvah.** Preparation at your child's pace. See [bar and bat mitzvah in Lamorinda](/learn/bar-bat-mitzvah-in-lamorinda) and the [program](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah).

## A simple first year

You do not need to do everything. Many families begin like this:

1. **This week:** light candles on Friday night and bless the children. See [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles).
2. **This month:** visit for Shabbat morning or a children's event on the [events calendar](/events).
3. **This season:** join the next holiday celebration and try its guide, such as [Chanukah for kids](/learn/chanukah-for-kids).
4. **This year:** choose the program that fits your child's age: preschool, Hebrew School or CKids.

Parents in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda often say the friendships their children make are the greatest gift. Questions about cost or fit? Call 925-384-7242. Hebrew School tuition has a payment plan, and the preschool offers need-based scholarships.

## Common questions

### Is there a Jewish preschool in Lafayette?
Yes. The Lamorinda Jewish Preschool at Chabad takes children from 12 months to 4 years. Call 925-384-7242 for a tour.

### When is Hebrew school in Lamorinda?
Sundays from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, for ages 3 to 13.

### Is there Jewish afterschool in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda?
CKids is weekdays from 3:00 to 6:00 PM for ages 5 to 13, with homework help, sports and Hebrew.

### Do I have to be a member to join?
No. There is no membership, and no child is turned away from Hebrew school for lack of funds.

### How do I start Jewish traditions with my children?
Begin with one: Friday night candles. See [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles) and add one tradition at a time.
