Nissan (נִיסָן) is the first month of the Jewish calendar and the month of redemption, when the Jewish people left Egypt. It falls in March or April, the Torah calls it "the month of spring" (Chodesh HaAviv), and it holds Passover, the blessing over blossoming trees and a special custom of reading about the twelve princes of Israel.

## The first of the months

The Torah says of the month of the Exodus, "This month shall be for you the head of the months" (Exodus 12:2). So even though the Jewish new year in the fall is Rosh Hashanah, the months are counted from Nissan. Chabad.org notes that the Jewish calendar has two "starts," Nissan for months and Tishrei for years, and you can read more in [the Jewish calendar](/learn/jewish-calendar) and about each [Rosh Chodesh](/learn/rosh-chodesh). Nissan has 30 days, and the month after it, [Iyar](/learn/iyar), has 29.

## Spring and new beginnings

In Nissan the whole Jewish people celebrate new life: trees bloom, the days are longer and the earth turns green again. The Exodus was the birth of the Jewish nation, so Nissan is the time to ask what in our own life needs to be set free. Many people use the month to start something new, such as a regular Shabbat, a class or a mitzvah.

## What happens in Nissan

- **The twelve princes (Nesi'im).** During the first 12 days of the month, each tribal leader brought a gift to dedicate the Tabernacle (Mishkan), one on each day. Today it is customary to read the Torah passage about each day's gift, along with a special prayer. Ask the Rabbi where to find it.
- **No fasting and no Tachanun.** Chabad custom is not to say the Tachanun prayer or fast for the whole month, because it is a happy month. Customs differ in other communities, so ask the Rabbi if you are unsure.
- **The blessing on blossoming trees.** When you see a fruit tree in bloom in Nissan, you say a blessing, once a year. Chabad.org says it can be done at any time in the month. See [the blessing on blossoming trees](/learn/blessing-on-blossoming-trees). Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are full of beautiful trees in the spring.
- **Passover.** Passover begins on the 15th of Nissan. Preparing starts well before: see [Passover for beginners](/learn/passover-for-beginners), [Passover cleaning](/learn/passover-cleaning), [kosher for Passover](/learn/kosher-for-passover) and [Maot Chitim](/learn/maot-chitim), the charity that helps others have a Seder.
- **The Omer.** On the second night of Passover we begin counting the Omer, 49 days leading up to Shavuot. See [counting the Omer](/learn/counting-the-omer).

## Two special days

**11 Nissan** is the birthday of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, and Chabad communities gather in his honor. Many people take on an extra act of kindness or study that day.

One opinion in the Talmud (Rabbi Yehoshua) is that the three Patriarchs, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, were born and passed away in Nissan.

## Why this is the month of redemption

Jewish tradition teaches that what happened in Egypt happens in every generation: every person has an Egypt, a place where they feel narrow and stuck. The Hebrew word for Egypt, Mitzrayim, is related to the word for "limits." Nissan is the time when G-d gives us the strength to break through them. It is not just ancient history. It is an invitation to be free today, to choose a better life and to help others do the same.

## Common questions

### What is the month of Nissan?
Nissan is the first month of the Jewish calendar, falling in March or April. It is the month of the Exodus and of Passover, and the Torah calls it the month of spring.

### Why is Nissan called the first month?
The Torah says of the month of the Exodus, "This month shall be for you the first of the months." Jewish months are counted from Nissan, even though the new year begins in Tishrei.

### Why don't we say Tachanun in Nissan?
The first twelve days commemorate the dedication of the Tabernacle, and since most of the month has no Tachanun, Chabad custom omits it all month. Ask the Rabbi about your community's custom.

### When do I say the blessing on blossoming trees?
You say it once a year, when you first see fruit trees in bloom, at any time in Nissan. Visiting a park or garden in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda in spring is a good way to do it.

### What is 11 Nissan?
It is the birthday of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, a day of increased kindness and learning in Chabad communities.
