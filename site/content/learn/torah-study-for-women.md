Torah belongs to every Jew. In Chabad, women's Torah study is not an afterthought. It is a priority, and many women find it becomes one of the most meaningful parts of their week.

## The Rebbe's encouragement

The Lubavitcher [Rebbe](/learn/the-rebbe) spoke often and strongly about women's Torah study. He taught that in our generation women should learn Torah in depth, including Chassidus, so that their Judaism is built on understanding and not only on habit. He encouraged women's classes, women's gatherings and Torah learning for girls everywhere.

## What women learn

Jewish law teaches that women should study the laws that apply to their own lives, so they know how to keep them. The Alter Rebbe, founder of Chabad, wrote this in his Laws of Torah Study. Beyond that, women in Chabad commonly study:

- **The [weekly parsha](/learn/weekly-parsha)** with commentaries such as Rashi.
- **Practical Jewish law:** Shabbat, kosher, holidays, blessings and family life.
- **[Tanya](/learn/tanya)** and other Chassidus: the soul, love and awe of G-d, and finding meaning in daily life. See [Chassidus](/learn/chassidus).
- **[Tehillim](/learn/tehillim)**, Pirkei Avot and the stories of great Jewish women in Tanach.
- **Daily study:** the Chabad custom of daily Chumash, Tehillim and Tanya (called Chitas), and the daily Rambam. See [Daily Torah Study](/learn/daily-torah-study).

## Women's mitzvahs

Three mitzvahs are traditionally entrusted especially to Jewish women:

1. **Lighting [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles)**, bringing light into the home.
2. **[Separating challah](/learn/hafrashat-challah)** when baking bread.
3. **[Family purity](/learn/family-purity)**, bringing holiness into marriage.

Women also shape the Jewish home: its kosher kitchen, its Shabbat table and its warmth. Learning gives depth and joy to all of these. See [Jewish Women](/learn/jewish-women).

## Ways to learn

- **One-on-one** with a teacher or a friend (a chavruta).
- **A women's class** or a group of friends studying together.
- **Five minutes a day** with a short daily study or a parsha thought.
- **Online:** recorded classes and articles you can listen to in the car or on a walk.
- **Before a life event:** a bride learns in [kallah classes](/learn/kallah-classes); a mother may learn with her daughter before her [Bat Mitzvah](/learn/bat-mitzvah).

## Common questions

### I never learned Hebrew. Can I still study Torah?
Absolutely. Much of Torah is available in English, and many women start that way. If you would like to learn to read Hebrew, see [Hebrew Reading for Adults](/learn/hebrew-reading-for-adults).

### Is it really encouraged for women to learn Chassidus?
Yes. The Rebbe placed great emphasis on it. Tanya speaks to every Jewish soul.

### I'm busy with young children. How can I learn?
Start small: a few minutes a day, a recorded class while you cook, or a short weekly call with a friend. Small, steady learning adds up.
