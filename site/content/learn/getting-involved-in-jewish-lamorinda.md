There is a place for you at Chabad of Lamorinda, whether you have an hour a month or a lifetime of experience. You can come, learn, help and give, in any order. The easiest first step is to show up once: come to Shabbat or a class, and the rest follows.

## Come

- **Shabbat.** Saturday services at 10:00 AM with a hot Kiddush lunch, and Friday night Kabbalat Shabbat at {kabbalat-shabbat}. See [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda), and the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) for a Friday night dinner.
- **Weekday mornings.** Morning services are Sunday at 8:00 AM and Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM. A [first-timer's guide](/learn/shacharit-morning-service) will help.
- **Events.** See the [events calendar](/events) and hear about holiday celebrations first through the [weekly email](/forms/newsletter).

## Learn

Weekly [Torah classes for adults](/programs/adult-education) include English Torah on Sunday at 9 PM, a Hebrew class with a meal on Tuesday at 8:30 PM, and a Tanya class on Wednesday at 1 PM. Prefer one-on-one? Ask the Rabbi for a study partner. Read more in [Torah classes in Lamorinda](/learn/torah-classes-in-lamorinda).

## Make friends

- The [Jewish Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle) offers creative evenings, challah bakes and workshops.
- The [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club) has Bagels, Lox and Tefillin, and good company.
- Families will find their place through [kids and teens](/programs/kids-and-teens) programs.

## Help

Chabad runs on volunteers. You can bake challah, set up the Kiddush, help at holiday events, or host guests at your own Shabbat table. Sign up on the [volunteer form](/forms/volunteer) and read [volunteering in Lamorinda](/learn/volunteering-in-lamorinda).

## Give

Chabad of Lamorinda has no membership dues. The community is carried by those who choose to give:

- The [Chai Club](/chai-club): monthly partners giving in multiples of 18.
- [Sponsor a Kiddush](/sponsor-a-kiddush) for a birthday, anniversary or yahrzeit. See [sponsoring a Kiddush](/learn/sponsoring-a-kiddush).
- A one-time or monthly [gift](/donate). Read [supporting Chabad of Lamorinda](/learn/supporting-chabad-of-lamorinda).

## Not sure where you fit?

Start with what you enjoy. Love to cook? Bake challah or help with a holiday meal. Enjoy people? Host a guest at your Shabbat table through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal). Like to learn? Join a class. Live in Moraga or Orinda and worry about the drive? Remember that Chabad is only minutes from all three towns, and that Orinda is a single BART stop away. Every Jew in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda is welcome, however much or little Judaism you grew up with. Call Rabbi Labkowski at 925-384-7242 and say what you like to do; we will find a place for it.

If you are brand new, our [moving to Lamorinda](/learn/moving-to-lamorinda) guide lists a good first month, and [the Jewish year in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-year-in-lamorinda) shows the festivals you can join.

## Common questions

### How can I volunteer at Chabad of Lamorinda?
Fill in the [volunteer form](/forms/volunteer) or call 925-384-7242. There are jobs for every schedule.

### How do I join the Jewish Women's Circle?
Join the list through the [Women's Circle page](/programs/womens-circle) to hear about the next evening.

### How can I sponsor a Kiddush in Lafayette?
Visit [sponsor a Kiddush](/sponsor-a-kiddush) to choose a Shabbat and dedicate it to a celebration or in memory of a loved one.

### Is there a fee to be part of the community?
No. There are no membership dues. Giving is a choice.

### What is the Chai Club?
A group of monthly partners who give in multiples of 18, which makes the community possible.
