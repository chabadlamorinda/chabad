A good Chanukah dinner has something fried in oil, usually latkes, and is built around either a meat menu or a dairy menu, never both at once. Chabad.org says eating oily foods is a custom of at least a thousand years, and dairy dishes are customary too, so both menus below are traditional.

## First, choose: meat or dairy?

Jewish law keeps meat and dairy apart (see [meat and milk](/learn/meat-and-milk)), so decide before you shop. The dairy custom recalls Yehudit (Judith), who fed cheese to an enemy general (see [Chanukah foods](/learn/chanukah-foods)). A meat dinner is just as welcome. Families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda can do dairy on one night and meat on another.

[Pareve](/learn/pareve) foods, such as plain potato latkes, vegetables, salad, fruit and fish, go with either menu.

## Menu one: a meat dinner

1. **Start:** a simple soup or a green salad.
2. **Main:** roast chicken or a brisket, whatever your family loves. Chabad.org even has a recipe for pastrami latkes with horseradish sauce, which can only go on a meat table.
3. **Latkes:** classic potato latkes made with oil, eggs and no dairy, with applesauce. See our [latkes recipe](/learn/latkes).
4. **Sides:** roasted carrots or other vegetables.
5. **Dessert:** pareve doughnuts ([sufganiyot](/learn/sufganiyot)) and pareve chocolate gelt.

The one rule: serve applesauce, not sour cream, with the latkes, and check that the doughnuts, cake and gelt are marked pareve. Margarine or a non-dairy creamer are pareve only if the label and kosher symbol say so.

## Menu two: a dairy dinner

1. **Start:** a creamy vegetable soup and bread.
2. **Main:** cheese latkes or zucchini-cheese latkes, both of which appear among Chabad.org's Chanukah recipes. Add baked fish, which is pareve, or a pasta dish.
3. **Latkes:** potato latkes with sour cream and applesauce.
4. **Side:** a big salad.
5. **Dessert:** sufganiyot, a cheesecake, or any dairy sweet, and the kids' gelt.

Cheese needs its own kosher check, so see [kosher cheese](/learn/kosher-cheese).

## Kosher notes for the kitchen

- Use ingredients with a reliable kosher symbol (see [kosher symbols](/learn/kosher-symbols)).
- Check leafy vegetables and herbs for insects ([checking for insects](/learn/checking-for-insects)).
- Use separate pots, pans and serving dishes for meat and dairy.
- Meat and dairy dishes should not share a table at the same meal. If you serve dairy after a meat meal, ask the Rabbi how long to wait, and keep your meat and dairy dishes clearly marked.
- Do not fry dairy and meat items in the same pan.

Need kosher meat, groceries or holiday foods in the East Bay? [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda, is always kosher.

## How to plan the evening

1. **Light first.** Light the menorah at the right time, then sit down to eat. Tonight's lighting is on the [menorah page](/menorah) and in [what to do each night](/learn/chanukah-night-by-night).
2. **Cook ahead.** Latkes freeze well, and soup and brisket taste better a day later. Reheat latkes in a hot oven.
3. **Keep one dish simple.** If the table is full of fried food, add a salad.
4. **Make it a holiday.** Sing, play [dreidel](/learn/dreidel) and give gelt. See [the festive meal on Chanukah](/learn/seudat-chanukah).

## Common questions

### What do you serve for a Chanukah dinner?
Serve something fried in oil, usually potato latkes, with either a meat menu or a dairy menu. Add salad, vegetables, and doughnuts or another dessert.

### Can I serve latkes with a meat meal?
Yes, if the latkes are pareve: made with potatoes, onion, eggs, flour, salt and oil, and no dairy. Serve them with applesauce, not sour cream.

### Is a dairy dinner on Chanukah required?
No. Dairy is a custom, not a command. Chabad.org suggests hosting a dairy party in memory of Yehudit, but a meat dinner is also fine.

### Can I have dessert with milk after a meat dinner?
Not right away. Jewish law requires waiting after meat before dairy. The Chabad custom, like that of most communities, is to wait six hours, though some families have other customs. Ask the Rabbi what is right for your family, or serve a pareve dessert.

### What if some guests keep kosher and others do not?
Keep the whole meal kosher, so everyone can eat at your table. See [hosting a kosher guest](/learn/hosting-a-kosher-guest).

### Are doughnuts and gelt pareve?
It depends on the ingredients and the kosher symbol, since some are dairy. Read the label, or ask Bay Kosher or the Rabbi.
