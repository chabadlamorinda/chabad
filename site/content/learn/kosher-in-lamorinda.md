Keeping kosher in Lamorinda is easier than you might think. Whether you have kept kosher all your life or you are just starting with one step, Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette is here to help families in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez, with food, guidance and holiday orders.

## Bay Kosher: kosher food, delivered

**[Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher)** is a project of Chabad of Lamorinda that makes keeping kosher in the East Bay easy. Order online and have it delivered:

- Kosher **meat and poultry**
- Kosher **groceries**
- **Holiday foods**
- **Judaica**, such as Shabbat candlesticks, menorahs and Kiddush cups

Bay Kosher brings kosher food to your door. You can also support it through the [Bay Kosher Market fund](/donate), which keeps kosher food available in the East Bay.

## Kosher your kitchen, at your pace

The Rabbi will happily help you kosher your kitchen, step by step, at your pace. That can mean a full kashering of your sink, stove and oven, or simply a first conversation about where to begin. Many families start small: kosher meat only, or separate dishes for meat and milk. Every step counts.

Call 925-384-7242 or [contact us](/contact) to set a time. Before he comes, read our guides:

- [Kosher basics](/learn/kosher)
- [Setting up a kosher kitchen](/learn/kosher-kitchen)
- [Meat and milk](/learn/meat-and-milk)
- [Kosher symbols](/learn/kosher-symbols) to read labels at any grocery store
- [Immersing new dishes](/learn/tevilat-kelim): ask the Rabbi how to do this locally

## Holiday orders

Several holidays need special items, and we make them easy to get here:

- **Passover:** [order handmade shmurah matzah and Seder-to-go kits](/forms/matzah), and [sell your chametz](/forms/sell-chametz) online through the Rabbi. See [kosher for Passover](/learn/kosher-for-passover) and the [Passover page](/holidays/passover).
- **Sukkot:** [order a lulav and etrog set](/forms/lulav-etrog), standard, mehudar or a children's set. See [Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot).
- **Chanukah:** ask us for a menorah and candles for your home. See [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah).

## Kosher at our table

Want to see a kosher Shabbat table in action? Come to the hot Kiddush lunch after Shabbat services on Saturday, or a Friday night Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family, with Jewish songs and warm conversation. [RSVP for Shabbat](/forms/shabbat-meal).
