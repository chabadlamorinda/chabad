Chanukah is the perfect holiday to open your home. It lasts eight nights, the main mitzvah takes a few minutes, and everyone, from toddlers to grandparents, can take part. A Chanukah party does not need to be big or fancy: a menorah, some latkes, a dreidel and a few friends make a wonderful evening.

## The heart of the party: lighting together

Plan the party around the lighting, right after nightfall (on Friday, before Shabbat begins; on Saturday night, after Shabbat ends). Gather everyone around the [menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah), turn down the lights, and:

1. Light the **shamash**, the helper candle.
2. Say the **blessings** (three on the first night, two on the other nights).
3. Light the candles, starting from the newest one on the left.
4. Sing **Haneirot Halalu** and **Maoz Tzur**.

Give each child, or each family, a menorah of their own to light; many families light one per person. Place the menorahs in a window or by the door so the light is seen outside. Let the candles burn at least 30 minutes, and do not leave them unattended.

## Food

It is a custom to eat foods made with oil, recalling the miracle of the oil: **latkes** and **sufganiyot** (jelly doughnuts) are the classics. Some also eat dairy foods, remembering the heroism of Yehudit. If your guests keep kosher, see [Chanukah foods](/learn/chanukah-foods) and [keeping a kosher kitchen](/learn/kosher-kitchen), or keep the menu simple with packaged kosher treats.

## Fun for everyone

- **[Dreidel](/learn/dreidel)** tournaments, with chocolate coins or nuts.
- **[Chanukah gelt](/learn/chanukah-gelt)** for the kids, with a little extra for tzedakah.
- **Tell the [Chanukah story](/learn/chanukah-story)**, short and dramatic, or let the kids act it out.
- **Decorate doughnuts** or make a menorah from simple crafts.
- **Light our online menorah** at [chabadoflamorinda.com/menorah](/menorah) to learn the blessings before the party.

## Invite someone new

Chanukah is about spreading light outward. Invite a neighbor, a new family in town, a college student home for break, a senior who lives alone, or someone who has never lit a menorah. See [welcoming guests](/learn/hachnasat-orchim).

## Common questions

### Can we have the party on Friday night?
Yes, but on Friday the menorah is lit just before the Shabbat candles, with candles big enough to burn until 30 minutes after nightfall. Then the evening becomes a Shabbat dinner. See [Chanukah on Shabbat](/learn/chanukah-on-shabbat).

### Can a guest light at my house?
Yes. Guests can light their own menorah at your home, with the blessings. If they will go home to light later that night, ask the Rabbi what is best.

### Do I need a lot of menorahs?
No. One menorah is enough for the household. More menorahs simply add more light and let more people take part.
