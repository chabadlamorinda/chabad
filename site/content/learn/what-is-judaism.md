Judaism is the faith and way of life of the Jewish people, built on belief in one G-d who created and runs the world, and on the Torah, the instructions He gave the Jewish people at Mount Sinai. It is both a religion and a family: a Jew is someone born to a Jewish mother or someone who converted properly, and every Jew is welcome at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, whatever his or her background.

## One G-d

Jewish belief begins with one G-d, the single Creator and Sustainer of the world. He has no partners, no helpers and no rivals. He is not physical, He knows everything, past, present and future, and He cares about what each of us does.

People have free choice. Judaism teaches that G-d rewards those who follow His ways, in this world and in the World to Come, and that history is moving toward a future time of peace, the era of Moshiach. You can read more in our guides on [belief in G-d](/learn/belief-in-g-d) and [Moshiach](/learn/moshiach).

## The Torah, written and oral

The Torah, the Five Books of Moses, tells of creation, of Abraham and his family, and of G-d's instructions for life. Nineteen more books, the Prophets and the Writings, follow later Jewish history. Alongside them is the [Oral Torah](/learn/oral-torah), the teachings of the sages, later written down in the Mishnah, the Talmud and the Midrash.

Torah is not only history. It is studied every day, in schools, yeshivahs, homes and online. See [what the Torah is](/learn/torah) and how to begin with [daily Torah study](/learn/daily-torah-study).

## Mitzvahs: a way of living

The Torah contains 613 commandments, called mitzvahs (מִצְוֹת). Many of them shape ordinary days:

- [Shabbat](/learn/shabbat), from Friday night to Saturday night, a day of rest, prayer and festive meals.
- [Kosher](/learn/kosher) food: which animals may be eaten, and the separation of meat and dairy.
- Daily prayer, centered on the [Shema](/learn/shema), and [tefillin](/learn/tefillin) for men.
- A [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) on the doorposts of a Jewish home.
- The holidays: the High Holidays, Sukkot, Chanukah, Purim, Passover and Shavuot.

Nobody does everything at once. Chabad teaches that each mitzvah matters and that every step counts.

## A people and a covenant

Judaism rests on G-d's covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob and on the revelation at Sinai, when the Jewish people accepted the Torah. That is why Judaism feels like family as well as faith. We call Abraham's descendants the Jewish people, and Jews everywhere share a story and a responsibility to one another. Non-Jews are not asked to become Jewish: Judaism teaches that they follow the seven [Noahide laws](/learn/noahide-laws). To learn who counts as a Jew, see [who is a Jew](/learn/who-is-a-jew).

## The Land of Israel and the Jewish calendar

The Land of Israel is the sacred inheritance of the Jewish people, with Jerusalem as its holiest city. The Western Wall is the central place of Jewish prayer today. Jewish time has its own rhythm too: see the [Jewish calendar](/learn/jewish-calendar) and this week's [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times).

## Home and community

Judaism lives mostly at home: candles on Friday night, a family meal, a kosher kitchen, a child learning to read Hebrew. It also lives in community. In Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, families gather at Chabad of Lamorinda for Shabbat, holidays and classes, and nobody is ever asked whether they are members.

## Common questions

### What is Judaism in simple words?
Judaism is the belief in one G-d and the Torah He gave the Jewish people, lived through mitzvahs, holidays and community. It is the oldest of the world's monotheistic faiths and a way of life, not only a set of ideas.

### What do Jews believe?
Jews believe in one G-d who created the world and cares about it, that the Torah was given at Sinai, that people have free choice, and that the world is moving toward a time of peace under Moshiach.

### Is Judaism a religion or a people?
Both. A person is Jewish by being born to a Jewish mother or by a proper conversion, and the faith and the people are one.

### Do you have to be religious to be Jewish?
No. A Jew is a Jew from birth or conversion, whatever he or she observes. Chabad invites every Jew to take on more at his or her own pace. Read [not religious? you are welcome](/learn/not-religious-welcome).

### What is the Torah?
The Torah is the Five Books of Moses, together with the oral teachings that explain it. It is Judaism's guide to life.
