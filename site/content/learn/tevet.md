Tevet is the fourth month of the Jewish year counting from Rosh Hashanah, and it usually falls in December and January. It opens in the middle of Chanukah, with the menorah still glowing in the window, and continues through the coldest, darkest weeks of winter.

## Days to know in Tevet

- **Rosh Chodesh Tevet** comes during Chanukah, so on that day we light the menorah, say Hallel and read from the Torah for both the new month and Chanukah.
- **The last days of Chanukah**, ending on 2 or 3 Tevet, with [Zot Chanukah](/learn/zot-chanukah), the eighth day.
- **5 Tevet, [Hey Teves](/learn/hey-teves).** The day in 1987 when an American court ruled that the library of the Chabad Rebbes belongs to the Chassidic community. Chabad celebrates it by buying and learning from new Jewish books.
- **10 Tevet, Asara B'Tevet.** A fast day from dawn until nightfall, remembering the day the Babylonian army began its siege of Jerusalem, the beginning of the destruction of the First Temple. See [the minor fast days](/learn/minor-fast-days).
- **20 Tevet.** The yahrzeit of the [Rambam](/learn/maimonides), Rabbi Moses Maimonides, in 1204.
- **24 Tevet.** The yahrzeit of the Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, the founder of Chabad and author of the [Tanya](/learn/tanya), in 1812.

## A month to turn the cold into warmth

Tevet begins with light and then turns serious: a fast for Jerusalem and the yahrzeits of two great teachers. The Chassidic lesson is to carry Chanukah's light into ordinary winter days. When it is cold and dark outside, we make the inside warm: Shabbat tables with guests, a regular class, a new book of Torah on the shelf, and a little more [tzedakah](/learn/tzedakah).

## How to make Tevet count

- **Finish Chanukah strong**, lighting all eight nights.
- **Buy a new Jewish book** on Hey Teves and learn from it.
- **Keep the fast** of the Tenth of Tevet if you are healthy, and add a prayer for Jerusalem.
- **Learn something from the Rambam** on 20 Tevet, and a teaching of the [Tanya](/learn/tanya) on 24 Tevet.

## Common questions

### When is Tevet this year?
Tevet usually begins in December, during Chanukah. See the [Jewish calendar](/calendar) for this year's dates.

### Can the Tenth of Tevet fall on a Friday?
Yes. It is the only fast that is kept on a Friday when it falls then, fasting right up to Shabbat. The exact end of the fast is on our calendar.

### Who should not fast on the Tenth of Tevet?
Pregnant and nursing women, people who are ill, and anyone for whom fasting is a health risk usually do not fast. Ask the Rabbi about your own situation.
