Chabad of Lamorinda has **no membership dues** and no building fund fee. Anyone can walk in for Shabbat, a class, a holiday or help in a hard time, and nobody is ever asked for a ticket. So how does it all happen? Through the generosity of families in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and the nearby towns who believe in a Jewish home that is open to every Jew. Every gift is tax deductible: Chabad of Lamorinda is a 501(c)(3), tax ID 47-4642750.

## What your support makes possible

Shabbat services and the weekly Kiddush lunch. Shabbat dinners with the Rabbi's family for newcomers and students. Lamorinda Jewish Preschool and Hebrew School, where no child is turned away for lack of funds. Holiday celebrations from Rosh Hashanah to Passover. Visits to the sick, help for families after a loss, and a Rabbi who answers the phone when someone needs him.

## The Chai Club: monthly partners

The [Chai Club](/programs/chai-club) is the backbone of our community. Partners give a monthly gift in multiples of 18, the number for "chai," life: **$18, $36, $72, $180, $360** and up. A steady monthly gift, at any level, lets us plan with confidence. Give Chai, get Chai!

## Choose where your gift goes

When you [donate](/donate), you can choose a fund:

- **General Fund**, where it is needed most
- **Chai Club**, monthly partnership
- **Jewish Preschool Scholarships**, helping every child attend
- **Children's Programs**: Hebrew School, CKids and children's events
- **Holiday Programs**, from Rosh Hashanah to Passover, for the whole community
- **Synagogue**: services, Kiddush and Torah classes
- **Bay Kosher Market**, keeping kosher food available in the East Bay
- **Building Campaign**, a permanent home for Jewish life in Lamorinda
- **End of Year Campaign**, and **paying a pledge**

## The Building Campaign

The Building Campaign is helping to build a permanent home for Jewish life in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda. Every level of giving is recognized, from Supporter ($1,800) and Patron ($5,000) through Benefactor ($18,000), Builder ($100,000) and up to Visionary. To talk about a gift or a dedication, call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242.

## Common questions

### Does Chabad of Lamorinda charge membership dues?
No. There is no membership and no dues. Chabad of Lamorinda is supported by donations, including monthly Chai Club partners.

### Are donations to Chabad of Lamorinda tax deductible?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, tax ID 47-4642750.

### Can I give from a donor advised fund or with stock?
Yes. Recommend a grant to Chabad of Lamorinda, tax ID 47-4642750. For stock, crypto, wire or ACH, call 925-384-7242 or email info@chabadoflamorinda.com.

### What is the Chai Club?
A monthly partnership in multiples of 18, starting at $18 a month. [Join the Chai Club](/programs/chai-club).
