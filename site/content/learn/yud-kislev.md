In the Hebrew month of Kislev, Chassidim mark two days in a row connected to the second Chabad Rebbe, **Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch** (1773 to 1827), known as the **Mitteler Rebbe**, the "middle Rebbe." On **9 Kislev** he was born, and on the same day, years later, he passed away. On **10 Kislev**, Yud Kislev, he was freed from prison.

## Who was the Mitteler Rebbe?

He was the son and successor of the Alter Rebbe, the founder of Chabad. He moved the center of the movement to the town of Lubavitch, which gave Chabad-Lubavitch its name, and wrote many deep works that explain Chassidic teaching at length, so that every Chassid could understand and live it. He was known for prayer so absorbed that he did not notice the world around him. See [the Chabad Rebbes](/learn/chabad-rebbes).

## The liberation of Yud Kislev

In 1826 the Mitteler Rebbe was arrested after false accusations were made against him to the Russian authorities, much like his father before him. After an investigation he was cleared and released on 10 Kislev. Chassidim celebrate the day as a festival of liberation, with gatherings, stories and songs, a few days before the Alter Rebbe's own liberation on [Yud Tes Kislev](/learn/yud-tes-kislev).

## Born and passed away on the same day

The Mitteler Rebbe passed away on 9 Kislev, his birthday. Jewish tradition sees this as a sign of a complete life: a righteous person "fills their days," finishing their mission exactly. The day is both a yahrzeit and a celebration.

## How people mark the days

- **Learn** a teaching of the Mitteler Rebbe, or a story about him.
- **Gather and farbreng**: friends sharing words of Torah, a song and a l'chaim.
- **Give tzedakah** and add a mitzvah in his merit.
- **Make a good resolution** for the month of Kislev, the month of light and miracles.

## Common questions

### When are 9 and 10 Kislev this year?
They come in the Hebrew month of Kislev, usually in late November or early December. See our [Jewish calendar](/calendar).

### Why do Chabad Chassidim celebrate a Rebbe's release from prison?
Because the Rebbes were arrested for spreading Torah and Chassidus. Their release was a victory for that teaching, and the days remind every Jew that light wins in the end.

### Is there a special prayer for these days?
There is no special prayer in the prayer book. The day is marked by learning, gatherings, tzedakah and good resolutions.
