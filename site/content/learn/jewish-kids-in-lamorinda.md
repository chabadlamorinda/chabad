Raising Jewish kids in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda? Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette has a place for every age, from a first birthday to a bar or bat mitzvah. Children are a central focus here, and every program is open to every Jewish family, with no membership required.

## Ages 12 months to 4: Lamorinda Jewish Preschool

[Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool) is a warm, play-based Jewish preschool, inspired by Reggio Emilia, with two classes: 12 to 24 months and 2 to 4 years. It runs Monday to Thursday 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM and Friday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and is directed by Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski. Enrollment for 2026 to 2027 is open. [Schedule a tour](/forms/preschool), and read [choosing a Jewish preschool](/learn/choosing-a-jewish-preschool) for what to ask.

## Ages 2 to 4 in summer: Lamorinda Jewish Summer Camp

[Lamorinda Jewish Summer Camp](/programs/summer-camp) is seven weeks of Jewish songs, Shabbat parties, water play, arts and crafts and friendship, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Spaces are limited, so [register early](/forms/summer-camp). Ask about winter camp too.

## Ages 3 to 13: Lamorinda Hebrew School

[Lamorinda Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) meets Sundays 10 AM to 12 PM. Early Childhood (ages 3 to 5) is stories, songs and crafts; Hebrew School (ages 5 to 12) teaches Hebrew reading with Aleph Champ, holidays and Jewish pride; and the Bar/Bat Mitzvah track (ages 12 to 13) prepares for the big day. Tuition is $600 per child and no child is turned away for lack of funds. See [Hebrew school in Lamorinda](/learn/hebrew-school-in-lamorinda).

## Ages 5 to 13 on weekdays: CKids Afterschool

[CKids Lamorinda Afterschool](/programs/ckids-afterschool) runs weekdays 3 to 6 PM with homework help, daily sports and STEAM, modern Hebrew, weekly specials such as drama, dance, karate and a Shabbat sing, holiday cooking, storytelling and field trips, plus snacks and a hot meal. [Register for CKids](/forms/ckids).

## Ages 11 to 13: Bar and Bat Mitzvah

Personal preparation with the Rabbi and Rebbetzin, including tefillin and Torah reading for boys and the Bat Mitzvah Club for girls. See [bar and bat mitzvah in Lamorinda](/learn/bar-bat-mitzvah-in-lamorinda).

## Holiday events for kids

Kids' favorites through the year include the Model Matzah Bakery before Passover, the Kids Hamantashen Bake for Purim, Make Your Own Menorah for Chanukah, Sushi in the Sukkah, the Havdalah Glow Party and Mezuzah 4 U. Upcoming ones are on the [kids and teen events page](/programs/kids-and-teens) and the [events calendar](/events). See [the Jewish year in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-year-in-lamorinda) for every holiday.

## Shabbat with kids

Children take part in Shabbat morning services every Saturday at 10 AM, and stay for the hot Kiddush lunch. Friday night Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family is a family-friendly table too. [RSVP here](/forms/shabbat-meal). For home, see [Shabbat with kids](/learn/shabbat-with-kids).

## Milestones

A new baby, an [upsherin](/learn/upsherin) at age three, a first mezuzah on a child's bedroom door: the Rabbi and Rebbetzin are here for each one. See [new baby help](/help/new-baby).

## Common questions

### What Jewish programs are there for kids in Lamorinda?
Lamorinda Jewish Preschool (12 months to 4 years), summer camp (ages 2 to 4), Sunday Hebrew School (3 to 13), CKids afterschool (5 to 13), bar and bat mitzvah preparation and holiday events, all at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette.

### Can kids from Moraga and Orinda join?
Yes. The Hebrew School, CKids and teen programs are open to students from Moraga, Orinda and Lafayette schools.

### Do we need to be members?
No. Every program is open to all, with no membership required.

### How do I hear about kids' events?
Join the [weekly email](/forms/newsletter) and check "Kids and family programs."
