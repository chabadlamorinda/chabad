Studying at **Saint Mary's College of California** in Moraga or at **Diablo Valley College** in Pleasant Hill, and wondering where to find Shabbat, a holiday meal or just a Jewish face? Chabad of Lamorinda, at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, is your Jewish home away from home. Jewish students are always welcome for Shabbat dinner and for the holidays. No membership, no cost to belong and no background needed.

## How close are we?

From Saint Mary's College, Chabad of Lamorinda is about ten minutes away in Lafayette, by Moraga Road or St. Mary's Road. From Diablo Valley College and Pleasant Hill, it is about 15 minutes via Pleasant Hill Road. We are close to downtown Lafayette and the Lafayette BART station. Questions about getting here? Just call 925-384-7242.

## Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family

On Friday nights, Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski and their family welcome guests to a traditional Shabbat dinner: a festive table, Jewish songs, good food and real conversation. Students are welcome any week of the year. Bring a friend.

Just [let us know you are coming](/forms/shabbat-meal) so we can set a place for you, and mention any dietary needs. Prefer to be hosted by a local family? The same form lets you ask, and we will make the match.

You can also come earlier for **Kabbalat Shabbat** at 6:30 PM on Friday evening (the time may change with the season), or on **Saturday from 10 AM to 12 PM** for services followed by a hot Kiddush lunch. Candle lighting times are on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).

## The holidays, away from home

Missing your family's Seder or the sound of the shofar? Celebrate with us:

- [Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah) services, a community dinner, and shofar and Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir
- [Yom Kippur](/holidays/yom-kippur) at the Lafayette Park Hotel, with a break-fast after Neilah
- [Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot) in the sukkah and [Simchat Torah](/holidays/simchat-torah) dancing
- [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah) with a grand menorah lighting, latkes and donuts
- [Purim](/holidays/purim) and the community [Passover](/holidays/passover) Seder

High Holiday services need no tickets. [Reserving a seat](/forms/high-holidays) helps us prepare enough food. See everything coming up on the [events calendar](/events).

## Learning, questions and a Rabbi to talk to

College raises big questions about identity, faith, relationships and the future. Rabbi Labkowski is glad to meet with you, study one-on-one on any topic, or just talk, in complete confidence. Weekly classes run on Sunday evening (English Torah), Tuesday evening (in Hebrew, with a meal) and Wednesday afternoon (Tanya). See [adult education](/programs/adult-education), or [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi).

Going through a hard time? You do not have to carry it alone. See [grief and hard times](/help/grief-and-hard-times).

## Common questions

### Is there Shabbat dinner near Saint Mary's College in Moraga?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette hosts Shabbat dinners with the Rabbi's family, and Saint Mary's students are always welcome. [RSVP here](/forms/shabbat-meal).

### Where can Diablo Valley College students go for Jewish holidays?
Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, about 15 minutes from Pleasant Hill, welcomes DVC students for Shabbat and every holiday.

### Do I need to be observant or know Hebrew?
No. Students of every background come as they are. The prayer book is in Hebrew and English, and questions are always welcome.

### Is there a cost for Shabbat dinner?
You are our guest. Just RSVP so we can set a place for you.
