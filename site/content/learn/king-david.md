King David (David HaMelech, דוד המלך) was a shepherd from Bethlehem who became the second king of Israel, made Jerusalem its capital, and composed much of the Book of Psalms. Jews still say his Psalms every day, and Jewish tradition teaches that Moshiach will come from his family.

## The shepherd chosen by G-d

David was the youngest son of Jesse, a descendant of Ruth, from the tribe of Judah. While Saul was king, G-d told the prophet Samuel to anoint David as the next ruler. David was still a boy tending sheep. He was also a gifted musician, and he was brought to Saul's court to play for the king and calm his troubled spirit.

## David and Goliath

During a war with the Philistines, the giant Goliath challenged Israel to send out a champion, and for forty days no one answered. David, who had come only to bring food to his brothers, stepped forward with a staff, a sling and five stones, and brought the giant down. The people sang his praises, and Saul grew jealous and tried to kill him. David fled and lived as a fugitive. Twice he had the chance to harm Saul and refused, because Saul was G-d's anointed king. Saul's son Jonathan, meanwhile, remained David's loyal friend.

## King in Jerusalem

After Saul died, David was crowned in Hebron, reigned there seven years, and then reigned thirty-three years from [Jerusalem](/learn/jerusalem) over all of Israel. He strengthened the kingdom and gathered materials for the Holy Temple. The prophet Nathan told him he would not build it himself, because he had fought wars. His son Solomon built the [Holy Temple](/learn/the-holy-temple).

## A king who admitted his mistakes

David is remembered not as a perfect man but as a man who came back. When he did wrong in the matter of Bathsheba and Uriah, the prophet Nathan confronted him. David did not make excuses: he said "I have sinned," did [teshuvah](/learn/teshuvah), and accepted what followed. Psalm 51 is his prayer of return. The Sages also discuss this episode at length, so for the deeper teaching, ask the Rabbi. What every reader can take is that a leader can be great and still need to come back to G-d, and that returning is always possible.

## The Psalms

David collected and composed the 150 chapters of Psalms (Tehillim, תהילים), poems of praise, trust, struggle and hope that speak to every kind of moment. They were written by David with the Divine inspiration, and he also included songs by earlier figures. The book is divided into five parts, like the Five Books of Moses, and into seven sections for the days of the week and thirty for the days of the month. Many Jews say a portion daily and, in hard times, gather to recite the whole book. Read more in [Tehillim](/learn/tehillim).

## Moshiach and the House of David

The prophets promised that [Moshiach](/learn/moshiach) will be a descendant of David, who will rebuild the Holy Temple, gather the exiles and bring an era of peace. David lives on in the Psalms and in that hope.

## Common questions

### Who was King David in the Bible?
King David was the second king of Israel, a shepherd who defeated Goliath, made Jerusalem the capital and wrote many of the Psalms.

### Did King David write the Psalms?
David wrote most of the Psalms, with Divine inspiration, and collected others from earlier figures such as Moses and Abraham.

### Why can't King David build the Temple?
The prophet Nathan told David that because he had fought wars, his son Solomon would build it instead. David prepared much of what was needed.

### Why will Moshiach come from King David's family?
The prophets promised that the Moshiach, the anointed king who will bring peace and rebuild the Temple, will descend from David's line.

### How should I say Psalms for someone who is ill?
Many read chapters of Tehillim and mention the person's Hebrew name and mother's name. Ask the Rabbi to help you, and see our guide to [Tehillim](/learn/tehillim).

### What can we learn from David's mistake?
That a person can fall and return. David's repentance is the model Jews look to when they want to start fresh.
