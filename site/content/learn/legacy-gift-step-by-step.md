A legacy gift is a gift you arrange now that takes effect later, and you make it in a few steps: decide what matters to you, talk with the Rabbi, choose the way to give, meet your attorney or advisor, tell your family and let the organization know. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts legacy gifts to support Jewish education and continuity in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## Step 1. Think about what matters to you

Which values and causes do you want to continue after you? Chabad.org presents a planned gift as a contribution to the continuity of the Jewish people. For Chabad of Lamorinda it means education for future generations and keeping Judaism alive in our community.

## Step 2. Talk with the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin

Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241. They would be honored to hear what matters to you and to make sure your gift is used as you intend. There is no obligation. Or [talk privately with the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi).

## Step 3. Choose the way to give

Chabad.org describes three groups of gifts:

- **A will or trust:** a set amount, a specific asset or a percentage. See [Leaving Chabad in Your Will](/learn/leaving-chabad-in-your-will) and [Naming Chabad in Your Living Trust](/learn/naming-chabad-in-your-living-trust).
- **A retirement account:** a beneficiary form. See [Leaving Your IRA or 401(k) to a Jewish Cause](/learn/leaving-your-ira-to-a-jewish-cause).
- **A beneficiary designation** on life insurance, an annuity, a bank account or a donor-advised fund. See [Giving Through Life Insurance](/learn/life-insurance-gift-to-charity) and [Your Donor-Advised Fund After You](/learn/donor-advised-fund-successor-chabad).

Chabad of Lamorinda also accepts grants from donor-advised funds and gifts of stock now. See [Giving Stock to Chabad](/learn/giving-stock-to-chabad).

## Step 4. Meet your attorney and your advisor

Chabad.org advises working with an estate attorney experienced in estate planning and discussing your wishes with your financial advisor. Give them the details: Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, CA 94549, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, tax ID 47-4642750. This guide is not legal or tax advice; ask your attorney or tax advisor about wording and taxes.

If you would like your plans to follow Jewish law for your heirs, ask the Rabbi too. See [A Jewish Will](/learn/halachic-will).

## Step 5. Tell your family

Chabad.org advises discussing your wishes with your family. See [Talking to Your Children About Your Will and Your Legacy Gift](/learn/talking-to-your-children-about-your-will). You may also write an [ethical will](/learn/ethical-will), a letter about your values.

## Step 6. Let Chabad of Lamorinda know

Telling the Rabbi is your choice. If you do, he can thank you in your lifetime and make sure your gift is used as you wish.  He is glad to speak with you privately. The [legacy page](/legacy) has more.

## Common questions

### How do I make a legacy gift?
Decide what matters to you, talk with the Rabbi, choose a way to give (will, trust, beneficiary form), meet your attorney or advisor, and tell your family. Chabad of Lamorinda is at 925-384-7242.

### What is a legacy gift, or planned gift?
It is a gift arranged now that takes effect later, most often through a will, trust or beneficiary form.

### Do I need an attorney?
For a will or trust, Chabad.org advises an experienced estate attorney. A beneficiary form often needs only the form from the account company, as Chabad.org notes.

### Can I make a legacy gift to Chabad of Lamorinda?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts bequests, retirement account and life insurance gifts, donor-advised fund grants and gifts of stock.

### How much should I give?
That is personal. See [How Much Should I Leave to Tzedakah in My Will?](/learn/how-much-to-leave-to-tzedakah-in-a-will) and ask the Rabbi.

### Can I change it later?
Chabad.org notes that gifts in a will or trust can be changed. Ask your attorney.
