Planning a bar or bat mitzvah with Chabad of Lamorinda is simpler than most parents expect. You make one call, we find the date together, your child learns one-on-one with the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin at their own pace, and we walk with you all the way to the day. No membership, no Hebrew and no Jewish background are needed, and every Jewish family in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and the nearby towns is welcome.

## Why it can be simple

A Jewish boy becomes bar mitzvah at 13 and a girl becomes bat mitzvah at 12, on the Hebrew birthday, whether or not there is any ceremony. Nothing has to be earned or passed. The preparation is a gift to your child: a chance to understand what the day means and to take a first real step as a Jewish adult. That is why we can keep it calm, personal and free of pressure.

## Step 1: Call the Rabbi

Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242, or fill in the short [bar and bat mitzvah form](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah). Tell us about your child and your family. There is no wrong answer and nothing you need to know in advance. About a year ahead is ideal, but a shorter time works too.

## Step 2: Find the date

Enter your child's birth date in our [bar and bat mitzvah date calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah). It shows the Hebrew birthday and the Torah portion. The Rabbi confirms it with you, including what happens if your child was born after sunset.

## Step 3: Personal lessons at your child's pace

Every child learns with the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin, one family at a time, starting from wherever your child is today.

- **Boys** learn to read from the Torah and to put on [tefillin](/learn/tefillin), and get ready to be called up for an [aliyah](/learn/aliyah).
- **Girls** join the **Bat Mitzvah Club** and learn about the mitzvot of a Jewish woman, such as lighting [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles), and about inspiring Jewish women.
- **Both** can prepare a few words of Torah and, if they wish, a mitzvah project.

## Step 4: Plan the day

Together we plan what fits your family: an aliyah in the synagogue, a Kiddush with the community, a family meal or a party. A meaningful day can be small and simple. See [a meaningful celebration](/learn/meaningful-bar-bat-mitzvah-celebration) and [a bar or bat mitzvah on a budget](/learn/bar-bat-mitzvah-on-a-budget).

## Step 5: Enjoy it

You do not have to run anything or know what comes next. We guide your child through each part of the day, so you can simply be a proud parent.

## What we take care of

- Confirming the Hebrew date and the Torah portion.
- A learning plan made for your child.
- Help getting a kosher pair of tefillin for a boy.
- Guiding your child through the aliyah, the blessings or the talk.
- Answering every question along the way, big or small.

## Common questions

### How do I plan a bar mitzvah in Lafayette?
Call Chabad of Lamorinda at 925-384-7242 or fill in the [bar and bat mitzvah form](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah). The Rabbi confirms the date with you, sets up personal lessons and helps plan the day. Families from Moraga and Orinda are welcome too.

### Does my child need to know Hebrew?
No. Lessons start wherever your child is, and many children begin with no Hebrew at all.

### Do we need to be members of Chabad?
No. There is no membership. Every Jewish family is welcome, whatever your background, affiliation or level of observance.

### What if we cannot afford a big celebration?
You do not need one. What matters is your child's first steps as a Jewish adult, and a simple celebration is beautiful. If cost is a worry, call the Rabbi and talk it through privately.

### Is it too late if the date is only a few months away?
Usually not. Call the Rabbi, and together you will make a calm plan for the time you have.
