The big moments of life, the joyful ones and the hard ones, are when a family most needs a Rabbi who knows them. For Jewish families in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez, the answer to "who do we call?" is simple: call **Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242**. He and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski help with every life event, whatever your background or level of observance. There is no membership, and you do not need to have been here before.

## A new baby

Mazal tov! Call the Rabbi soon after the birth.

- **For a boy:** the bris is on the eighth day. The Rabbi helps work out the correct date, arrange a qualified mohel, and plan the honors and the blessings.
- **For a girl:** she is named at the Torah, usually at the first Torah reading after the birth. Many families celebrate by [sponsoring a Kiddush](/sponsor-a-kiddush).
- **A Hebrew name:** the Rabbi is glad to help you choose one.
- **Pidyon haben:** for a firstborn son, when it applies, on the 31st day.

Full details: [new baby](/help/new-baby).

## Bar and bat mitzvah

A boy becomes bar mitzvah at 13 and a girl becomes bat mitzvah at 12, by the Hebrew calendar. Find the date with our [bar and bat mitzvah calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah), then call about a year ahead. Boys learn to read from the Torah and put on tefillin; girls join the Bat Mitzvah Club with the Rebbetzin. Adults who never had one are welcome too. Start with the [bar and bat mitzvah form](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah), or see [planning a bar or bat mitzvah](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah) and our [program page](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah).

## A wedding

Mazal tov on your engagement! Meet with the Rabbi as early as you can, ideally several months before. He helps with choosing a date, the ketubah and the ceremony under the chuppah, and the Rebbetzin meets privately with the bride to prepare for the mikvah. See [getting married](/help/getting-married).

## When someone is sick

Call the Rabbi for a visit at home, in the hospital or in a care facility, anywhere in our area. We will add your loved one's name to our [Tehillim prayer list](/tehillim), say a Mi Shebeirach at the Torah reading, and can arrange to have the mezuzahs and tefillin in the home checked. See [someone is sick](/help/someone-is-sick), and if the illness is serious, [end of life](/help/end-of-life).

## When a loved one passes away

Please call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242 right away, ideally **before** calling a funeral home or signing anything. If a death is sudden, call 911 first. The Rabbi coordinates with the Chevra Kadisha for the tahara and a Jewish burial, helps plan the funeral and can officiate, and helps set up shiva and a minyan. Every Jew is entitled to a Jewish burial, and we do not want cost to stand in the way. See [when a loved one passes away](/help/someone-passed-away).

## Afterward: Kaddish and yahrzeit

Join our morning minyan to say Kaddish, or let us arrange for Kaddish to be said for you at [Kaddish](/kaddish). Find the yahrzeit with our [yahrzeit calculator](/tools/yahrzeit), add names to the [Yizkor booklet](/forms/yizkor), and if you would like to plan ahead for yourself or a parent, see [planning a Jewish funeral](/help/planning-a-jewish-funeral).

## Common questions

### Who do I call for a bris in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda?
Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 soon after the birth. He will help you set the date and arrange a qualified mohel.

### Can a Rabbi officiate a Jewish funeral if we are not members anywhere?
Yes. Rabbi Labkowski helps every Jewish family, member or not, observant or not. Call 925-384-7242.

### Where can my child prepare for a bar or bat mitzvah in Lamorinda?
With the Rabbi and Rebbetzin at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette. Lessons are personal and at your child's pace. [Get started here](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah).

### Can the Rabbi visit someone in the hospital?
Yes. The Rabbi visits at home, in hospitals, care facilities and hospice. Call 925-384-7242.

### Can we celebrate a simcha with the community?
Yes. [Sponsor a Kiddush](/sponsor-a-kiddush) after Shabbat services, or see more ways to mark an occasion on the [sponsor page](/sponsor).
