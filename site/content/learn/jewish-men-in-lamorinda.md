Between work, the commute through Orinda or Lafayette, kids' games in Moraga and everything else, it is easy for a Jewish man to go weeks without a moment for his soul. At Chabad of Lamorinda, at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, there are simple ways to change that: a few minutes of tefillin, a morning minyan, an evening class, or a l'chaim with friends at the Men's Club. No membership, no dues and no experience needed.

## The Men's Club

The [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club) is about fellowship, learning and a l'chaim, in good company. Past gatherings have included **Bagels, Lox & Tefillin** and **Scotch & Steaks in the Sukkah** during Sukkot, as well as community evenings like **Spaghetti, Meatballs & Torah**. To hear about the next one, [join our weekly email](/forms/newsletter) and tick "Men's Club," or call 925-384-7242.

## Tefillin with the Rabbi

Tefillin are two small leather boxes containing handwritten Torah passages, worn on the arm and head during weekday morning prayers. It takes just a few minutes, and it is one of the most powerful mitzvahs a Jewish man can do. Whether you have not put on tefillin since your bar mitzvah, or never have, Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski will be glad to help you, step by step.

Through our [mezuzah and tefillin form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin) you can:

- **Learn to put on tefillin**
- **Buy a kosher pair** of your own (also a beautiful bar mitzvah gift)
- **Have your tefillin checked** by a scribe

Read more in our [tefillin guide](/learn/tefillin).

## The morning minyan

A minyan, ten Jewish men praying together, is the heart of a community. Our Shacharit (morning prayers) is **Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM** and **Sunday at 8:00 AM**, in Lafayette. If you commute through Orinda or Lafayette BART, it is a short stop on your way. Every man who comes helps make the minyan, so that others can say Kaddish and hear the Torah read.

Saying Kaddish for a parent or loved one? Let us know and we will make sure there is a minyan. See [Kaddish](/kaddish) and our [minyan guide](/learn/minyan).

## Shabbat

**Kabbalat Shabbat** is on Friday evening at 6:30 PM (the time may change with the season). On **Saturday from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM** we have Shabbat services with the Torah reading, a short talk and children taking part, followed by a hot Kiddush lunch. The prayer book is in Hebrew and English, and the Rabbi announces the pages. Many men come with their kids. See the [Shabbat page](/shabbat) for this week's times.

## Weekly classes

- **Sunday, 9:00 to 10:00 PM:** weekly Torah class in English
- **Tuesday, 8:30 to 9:30 PM:** Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal
- **Wednesday, 1:00 to 2:00 PM:** Tanya class in English

Prefer to learn one-on-one? Ask the Rabbi to set up a study partner on any topic, from the weekly [Torah portion](/learn/weekly-parsha) to Talmud and Kabbalah. See [adult education](/programs/adult-education).

## Fathers and sons

Getting ready for a bar mitzvah? Boys learn to read from the Torah and to put on tefillin with the Rabbi. See [bar and bat mitzvah](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah). And it is never too late for an adult bar mitzvah: for a man, it can be as simple as putting on tefillin and being called to the Torah. See [planning a bar or bat mitzvah](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah).
