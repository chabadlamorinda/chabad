## Why get it through the Rabbi

Some Jewish items are only as good as what is inside them. A mezuzah or a pair of tefillin is a handwritten scroll, and you cannot see from the outside whether it is kosher. Many mezuzah cases sold online or in gift shops come with a printed "scroll" that is not a mezuzah at all.

When you get it through Chabad of Lamorinda:

- **The scroll is kosher,** handwritten by a qualified scribe and from a reliable source.
- **It is the right one for you:** the right size for your doorpost or case, and the right tefillin for a bar mitzvah boy or a grown man, following the custom you keep.
- **You are not on your own.** The Rabbi is glad to help you put your mezuzahs up with the blessing, or show you how to put on tefillin.

For candlesticks, cups and other items, buying through us simply saves you the search, and the Rabbi can explain what each one is for.

## Checking mezuzahs and tefillin (safrus)

Mezuzahs and tefillin are checked by a sofer (scribe), because ink fades and letters crack over the years, often with no sign on the outside. Jewish law asks for a check twice in seven years, and many check every year in Elul. We can have yours checked: bring them to us or ask the Rabbi to arrange it. See [How Often Should a Mezuzah Be Checked?](/learn/checking-mezuzahs-and-tefillin) and fill in the [mezuzah and tefillin form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin).
