A bar or bat mitzvah is a milestone your child will carry for life. For families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, Chabad of Lamorinda offers personal, meaningful preparation, with the Rabbi and Rebbetzin teaching each child at their own pace. There is no membership required, and every family is welcome, whatever your background.

## When is it?

A boy becomes bar mitzvah at **13** and a girl becomes bat mitzvah at **12**, on their Hebrew birthday. That date can be different from the English one. Find it with our [bar and bat mitzvah date calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah), or ask the Rabbi to confirm.

## How preparation works here

Our [Bar and Bat Mitzvah program](/programs/bar-bat-mitzvah) is for ages 11 to 13. Every child learns with **Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski** or **Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski**, one family at a time.

**For boys:** your son learns to read from the Torah, with a hands-on experience of what that means, and learns to put on **tefillin**, the mitzvah he begins at his bar mitzvah. The Rabbi can help you get a kosher pair. He also learns about being called to the Torah for an [aliyah](/learn/aliyah) and prepares a short talk on his Torah portion.

**For girls:** your daughter joins the **Bat Mitzvah Club**, learning about the mitzvot of a Jewish woman, such as lighting Shabbat candles, and about Jewish role models. Many girls add a mitzvah or charity project. The Rebbetzin often works closely with bat mitzvah girls.

## Starting in Hebrew School

Children in [Lamorinda Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school) build Hebrew reading and Jewish knowledge from a young age, and the school's Bar/Bat Mitzvah track (ages 12 to 13) leads right into personal preparation. See [Hebrew school in Lamorinda](/learn/hebrew-school-in-lamorinda).

## A step-by-step plan

1. **Call the Rabbi** about a year ahead, or earlier. We talk about your child and your family's hopes.
2. **Confirm the Hebrew date** with the [calculator](/tools/bar-bat-mitzvah).
3. **Begin lessons** tailored to your child.
4. **Plan the celebration:** a Torah reading in synagogue, a family gathering or a party. We help you find what fits your family.

Our [bar and bat mitzvah help page](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah) goes deeper into each step.

## Celebrate with the community

A bar mitzvah boy can be called to the Torah at Shabbat services on Saturday morning. Ask the Rabbi to plan it with you. You can [sponsor the Kiddush](/kiddush/) in honor of the bar or bat mitzvah and share the joy with the whole community. A pair of tefillin is also a beautiful bar mitzvah gift; ask through the [tefillin form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin).

## Never had one?

It is never too late. Many adults, men and women, celebrate a bar or bat mitzvah later in life. For a man, it can be as simple as putting on tefillin and being called to the Torah. The [get-started form](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah) has an option for adults.

## Common questions

### Where can my child prepare for a bar mitzvah in Lafayette?
At Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette. Lessons are personal, with the Rabbi or Rebbetzin. Families from Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez are welcome.

### Do we need to be members or observant?
No. There is no membership, and we meet every family where they are.

### How long does preparation take?
It depends on your child. Many families start about a year ahead, but we can work with shorter timelines. Call 925-384-7242 to talk it through.

### What is the Bat Mitzvah Club?
A group for bat mitzvah girls to learn and celebrate together with the Rebbetzin. Sign up through the [bar and bat mitzvah form](/forms/bar-bat-mitzvah).

### Can an adult have a bar or bat mitzvah?
Yes. Many adults find it deeply moving. Call the Rabbi.
