To keep Shabbat at home you need candles and candlesticks, a Kiddush cup, wine or grape juice, two challahs on a board with a cover, salt, a washing cup, and, for the end of Shabbat, a Havdalah set. You do not have to buy everything at once: candles, grape juice and bread are enough to begin.

For families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda setting up a Jewish home, here is a simple shopping list in the order you will use the items.

## For Friday night

- **Candles and candlesticks.** Two candles are lit about 18 minutes before sunset, so you need holders. Any holders will do. See [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles).
- **A Kiddush cup.** A cup or goblet for the wine or grape juice. Many families keep a nicer one for Shabbat. See [Kiddush](/learn/kiddush).
- **Kosher wine or grape juice.** See [kosher wine](/learn/kosher-wine).
- **A washing cup.** A large cup for pouring water over the hands before eating bread. See [netilat yadayim](/learn/netilat-yadayim).
- **Challah, a board and a cover.** Two whole loaves on a board under a cloth. See [challah](/learn/challah).
- **A salt dish and a festive tablecloth.** A white cloth is ideal. For what goes where, see [setting the Shabbat table](/learn/setting-the-shabbat-table).
- **Printed blessings and a Grace after Meals booklet,** in translation or transliteration, helpful for beginners and guests.

## For keeping food warm

Cooking is not done on Shabbat, so food is ready before candle lighting and kept warm. You will need:

- **A blech**, a metal sheet that covers the stovetop, or a hot plate with a single setting.
- **An electric urn or a non-whistling kettle** for hot drinks, filled before Shabbat.

Details and rules are in [how to keep food warm on Shabbat](/learn/keeping-food-warm-on-shabbat). A slow cooker works too; see [slow cooker Shabbat recipes](/learn/slow-cooker-shabbat-recipes). Ask the Rabbi about your own kitchen.

## For the rest of the house

- **Timers.** Lights, heating and air conditioning can be set before Shabbat on timers, a simple way to avoid using electricity. See [electricity on Shabbat](/learn/electricity-on-shabbat).
- **Light switch covers.** Guards so that no one flips a switch by accident.
- **A plan for the fridge light,** which you can turn off before Shabbat. Ask the Rabbi what works for your model.
- **A Shabbat basket of toys and books** for children, without batteries. See [Shabbat with kids](/learn/shabbat-with-kids).

## For the end of Shabbat

Havdalah (הַבְדָּלָה) marks the end of Shabbat. It needs three items:

1. **A cup of wine or grape juice,** filled to overflowing.
2. **A spice box** with fragrant spices.
3. **A braided candle** with several wicks.

See [Havdalah](/learn/havdalah). A Havdalah set can wait until you have kept Shabbat for a few weeks.

## Common questions

### What do I need for Shabbat?
Candles and holders, wine or grape juice, a cup, two whole challahs and salt. For the end of Shabbat, add a spice box, a braided candle and a cup.

### Do I need a special Kiddush cup?
No. Any clean cup holds the wine or juice. Many people buy a nicer one later.

### What do I need to buy for Havdalah?
A candle with several wicks, fragrant spices and a cup of wine or grape juice. See [Havdalah](/learn/havdalah).

### Do I need a blech?
Not to begin. A blech, or a hot plate with a single setting, keeps cooked food warm. Ask the Rabbi what works in your kitchen.
