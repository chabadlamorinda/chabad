Chanukah in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda is celebrated at home by lighting the menorah each night for eight nights, and together at Chabad of Lamorinda's **Grand Chanukah Menorah Lighting** on **Sunday, December 6 at 4:00 PM** at Lafayette Plaza Park, with a Gelt Drop, donuts, hot cocoa and a giant dreidel game. It is free. [See the details and sign up](/events/chanukah-grand-menorah-lighting-2026).

## What is a public menorah, and where did the idea begin?

A public menorah is a large Chanukah menorah lit outdoors so the whole community can see the light. Chabad.org tells how the first giant one was lit in 1975 in San Francisco's Union Square, by Rabbi Chaim Drizin, with rock promoter Bill Graham helping to build it; more than a thousand people came. Today Chabad centers light public menorahs around the world, because the mitzvah of Chanukah is to "publicize the miracle" (*pirsumei nisa*, פרסומי ניסא): to share the light with everyone who passes by.

Our own area has a story too. In 2017, more than 200 neighbors gathered at Lafayette Plaza Park for the first night of Chanukah, with music, latkes and doughnuts, as the local paper Patch reported. This year's celebration is back at the same park: [Grand Chanukah Menorah Lighting, December 6](/events/chanukah-grand-menorah-lighting-2026).

## Ways to celebrate Chanukah in Lamorinda

1. **Come to the grand menorah lighting.** It is open to every Jew and every neighbor, with no membership and no tickets needed to be part of the light.
2. **Make a menorah with your kids.** Chabad of Lamorinda's Chanukah programs include a make-your-own-menorah activity for children. See [Chanukah for kids](/learn/chanukah-for-kids).
3. **Light at home each night.** Learn the blessings and the order in [how to light the Chanukah menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah), and see tonight's candle lighting time for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda on the [Chanukah page](/holidays/chanukah).
4. **Invite friends over.** [Hosting a Chanukah party](/learn/hosting-a-chanukah-party) has simple ideas, and [Chanukah foods](/learn/chanukah-foods) explains why we eat latkes and donuts.
5. **Sponsor a night.** Be a Chanukah lamplighter by sponsoring a night of the community menorah. Call 925-384-7242.

## Need a menorah and candles?

Many homes in Contra Costa County do not own a menorah yet. Ask Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette for a menorah and candles for your home, or order Judaica from [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher). Light after nightfall and place the menorah in a window, so the light reaches the street.

## Common questions

### Where can I see a public menorah lighting in Lafayette?
Chabad of Lamorinda's Grand Chanukah Menorah Lighting is Sunday, December 6 at 4:00 PM at Lafayette Plaza Park, at the corner of Mount Diablo Blvd and Moraga Rd. It is free. [Sign up here](/events/chanukah-grand-menorah-lighting-2026).

### Are there Chanukah events for kids near Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda?
Yes. The Dec. 6 menorah lighting has a Gelt Drop, Decorate Your Own Donut, a Giant Dreidel Game and LED light wands for kids, with free hot cocoa, donuts and gelt. [Sign up](/events/chanukah-grand-menorah-lighting-2026).

### What do I need to light a menorah at home?
You need a menorah (or eight safe oil lights), candles and matches. Light after nightfall, one candle the first night and one more each night. The steps are in [how to light the Chanukah menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah).

### Do I have to be a member to come to Chabad's Chanukah celebration?
No. Every Jew is welcome, and neighbors of every background are warmly invited. There is no membership or dues.

### Can I sponsor a night of Chanukah in Lamorinda?
Yes. You can be a Chanukah lamplighter and sponsor a night of the community menorah. See the [Chanukah page](/holidays/chanukah) or call 925-384-7242.

### When is Chanukah this year?
Chanukah starts at sundown on the first night and lasts eight nights. All dates and candle lighting times are computed for you on the [Jewish holidays page](/jewish-holidays).
