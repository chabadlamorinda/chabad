Yes, your first apartment can be a Jewish home from day one, and the first step is a kosher mezuzah (מְזוּזָה) on the doorpost. According to Chabad.org, a renter outside Israel is not obligated to put up a mezuzah for the first 30 days, but it is the custom of Chabad and many others to hang the mezuzahs right away, without the blessing, and to say the blessing after 30 days.

## The mezuzah in a rental

Here is what Chabad.org teaches about when to put up a mezuzah:

- **If you buy a home:** put up the mezuzah, with its blessing, when you move in.
- **If you rent outside Israel** (including the East Bay and the rest of the Bay Area): the obligation starts after 30 days. Chabad custom is to hang mezuzahs right away without the blessing. After 30 days, one mezuzah is taken down to be checked or replaced, and the blessing is recited when it is hung again.
- **If you rent in Israel:** mezuzahs are put up right away, with the blessing.

Because every situation is a little different, such as a short lease or a roommate, ask the Rabbi. For how to put one up, which doors need one and what to say, see [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah), [mezuzah questions](/learn/mezuzah-questions), and the [mezuzah and tefillin](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin) form. Read also [checking mezuzahs and tefillin](/learn/checking-mezuzahs-and-tefillin).

## A tzedakah box

A tzedakah box, or [pushka](/learn/pushka), on a shelf or by the door turns giving into a habit. Drop in a coin or two when you come home, and let it fill. Read about [tzedakah](/learn/tzedakah).

## A kosher start in the kitchen

If you want to keep kosher, the kitchen is the place to begin. Read [kosher kitchen](/learn/kosher-kitchen) and ask the Rabbi any questions. For kosher food around Lamorinda, see [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher), a project of Chabad of Lamorinda.

## Make it yours

A few small touches make an apartment feel Jewish: a Jewish book on the shelf (see [Jewish home library](/learn/jewish-home-library)), a Kiddush cup, a menorah for Chanukah, and a place for the Shabbat table. For more ideas, read [setting up a Jewish home](/learn/setting-up-a-jewish-home).

## Common questions

### Do I need a mezuzah in a rented apartment?
Yes, after 30 days if you rent outside Israel, but Chabad custom is to hang them right away without the blessing. Say the blessing after 30 days.

### Do I need a mezuzah on every door?
Doorways used by people, such as the front door and bedrooms, need one. Bathrooms do not. Ask the Rabbi about unusual doors.

### Can I buy a mezuzah scroll online?
The scroll must be kosher and handwritten. Ask the Rabbi to help you find one, using the [mezuzah and tefillin](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin) form.

### What do I do when I move out?
Ask the Rabbi what to do with the mezuzahs when you move.

### How can I light Shabbat candles in a building where open flames are not allowed?
Please ask the Rabbi about your situation.
