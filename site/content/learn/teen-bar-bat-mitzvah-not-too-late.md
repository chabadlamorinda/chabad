If your teenager never had a bar or bat mitzvah, it is not too late. A Jewish boy became bar mitzvah at 13, and a girl bat mitzvah at 12, on the Hebrew birthday, with or without a ceremony. Marking it now, at 14, 15 or 17, is just as real and often more meaningful, because a teen understands the step. At Chabad of Lamorinda it is simple, private if you wish, and open to every Jewish family in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and nearby.

## Why teens miss it

Families move. A parent was ill. The family was not connected to a synagogue, or the date fell in a hard year. Sometimes a child did not want it at 13 and feels differently now. None of this matters today. What matters is that your teen is ready to take the step.

## What it looks like for a boy

- **Tefillin.** He learns to put on [tefillin](/learn/tefillin), the central mitzvah of a bar mitzvah. Since he is already past 13, he can begin right away, with the blessing.
- **An aliyah.** He is called up to the Torah to say the blessings. If he wishes, he can also learn to read a section.
- **A few words.** He shares a short thought on a Torah portion.

Many teens find that putting on tefillin with the Rabbi for the first time is a moment they never forget.

## What it looks like for a girl

- **Learning** with the Rebbetzin about the mitzvot of a Jewish woman and about Jewish heroines.
- **Shabbat candles.** Taking on lighting her own [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles) each Friday.
- **A gathering.** A meal or an evening where she shares what she learned and what it means to her.

## As quiet or as big as your teen wants

Some teens want a celebration with friends; others prefer a quiet aliyah on a weekday morning with just the family. Both are beautiful. Teens can feel self-conscious about being "older," so we keep it comfortable: lessons are one-on-one, at their pace, and no one needs to know more than they choose to share.

## Common questions

### Can you have a bar mitzvah at 14 or 15?
Yes. A boy became bar mitzvah on his 13th Hebrew birthday, and he can mark it with tefillin and an aliyah at any age after that.

### Can my 16-year-old daughter still have a bat mitzvah?
Yes. Every Jewish girl became bat mitzvah at 12, and a celebration of learning and mitzvot is meaningful at any age. Call the Rebbetzin at 925-384-7241.

### Does my teen need to know Hebrew?
No. Lessons start wherever your teen is, and every word is explained.

### How long does it take to prepare?
It depends on your teen and the celebration you choose. A boy can put on tefillin and have an aliyah after a short preparation. Call 925-384-7242 to make a plan.

### Do we need to be members?
No. Chabad of Lamorinda has no membership, and every Jewish family from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and nearby is welcome.
