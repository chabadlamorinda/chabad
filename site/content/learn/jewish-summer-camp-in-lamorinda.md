Lamorinda Jewish Summer Camp in Lafayette is a toddler summer camp for ages 2 to 4, running seven weeks in summer, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, with Jewish songs, Shabbat parties, water play and art. It is $3,200 for the full summer or $500 per week, spaces are limited, and early registration is recommended on the [summer camp form](/forms/summer-camp).

## What a day at Jewish summer camp looks like

Camp is run by Chabad of Lamorinda for families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda. The program includes:

- Jewish songs and movement
- Shabbat celebrations, so children feel the joy of the week's holiest day
- Water play on warm East Bay days
- Arts and crafts and sensory play
- Hebrew and traditions
- Mitzvahs and kindness

Each day is long enough to work for busy families: 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Call 925-384-7242 to ask about what an ordinary day includes, or to visit.

## Why a Jewish summer matters

Chabad.org gives many reasons to send children to Jewish summer camp. The idea is simple: when Judaism is joyful, children remember it. Songs, friends and Shabbat parties give a child a happy Jewish memory that school-year routines may not. The Rebbe encouraged Jewish summer camps for every Jewish child, and Chabad.org describes Jewish day camps in small towns as a "lifeline" for families who might otherwise have no Jewish setting for their kids. Camp also helps parents meet other Jewish families nearby.
