A minyan is a group of ten Jewish men age 13 or older who gather for prayer. Certain holy parts of the service, like Kaddish, Kedushah and the Torah reading, can only be said when a minyan is present.

## What a minyan is

Minyan means "count" or "number." Ten is the minimum number for a public Jewish congregation. When ten Jews gather to pray, the Sages teach, the Divine Presence rests among them in a special way.

Every Jewish man from bar mitzvah age counts, whatever his level of observance or knowledge. A Jew who has never been to synagogue counts exactly the same as the rabbi.

## Why ten?

The Torah says, "I shall be sanctified among the Children of Israel" (Leviticus 22:32). The Talmud links this verse to the story of the spies, where a group of ten is called an "assembly" (Numbers 14:27). From this we learn that a holy assembly, a congregation, begins with ten.

There is also a deeper idea. Each individual has a unique role, but when ten come together, they become a community, greater than the sum of its parts.

## What requires a minyan

These prayers can only be said with a minyan:

- **Kaddish,** including the Mourner's [Kaddish](/learn/kaddish)
- **Barchu,** the call to prayer
- **Kedushah,** said during the repetition of the [Amidah](/learn/amidah)
- **The chazan's repetition** of the Amidah
- **The public [Torah reading](/learn/torah-reading)** and Haftarah with their blessings
- **The Priestly Blessing**

A minyan is also needed for the seven blessings at a wedding and for [sheva brachot](/learn/sheva-brachot). When ten are present at a meal, a phrase is added to the invitation to Birkat Hamazon.

## Praying without a minyan

You can and should pray even when there is no minyan. Most of the prayers are said alone just the same, skipping the parts that need ten. Still, the Sages teach that prayer with a congregation is especially precious and more readily accepted.

## How you can help

Making a minyan is a real act of kindness. A mourner who needs to say Kaddish, a family sitting [shiva](/learn/shiva), or a community on a quiet weekday morning may be waiting for the tenth man. Showing up for 30 minutes can mean the world to someone.

## Common questions

### Do women count in a minyan?
In traditional Jewish law, the minyan for public prayer is made up of men, who have the obligation of communal prayer. Women's prayer is equally precious and is not dependent on a minyan. Women have their own unique role and many mitzvot, as described in our guide to [Jewish women](/learn/jewish-women).

### Can a minyan be on Zoom?
No. The ten must be together in one place. Joining a service online can be inspiring, but you cannot be counted or answer as part of the minyan that way.

### What if there are only nine?
The prayers that need a minyan are skipped. Many synagogues will call around to find a tenth person. It is a great mitzvah to answer that call.

### Does a boy under 13 count?
No. A boy counts from his bar mitzvah, at 13. Read more about [bar mitzvah](/learn/bar-mitzvah).
