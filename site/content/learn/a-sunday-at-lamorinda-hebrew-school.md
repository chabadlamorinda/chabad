On Sunday mornings from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, children from Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda come to Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette for Lamorinda Hebrew School. In two lively hours they read Hebrew, hear the stories of the Torah, get ready for the next holiday, play, sing and make things with their hands. It is fun, exciting and hands-on, and children leave proud of who they are.

## The basics

- **When:** Sundays, 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Where:** Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette
- **Who:** children ages 3 to 13, from every kind of Jewish family
- **Director:** Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski, 925-384-7241
- **Tuition:** $600 per child per year, including books and supplies; no membership needed, and no child is turned away for lack of funds

## Three tracks, by age

**Early Childhood (ages 3 to 5).** The youngest children discover Judaism through stories, songs, crafts and holiday celebrations. They meet the Hebrew letters, sing the blessings, and learn about Shabbat and the holidays with their hands and hearts.

**Hebrew School (ages 5 to 12).** The heart of the school. Children learn:
- **Hebrew reading with Aleph Champ**, level by level, each at their own pace, with a celebration at every new level. See [Aleph Champ](/learn/aleph-champ-hebrew-reading).
- **Prayer and the siddur**: the blessings, the Shema and the main prayers, and how to find their way in a prayer book.
- **The weekly Torah portion**: the story of the week, its heroes and its lesson.
- **Israel and Jewish history**: the land, the people and the story of how we got here.
- **Shabbat and the holidays**, from Rosh Hashanah to Shavuot, so children know what is coming and why we celebrate.
- **Jewish values and mitzvahs**: kindness, honesty, respect, tzedakah and more. See [Jewish values kids learn](/learn/jewish-values-kids-learn-in-hebrew-school).

**Bar/Bat Mitzvah (ages 12 to 13).** Personal preparation for the big day, and for girls, the Bat Mitzvah Club. See [from Hebrew school to Bar and Bat Mitzvah](/learn/hebrew-school-to-bar-bat-mitzvah).

## How children learn: multisensory and hands-on

Children learn best when they use all their senses. Lessons at Lamorinda Hebrew School are multisensory: children see, hear, say, touch and do. Games, creative projects, songs and stories turn ideas into memories. Before a holiday, children learn about it by doing, so that when the holiday comes, they are the ones explaining it at home. Read more in [hands-on Jewish learning for kids](/learn/hands-on-jewish-learning-for-kids).

## Why Sunday morning works

During the week, Lamorinda children are busy with school, homework, sports and activities. Sunday morning gives them two steady hours of Jewish learning and friendship without crowding the week. See [Hebrew school and busy schedules](/learn/hebrew-school-and-busy-schedules).

## Friends who share their heritage

Many Jewish children in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are the only Jewish child in their class. On Sunday they are with friends who celebrate the same holidays and know the same songs. That sense of belonging is one of the best things Hebrew school gives.

## Bring Sunday home

Ask your child what they learned, and let them teach you. Light Shabbat candles together on Friday, say a blessing before eating, and talk about the [parsha at the Shabbat table](/learn/parsha-for-kids). A little at home makes Sunday's learning stick.
