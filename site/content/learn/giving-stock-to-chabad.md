To donate stock to a synagogue, you contact the organization first, then ask your broker or financial advisor to transfer the shares to it. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts gifts of stock and has a Fidelity brokerage account for them: call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241 to arrange one.

## Steps for giving stock

1. **Call the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin first.** Tell them you would like to give stock. Chabad of Lamorinda has a Fidelity brokerage account for gifts of stock, and we will give you or your advisor the transfer details.
2. **Decide your intention.** Is this gift maaser, a holiday gift, a gift in someone's memory? Say so when you call. See [Maaser](/learn/maaser).
3. **Ask your broker or advisor to make the transfer.** Along with the transfer details, the broker will ask for the organization's details: Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, CA 94549, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, tax ID 47-4642750. The same details appear on the [legacy page](/legacy).
4. **Keep your records.** Ask your tax advisor what you need for your tax return.
5. **Let us know when it is done,** so we can thank you and confirm that it arrived.

This guide is not tax or legal advice. How a gift of stock is taxed depends on your situation, and the questions belong with your tax advisor.

## Does a gift of stock count as maaser?

Maaser (מַעֲשֵׂר) means "a tenth," the custom of giving a tenth of income to tzedakah. Chabad.org's guide to maaser says that income includes salary, gifts, inheritance and interest, and that gains on investments are generally due only once the profit is actually realized, such as when an asset is sold. The guide does not discuss gifts of stock directly. So how a particular gift of stock counts toward maaser is a question for the Rabbi, who is glad to help. Call 925-384-7242. See also [Donor-Advised Funds, Stock and Maaser](/learn/donor-advised-funds-and-tzedakah).

## Why give this way

Whether giving stock is right for you is for your tax advisor to say. What we can say is what the gift does: it helps Chabad of Lamorinda provide Jewish education and Jewish life for families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## Common questions

### How do I donate stock to a synagogue?
Contact the synagogue, then ask your broker or advisor to transfer the shares to it. For Chabad of Lamorinda, call 925-384-7242 or 925-384-7241 first.

### Does Chabad of Lamorinda accept gifts of stock?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts gifts of stock through its Fidelity brokerage account. For the transfer details, call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241.

### Does a gift of stock count as maaser?
Chabad.org's maaser guide does not address stock directly. Ask the Rabbi how it counts, and ask your tax advisor about taxes.

### What information does my broker need?
The transfer details for Chabad of Lamorinda's Fidelity account, which we will give you when you call, and the name Chabad of Lamorinda with the address and tax ID on the [legacy page](/legacy). The Rabbi can help with anything else.

### Can I give stock in someone's memory?
Yes. Tell the Rabbi when you call. See [Giving in Someone's Honor or Memory](/learn/giving-in-someones-honor).

### Is there a tax benefit?
That depends on your situation. Please ask your tax advisor.
