After a move, a divorce, the loss of a spouse, retirement, or simply a busy life, many people feel lonely. You are not alone in feeling alone, and Judaism has always been a religion of community.

## "It is not good for man to be alone"

These are among the first words G-d says about people (Genesis 2:18). Many mitzvahs are built for togetherness: praying with a minyan, a Shabbat table with guests, visiting the sick, comforting mourners, celebrating together. A Jewish community is meant to be a family.

## Small steps that help

- **Say yes to one invitation** this month, even if it feels hard.
- **Come to Shabbat services and Kiddush.** Nobody needs to know the prayers; people come to talk and eat together.
- **Join a weekly class.** Seeing the same faces each week is how friendships form. See [Torah classes](/learn/torah-classes-in-lamorinda).
- **Volunteer.** Helping others is one of the fastest ways to feel connected. See [volunteering](/learn/volunteering-in-lamorinda).
- **Host a small Shabbat meal,** even simple, and invite one or two people. See [being a Shabbat guest](/learn/shabbat-guest-guide).
- **Call someone** who might also be lonely.

## For older adults

Loneliness can affect health as much as illness. If getting out is hard, ask about a visit, a phone call or help joining a class. See [Jewish seniors in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-seniors-in-lamorinda).

## When loneliness is heavy

If loneliness comes with sadness that won't lift, talk to a doctor or counselor too. In a crisis, call or text 988. See [mental health](/learn/mental-health-jewish-view).

## Common questions

### I don't know anyone at Chabad. Will I feel out of place?
No. Most people came the first time not knowing anyone. The Rabbi and Rebbetzin will introduce you.

### I'm not religious. Is that a problem?
Not at all. Every Jew is welcome as they are. See [not religious? You're welcome](/learn/not-religious-welcome).

### Can someone visit me?
Yes. Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242.

### Can I be invited for a Shabbat meal?
Yes. Ask the Rabbi or use the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal).
