You can give through life insurance by naming a charity, such as a synagogue, as a beneficiary of your policy with a change-of-beneficiary form from your insurance company. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts gifts through life insurance, and it is one of the simplest ways to leave a lasting gift for Jewish life in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## A simple kind of legacy gift

Chabad.org lists life insurance together with annuities, bank accounts and donor-advised funds as gifts made through a beneficiary designation. It describes it as simple: usually a one-page form, with no attorney needed and no change to your will. Your insurer, bank or fund manager can send you a "Change of Beneficiary" form.

This guide is not legal, tax or financial advice, and it does not recommend any insurance product. Please ask your attorney or advisor.

## Ways people name a charity

- **The whole policy:** the charity is the beneficiary.
- **Part of the policy:** you may be able to name more than one beneficiary, for example family and a charity. Ask your insurer how it works.

## Common questions

### How do I name a charity as the beneficiary of my life insurance?
Ask your insurer for a change-of-beneficiary form, write in the charity's name, address and tax ID, and return it. Chabad.org describes this as usually a one-page form.

### Can I name Chabad of Lamorinda as a life insurance beneficiary?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts legacy gifts through life insurance. Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241.

### Do I need to change my will?
Chabad.org says a beneficiary designation usually needs no will change and no attorney. Ask your attorney if you are unsure how it fits with your other plans.

### Can I name both my family and a charity?
Often yes, by dividing the benefit between beneficiaries. Ask your insurer.

### Can I change my mind later?
Usually you can submit a new form. Ask your insurer.

### Is there a tax benefit?
That depends on your situation. Please ask your tax advisor or attorney.
