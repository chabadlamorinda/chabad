How we start the morning shapes the whole day. Judaism offers a beautiful morning routine that takes as little or as much time as you have. Even five minutes can turn a rushed morning into a calm, grateful one.

## 1. Modeh Ani: thank you, before anything else

Before getting out of bed, say: "Modeh ani lefanecha, Melech chai v'kayam, shehechezarta bi nishmati b'chemlah, rabbah emunatecha." I thank You, living and eternal King, for returning my soul to me with compassion; great is Your faithfulness. See [Modeh Ani](/learn/modeh-ani).

## 2. Wash your hands

Pour water three times on each hand, alternating right and left. It marks the start of a new day, ready for holiness. See [washing hands](/learn/netilat-yadayim).

## 3. The morning blessings

After using the restroom, say [Asher Yatzar](/learn/asher-yatzar), thanking G-d for a healthy body. Then the morning blessings in the [siddur](/learn/siddur), thanking G-d for sight, for clothing, for strength.

## 4. Pray

Even a few minutes count: the [Shema](/learn/shema), a few lines of the morning prayer, or speaking to G-d in your own words. Men can put on [tefillin](/learn/tefillin) on weekdays. See [daily prayers](/learn/daily-prayers).

## 5. Give tzedakah

Put a coin in a [pushka](/learn/pushka), a charity box, every weekday morning. Children love doing this; it teaches them that every day starts with giving.

## 6. Learn something

A verse of the [weekly parsha](/learn/weekly-parsha), a page of [Tanya](/learn/tanya), a short guide: a few minutes of Torah set the tone for the day. See [daily Torah study](/learn/daily-torah-study).

## Make it yours

Start with one step, Modeh Ani, and add another when it feels natural. Keep a pushka and a siddur by the coffee maker. Before checking your phone, give the first minutes of your day to what matters most.

## Common questions

### How long does a Jewish morning routine take?
As little as two minutes (Modeh Ani, washing hands, a coin for tzedakah) or as long as a full morning prayer, about 30 to 45 minutes. Start small.

### Can I say Modeh Ani in English?
Yes. G-d understands every language. Many people say it in Hebrew and think of the meaning in their own words.

### What if I already started my day without it?
You can still give tzedakah, pray and learn later in the morning. Tomorrow is a fresh start.
