Whether you have lived in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda for fifty years or just moved here to be near your grandchildren, Chabad of Lamorinda is your Jewish home. We are at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, close to downtown and the Lafayette BART station, and we serve nearby Pleasant Hill and Martinez too. There is no membership and no dues. You are simply welcome.

This guide is also for adult children who want to help a parent stay connected to Jewish life.

## Learning, at your pace

Torah study keeps the mind sharp and the soul young. Our weekly [classes for adults](/programs/adult-education) welcome every level:

- **Tanya class (English):** Wednesday, 1:00 to 2:00 PM. A daytime class on Chassidic philosophy, easy to fit into your week.
- **Weekly Torah class (English):** Sunday, 9:00 to 10:00 PM.
- **Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal:** Tuesday, 8:30 to 9:30 PM.

Prefer to learn one on one? Ask the Rabbi to set up a study partner. Questions are always welcome. See [Torah classes in Lamorinda](/learn/torah-classes-in-lamorinda).

## Shabbat and the holidays

Come for **Shabbat morning services**, Saturday 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, with a bilingual prayer book and a **hot Kiddush lunch** afterward, where friends from all three towns sit together. On Friday nights the Rabbi's family hosts Shabbat dinners any week of the year; let us know through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) or at 925-384-7242. Times are on the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).

The holidays are richer with every generation at the table. Bring your grandchildren to the menorah lighting on [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah), the Megillah on [Purim](/holidays/purim) or the community Seder on [Passover](/holidays/passover). On [Yom Kippur](/holidays/yom-kippur), services are held at the Lafayette Park Hotel, with no tickets and no membership; [reserve a seat](/forms/high-holidays) so we can save you a place.

## Remembering loved ones

- **Kaddish:** join the weekday minyan, Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM and Sunday at 8:00 AM. Tell us you are saying Kaddish and we will make sure there is a minyan, or we can arrange for Kaddish to be said on your behalf at [Kaddish](/kaddish).
- **Yahrzeits:** find the date with the [yahrzeit calculator](/tools/yahrzeit) and save it in [your account](/account) for a reminder every year.
- **Yizkor:** add your loved ones' names to the [Yizkor memorial booklet](/forms/yizkor).
- **A Kiddush in their memory:** [sponsor the Shabbat Kiddush](/kiddush/) on a yahrzeit and share the memory with the community.

## When someone is ill

Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski visits people at home, in hospitals, care facilities and hospice, across Lamorinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez. He will pray, offer a blessing, or simply sit and talk. We will add your loved one's name to our [Tehillim prayer list](/tehillim) and say a Mi Shebeirach at the Torah reading. See [when someone is sick](/help/someone-is-sick) and, when an illness is serious, [end of life](/help/end-of-life).

Many people find peace of mind in [planning a Jewish funeral in advance](/help/planning-a-jewish-funeral). The Rabbi is glad to sit with you privately, with no pressure.

## Loneliness and hard times

Living alone, or far from family, can be hard. Judaism teaches that it is not good to be alone, and we mean it when we say we would love to have you at our table. Call 925-384-7242, or read [grief, loneliness and hard times](/help/grief-and-hard-times).

## For grandparents

Your stories are a treasure. Share them, bless your grandchildren on Friday night, and bring them along to a holiday. See [Jewish grandparents](/learn/jewish-grandparents). On your birthday, the Rebbe encouraged saying your own chapter of [Tehillim](/tehillim) every day of the new year.

## Common questions

### Is there a daytime Jewish class for seniors in Lafayette?
Yes. The Tanya class meets Wednesday from 1:00 to 2:00 PM at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette. Every level is welcome.

### Can a Rabbi visit my parent in a care facility near Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda?
Yes. Rabbi Labkowski visits at home, in hospitals, care facilities and hospice. Call 925-384-7242 to arrange a visit.

### Where can I say Kaddish near Moraga or Orinda?
At the morning minyan in Lafayette: Monday to Friday at 6:30 AM and Sunday at 8:00 AM. Let us know and we will make sure there is a minyan.

### How can I remember a parent's yahrzeit every year?
Use the [yahrzeit calculator](/tools/yahrzeit), save it in your account for a yearly reminder, and consider sponsoring a Kiddush in their memory.

### Do seniors need to be members?
No. There is no membership and no dues. Every Jew is welcome at every service, class and event.
