Daily Rambam study is a worldwide learning cycle in which Jews study the Rambam's Mishneh Torah, a complete code of Jewish law, on a fixed daily schedule. It was launched by the Lubavitcher Rebbe in 1984 so that Jews everywhere could learn the entire Torah's laws together.

## Who was the Rambam?

Rambam is an acronym of Rabbi Moshe ben Maimon, also known as [Maimonides](/learn/maimonides). Born in Cordoba, Spain, in the 12th century, he became a great Torah scholar, philosopher and physician, and lived his later years in Egypt. A famous saying about him is, "From Moses to Moses, there was none like Moses."

## What is the Mishneh Torah?

The Mishneh Torah is the Rambam's masterwork. It is the only Jewish legal code that covers every area of Torah law, including laws that apply only in the Temple era or in the Land of Israel.

- It has **14 books**, so it is also called the Yad Hachazakah, since the numerical value of yad is 14.
- It is written in clear, beautiful Hebrew.
- It covers belief, prayer, holidays, kosher, marriage, business, ethics, the Temple and the laws of kings and Moshiach.

The Rambam wrote that with this book a person could know the whole Oral Torah. It begins with the foundations of belief in G-d and ends with the era of [Moshiach](/learn/moshiach).

## Why the Rebbe launched daily study

In 1984, [the Rebbe](/learn/the-rebbe) called for Jews worldwide to study the Mishneh Torah every day. He explained that it brings unity, as Jews everywhere learn the same text on the same day. Studying all of Torah law also means knowing all 613 mitzvot, including those of the Holy Temple, preparing us for the Redemption.

The completion of each cycle is celebrated with a worldwide siyum, a joyous celebration.

## The three study tracks

1. **Three chapters a day:** finishes the entire Mishneh Torah in about one year.
2. **One chapter a day:** finishes it in about three years.
3. **Sefer Hamitzvot:** a short daily study of the Rambam's Book of Mitzvot, which lists the [613 mitzvot](/learn/613-mitzvot). It covers the same topics as the one-chapter track and is designed for everyone, including children.

## How to join

1. **Choose a track** that fits your time. Sefer Hamitzvot takes just a few minutes.
2. **Find today's portion** in a Jewish calendar or study app.
3. **Use a translation** with clear explanations.
4. **Learn with others,** in a class or with a partner.

## Common questions

### Do I need to know Hebrew?
No. Fine English translations of the Mishneh Torah and Sefer Hamitzvot are available. Many learn in English and look at the Hebrew as they grow.

### Is the Rambam the final word on Jewish law?
The Rambam is one of the greatest authorities. Practical Jewish law today is usually decided according to the Shulchan Aruch and its commentaries, which draw heavily on the Rambam. For practical questions, ask the Rabbi.

### What is the difference between this and Maimonides?
They are the same person. This guide focuses on the daily study cycle. Our guide to [Maimonides](/learn/maimonides) tells more about his life and other works.

### Can children join?
Yes. The Sefer Hamitzvot track was meant for children and adults alike. Many families learn it together.
