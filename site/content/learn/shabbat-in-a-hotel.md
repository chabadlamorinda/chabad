Keeping Shabbat in a hotel is very doable with some preparation. Tell the hotel your needs ahead of time, ask for a room with a regular key, bring your food and candles, and ask your rabbi about the details that apply to you.

## Before you go

Chabad.org recommends contacting the hotel early to flag possible issues, since staff are usually accommodating once they understand. Ask for:

- **A regular key.** Key cards cannot be used on Shabbat. Request a metal key, or arrange for staff to open the door before Shabbat.
- **A lower floor,** because elevators pose many challenges. If the hotel has a "Shabbat elevator," ask your rabbi about using it.
- **Continuous hallway lights,** if motion sensors would otherwise turn them on.
- **A spare key** if your room needs a card in a slot for power.

## Sensors and doors

Many sensors can be covered with paper or a similar item before Shabbat. For automatic doors, ask for a regular door such as an employee entrance. For electronic locks and other specifics, ask your rabbi; practices differ.

## What to bring

- Candles (see below) and matches
- Challah, grape juice or wine, and food that needs no cooking
- Printed Kiddush and Grace after Meals

See [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles) and [kosher when traveling](/learn/kosher-when-traveling). Chabad.org says to light candles even while traveling. Hotels may restrict open flames, so ask the hotel and the Rabbi what to do.

## Friday checklist

1. Check [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times) for your destination.
2. Cover sensors, set up the door and leave the lights as you want them.
3. Prepare the table, candles and food.
4. Light candles on time, then enjoy Shabbat.

## Carrying and the minibar

Chabad.org notes that most authorities are lenient about carrying inside a single hotel building, but carrying outdoors between buildings needs an eruv. Minibars with sensors are a problem. When in doubt, ask. See [traveling on Shabbat](/learn/traveling-on-shabbat).

## Common questions

### Can I use a key card on Shabbat?
No. Key cards are not used on Shabbat, so request a regular key or have staff open the door beforehand.

### Can I use the elevator on Shabbat?
It is complicated, so request a lower-floor room, and ask your rabbi about a "Shabbat elevator."

### Should I light candles in a hotel room?
Yes, light even while traveling. Check the hotel's rules and ask the Rabbi.

### What do I bring for Shabbat in a hotel?
Candles, challah, wine or grape juice, food, and printed Kiddush and Grace after Meals.
