Many families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda bring together a Jewish parent and a parent who is not Jewish. If that is your family, you are welcome at Chabad of Lamorinda. Come to Shabbat, bring the kids to a holiday, sit in on a class or simply call to talk. There is no membership, no dues and no judgment.

## Chabad's approach: every Jew is welcome

Chabad follows the way of the Lubavitcher Rebbe: joy, learning and unconditional love for every Jew. Nobody is "too far" or "not Jewish enough." We do not ask about your background at the door, and we do not expect you to know the prayers or the customs. You learn by coming, and questions are always welcome.

Our services are traditional, in a casual and joyful atmosphere, with a bilingual Hebrew and English prayer book. The Rabbi explains as we go and announces the pages. Many people at our services are attending for the first time.

## Talk to the Rabbi

Interfaith families often have real questions: about their children, about the holidays, about a wedding, a bris or a funeral. Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski are glad to meet with you, listen with respect, and explain the Jewish view honestly and warmly. Each family's situation is different, so for questions of Jewish law, ask the Rabbi personally. Call 925-384-7242 or see [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi) and [am I Jewish?](/help/jewish-identity).

## Programs for your kids

Children love to belong. Our programs give them Jewish pride, friends and happy memories:

- [Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool) for ages 12 months to 4 years, a warm, play-based school in Lafayette guided by Jewish values like kindness and respect.
- [Lamorinda Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school), Sundays 10 AM to 12 PM for ages 3 to 13. It is open to all, with no membership required, and no child is turned away for lack of funds.
- [CKids Afterschool](/programs/ckids-afterschool), weekdays 3 to 6 PM for ages 5 to 13, with homework help, sports, Hebrew and a hot meal.
- [Kids and teen events](/programs/kids-and-teens), with favorites like the Model Matzah Bakery, Kids Hamantashen Bake, Make Your Own Menorah and the Havdalah Glow Party.

Thinking about a bar or bat mitzvah? Call the Rabbi to talk about your child. See [bar and bat mitzvah](/help/bar-bat-mitzvah).

## Holidays at home

You do not need to do everything at once. Start with one thing your family can enjoy together:

- **Shabbat:** light candles on Friday before sunset, say Kiddush and share challah. Our guides to [Shabbat for beginners](/learn/shabbat-for-beginners) and [Shabbat with kids](/learn/shabbat-with-kids) walk you through it.
- **Chanukah:** light the menorah in a window each night and share the light. See [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah).
- **Passover:** hold a Seder at home with a Seder-to-go kit, or join the community Seder. See [Passover](/holidays/passover).
- **A mezuzah:** put one up on your front door. Use the [mezuzah form](/forms/mezuzah-tefillin) and we will help.

Need candles, a menorah or a Kiddush cup? Ask about Judaica through [Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher).

## Come as a family

The easiest first step is a Friday night Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family, with Jewish songs and a festive table. Let us know you are coming through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal). Or come to a holiday celebration on the [events calendar](/events).

## Common questions

### Are interfaith families welcome at Chabad of Lamorinda?
Yes. Jews of every background, and interfaith families, are welcome at Shabbat, holidays, classes and events in Lafayette, with no membership and no judgment.

### Can my children join Hebrew school in Lamorinda if only one parent is Jewish?
Lamorinda Hebrew School is open to all, with no membership required. Call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242 to talk about your family.

### Is my child Jewish?
According to Jewish law, Jewish status passes through the mother. Every situation is personal, so please ask the Rabbi. He will talk with you warmly and privately.

### Can an interfaith couple talk to the Rabbi about a wedding or a baby?
Yes. The Rabbi is happy to meet with you and have an honest, caring conversation about your situation. Call 925-384-7242.

### My spouse is not Jewish. Can we come to Shabbat dinner together?
Please do. Let us know you are coming through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) or call 925-384-7242.
