You can talk to a rabbi in Lafayette by calling Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski of Chabad of Lamorinda at 925-384-7242, or by texting or using WhatsApp. Every Jew in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and the East Bay is welcome to ask, it is private, and no membership is required.

## What can I talk to a rabbi about?

A rabbi is a teacher and guide, and no question is too small or too big. People reach out about:

- **A Jewish question**: a holiday, Shabbat, keeping kosher, a blessing, a custom. See [what is a rabbi](/learn/what-is-a-rabbi).
- **A hard time**: illness, loss, a family conflict, or a decision weighing on you. Our [help pages](/help) are a good place to start.
- **A life event**: a baby, a bar or bat mitzvah, a wedding, a funeral. Start with [life events in Lamorinda](/learn/life-events-in-lamorinda).
- **Your home**: putting up a [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) or checking one you already have.
- **Your Jewish background**, a name, or a family story. See [who is a Jew](/learn/who-is-a-jew).
- **Faith and big questions**: you do not need to believe anything first. Honest questions are welcome.

## How to reach the Rabbi

1. **Call 925-384-7242** and leave your name and number if he cannot answer.
2. **Text or WhatsApp.** The buttons on [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi) open a message with a few words already written for you.
3. **Ask to meet.** The same page lets you say what you would like to talk about, so the conversation starts well.
4. **Write to us** through the [contact page](/contact).

For private questions to the Rebbetzin, Tzipora Labkowski, the page has a Talk privately option as well.

## Is it private?

Yes. A conversation with a rabbi is a confidence, and you can say as much or as little as you wish. You do not need to give your whole story in the first message.

## Do I have to be a member?

No. Chabad of Lamorinda welcomes every Jew, whatever your background, level of knowledge or practice. You do not need to belong to anything, and you do not need to pay to ask a question.

## What if I don't know what to ask?

That is common. Say, "I'd like to meet and learn something," and the Rabbi will take it from there. Many people begin with one small question and find a friend. If you are new to the area, read [start here: Jewish Lamorinda](/learn/start-here-jewish-lamorinda).

## Common questions

### How do I talk to a rabbi near me?

Call Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or use the Call, Text and WhatsApp buttons on the Talk to the Rabbi page. He serves Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and the East Bay.

### Can I ask a rabbi a question if I am not religious?

Yes. Every Jew is welcome, whatever your background or practice, and you can ask anything.

### Do I need to be a member to talk to the Rabbi?

No. There is no membership, and asking a question is free.

### Can I text the Rabbi?

Yes. You can text or use WhatsApp, and the Talk to the Rabbi page prepares a short message for you.

### Is what I say kept private?

Yes. A conversation with the Rabbi is private, and you decide what to share.

### Can I talk to the Rebbetzin instead?

Yes. Women and families often prefer to speak with Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski, and the Talk to the Rabbi page offers a private option for her too.
