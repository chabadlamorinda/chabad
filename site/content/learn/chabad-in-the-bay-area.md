A Chabad House is a Jewish community center led by a rabbi and his wife, known as shluchim (emissaries), who move to a place to serve every Jew who lives there. Across the Bay Area, local Chabad Houses are each a little different, because each is shaped by its own community. Chabad of Lamorinda, in Lafayette, is the one for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## What is a shliach?

Shliach means "emissary." Chabad families are sent, guided by the teachings of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, to wherever Jews live: big cities, suburbs, college towns and small communities. A shliach and his wife build a Jewish home that anyone can walk into, with Shabbat meals, classes, holidays, a school or preschool, and a listening ear. Chabad.org describes a Chabad House as a Jewish community center in the truest sense, serving the whole community from children to seniors.

Chabad.org reports roughly 4,900 emissary families running about 3,500 institutions in 100 countries and territories. For more on the movement, see [What is Chabad?](/learn/chabad) and [the Rebbe](/learn/the-rebbe).

## Chabad comes to the Bay Area

Chabad.org tells that Rabbi Chaim Itche Drizin came to the Bay Area as an emissary in 1971, and that he helped found Chabad's presence in Berkeley and San Francisco in the early 1970s. In 1975, on the first night of Chanukah, more than a thousand people gathered in San Francisco's Union Square for what is considered the first large public menorah lighting of modern times. Rabbi Drizin led the idea, together with rock promoter Bill Graham, and the menorah was a 25-foot structure of mahogany. Today public menorah lightings take place around the world. Read about [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah) and the [Chanukah menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah).

## What every Chabad House has in common

Every Chabad House works from the same few ideas:

- **Every Jew is welcome.** No membership is required, and no one needs any background.
- **One mitzvah at a time.** Chabad teaches that every mitzvah counts. See the [ten mitzvah campaigns](/learn/ten-mitzvah-campaigns).
- **A warm Shabbat table.** Hospitality, or [hachnasat orchim](/learn/hachnasat-orchim), is at the heart of it.

We cannot speak for what other Chabad Houses offer, and each one runs its own programs. Check with your local one.

## Chabad of Lamorinda

Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski lead Chabad of Lamorinda at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette. Families come from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, and nearby Pleasant Hill and Martinez. There are Shabbat services and a Kiddush lunch, [Lamorinda Jewish Preschool](/programs/jewish-preschool), [Hebrew School](/programs/hebrew-school), [adult classes](/programs/adult-education), the [Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle) and the [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club). There are no dues. Bay Kosher, a project of Chabad of Lamorinda, delivers kosher food and Judaica ([Bay Kosher](/programs/bay-kosher)).

## Common questions

### What is a Chabad House?
It is a Jewish community center run by a Chabad rabbi and his wife, who serve the Jews of their area with Shabbat, holidays, classes and support.

### What does shliach mean?
Shliach is Hebrew for "emissary." Chabad rabbis and their families are sent to communities around the world as the Rebbe's emissaries; the plural is shluchim.

### Do I have to be religious to go to a Chabad House?
No. Every Jew is welcome, whatever their background, with no membership and no dues.

### Is there a Chabad in Lafayette?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda is at 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette, serving Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda. Call 925-384-7242.

### Who founded Chabad in the Bay Area?
According to Chabad.org, Rabbi Chaim Itche Drizin came as an emissary in 1971 and helped establish Chabad in Berkeley and San Francisco.
