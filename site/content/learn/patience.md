Traffic on Highway 24, a toddler who will not put on shoes, a prayer that seems unanswered. Patience is tested every day. Judaism sees it not as a personality trait some people are born with, but as a quality anyone can grow, and one of the keys to a peaceful life.

## Patience is G-dly

When G-d revealed His Thirteen Attributes of Mercy to Moses, one of them was "erech apayim," slow to anger, literally "long of face": patient. The Sages taught that we should walk in G-d's ways: just as He is patient, we try to be patient. Every time we hold back an impatient word, we are acting in a G-dly way.

## Hillel's patience

The Talmud tells how two men bet that they could make the great sage Hillel lose his temper. One of them came to his door on a Friday afternoon, as Hillel was getting ready for Shabbat, and asked a string of silly questions. Hillel answered each one gently. The man lost his bet. The Sages concluded: "A person should always be gentle and humble like Hillel, and not short-tempered."

## Patience with ourselves

Growth takes time. The Tanya teaches that we change little by little, day by day, and that a moment of struggle is not failure; it is exactly where growth happens. Start one mitzvah, keep at it, and be kind to yourself.

## Patience with G-d

Trusting that things will unfold in their time is part of [bitachon](/learn/bitachon). Abraham and Sarah waited decades for a child. Waiting is not wasted; often it is preparation.

## Practical ways to grow patience

- **Pause before you answer.** A breath, or counting to five, can change a conversation.
- **Lower the volume.** A soft answer turns away anger, says King Solomon.
- **Expect delays.** Leave ten minutes earlier; the traffic stops being an enemy.
- **Look for the good.** Ask what the other person might be going through.
- **Say Modeh Ani** in the morning and start the day grateful; gratitude makes room for patience.

## Common questions

### Is it wrong to feel impatient?
Feelings come and go. What matters is what we do with them. Noticing impatience and choosing a calm response is exactly the work.

### How do I stay patient with my children?
Rest, realistic expectations, and remembering that children grow slowly help a lot. See [Jewish parenting](/learn/jewish-parenting), and [anger](/learn/anger-jewish-view).

### Is there a prayer for patience?
You can ask G-d in your own words before a hard conversation or a long day. See [talking to G-d in your own words](/learn/talking-to-g-d-in-your-own-words).
