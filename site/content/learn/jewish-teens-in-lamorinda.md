Being a Jewish teen in Lamorinda can feel lonely at times. Your school may have only a few Jewish students, and between classes, sports and college applications, there is not much time. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette is a place where Jewish teens from Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda can belong, ask real questions and have fun, with no membership required.

## Teen programs and events

Our teen programs are open to students from **Acalanes High School** and **Stanley Middle School** in Lafayette, **Campolindo High School** and **Joaquin Moraga Intermediate** in Moraga, and **Miramonte High School** and **Orinda Intermediate** in Orinda.

Past teen programs have included **Krav Maga for Teens** and **the Rabbi's Kitchen** for teens. Check the [kids and teen events page](/programs/kids-and-teens) and the [events calendar](/events) for what is coming up, or call the Rabbi to ask.

## Bar and bat mitzvah, and after

For 12 and 13 year olds, bar and bat mitzvah preparation is personal, with the Rabbi and Rebbetzin, including tefillin and Torah reading for boys and the Bat Mitzvah Club for girls. See [bar and bat mitzvah in Lamorinda](/learn/bar-bat-mitzvah-in-lamorinda).

A bar or bat mitzvah is a beginning, not a graduation. Teen boys are welcome to put on [tefillin](/learn/tefillin) with the Rabbi, and teen girls can take on lighting [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles). Each mitzvah is a way of saying, "This is who I am."

## Volunteer and lead

Teens make a real difference here. Sign up on the [volunteer form](/forms/volunteer) to help with holiday events, setting up the sukkah, baking challah, Kiddush setup, teaching or tutoring younger kids, or photography. Ask whether your hours can count toward school community service requirements.

## Shabbat and the holidays

You are welcome at Shabbat services on Saturday at 10 AM, with a hot Kiddush lunch after, and at Friday night dinner with the Rabbi's family. Bring a friend. [RSVP for Shabbat](/forms/shabbat-meal). Holidays are some of the best times to come: dancing with the Torah on [Simchat Torah](/holidays/simchat-torah), the [Purim](/holidays/purim) party, and the Lag BaOmer BBQ in Lafayette. See [the Jewish year in Lamorinda](/learn/jewish-year-in-lamorinda).

## Big questions

Teens ask the best questions: about G-d, Israel, antisemitism, and what being Jewish really means. The Rabbi loves them. Come to a [class](/programs/adult-education), [ask the Rabbi](/ask) online, or set up a time to talk. Our guides to [Jewish pride](/learn/jewish-pride) and [Israel and the Jewish people](/learn/israel-and-the-jewish-people) are a good start.

## Heading to college

Graduating soon? Read our guide for [Jewish college students](/learn/jewish-college-students). And if you stay close to home, Jewish students at Saint Mary's College in Moraga and Diablo Valley College in Pleasant Hill are always welcome for Shabbat dinner and the holidays.

## For parents

Want your teen more connected? Call the Rabbi at 925-384-7242. He will gladly meet your teen, one-on-one or with friends.

## Common questions

### Are there Jewish teen programs in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda's teen programs are open to students from Acalanes, Campolindo and Miramonte high schools and the middle schools in all three towns. Call 925-384-7242 to hear what is coming up.

### Can teens volunteer at Chabad of Lamorinda?
Yes. Teens help with holiday events, the sukkah, challah baking, Kiddush setup and tutoring. Sign up on the [volunteer form](/forms/volunteer).

### My teen never had a bar or bat mitzvah. Is it too late?
Never. Call the Rabbi to talk about preparation at any age.

### Can my teen bring friends?
Yes. Friends are welcome at Shabbat, holidays and teen events.
