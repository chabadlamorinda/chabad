Shabbat is often pictured as a big family table. But many people live alone: students, singles, widows and widowers, people whose children have grown and moved away. Shabbat is just as much yours. It is a gift for every Jew, and it can be deeply peaceful and beautiful on your own.

## Making it special

- **Prepare a little.** Set the table with a white cloth, your nicest plate and two candles. Buy flowers or a small challah. Shabbat begins in the preparation.
- **Light candles.** Every Jewish woman lights; a man who lives alone lights too. Say the blessing and take a quiet moment for a personal prayer. See [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles).
- **Make Kiddush** over wine or grape juice, then the blessing on challah. See [Kiddush](/learn/kiddush).
- **Eat a real Shabbat meal**, even a simple one: soup, chicken or fish, something you love. Eat slowly. Sing a song you know, or read the weekly parsha.
- **Rest.** Put the phone away, read a good book, take a walk, nap. Shabbat is a day to stop and simply be.
- **Say Havdalah** on Saturday night to end Shabbat with light and spices.

## When you want company

Being alone on Shabbat should be a choice, never a sadness. Chabad houses around the world, including ours, keep a seat at the table for anyone who wants one. Come for Kabbalat Shabbat and dinner, join Shabbat morning services and the Kiddush, or ask the Rabbi to connect you with a family who would love a guest. Inviting someone else who lives alone is a beautiful mitzvah too. See [being a Shabbat guest](/learn/shabbat-guest-guide).

## Common questions

### Do men who live alone light Shabbat candles?
Yes. In a home without a woman to light, a man lights the Shabbat candles himself, with the blessing.

### Do I make Kiddush if I am eating alone?
Yes. Kiddush and the blessing on challah are said by everyone, alone or with others.

### I feel lonely on Shabbat. What can I do?
Reach out: call the Rabbi or the Rebbetzin and come for a meal. You are part of a community. See [feeling lonely](/learn/feeling-lonely).
