You can name a synagogue in your living trust by asking your attorney to add a charitable gift to the trust, naming the organization with its name and tax ID; for us the name is "Chabad of Lamorinda." Chabad of Lamorinda accepts legacy gifts through a trust, and such a gift helps keep Jewish education and Jewish life strong in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## How a gift in a trust works

A living trust is a legal document in which you place your assets under the care of a trustee, with instructions for what should happen to them. A gift in a will or a trust works like this: you may leave a set dollar amount, a specific asset or a percentage, it costs nothing now, it leaves your cash flow intact, and it can be changed later.

The same three choices described in [How to Leave Money to a Synagogue in Your Will](/learn/leaving-chabad-in-your-will) apply to a trust. The difference is mostly in the paperwork: instead of a will, the instructions are in the trust document.

## If you already have a trust

You do not have to start over. You can ask your attorney to update the trust to add a gift to Chabad of Lamorinda. Your attorney will tell you the right way for your documents. This guide is not legal or tax advice; please ask your attorney or tax advisor.

Questions you may want to bring to your attorney:

- Should the gift be a set amount, a percentage or a share of what remains?
- If a beneficiary passes away before you, who then receives their share?
- Who is the trustee, and do they know about your wishes?
- Should the gift be in memory of someone? See [Giving in Someone's Honor or Memory](/learn/giving-in-someones-honor).

## Common questions

### Can I name a synagogue in my living trust?
Yes. Ask your attorney to add a charitable gift to the trust, naming the synagogue exactly; for us, "Chabad of Lamorinda." Such a gift can be a set amount, a specific asset or a percentage.

### Is a gift in a trust different from a gift in a will?
The choices are similar. The difference is where the instructions are written. Your attorney can explain how your own documents work.

### Can I change my trust after I name Chabad?
Such gifts can be changed later. Ask your attorney how to amend your trust.

### Can Chabad of Lamorinda be one of several beneficiaries?
Yes. You can divide your estate among family and charities. Many people name a percentage, which your attorney can set up.

### What if I do not have a trust?
You can leave a gift in a will, or use a beneficiary form for a retirement account or insurance. See [How to Make a Legacy Gift, Step by Step](/learn/legacy-gift-step-by-step).
