Gematria (גִּימַטְרִיָּה) is the Jewish practice of finding meaning in the numerical value of Hebrew letters and words. In Hebrew each letter is also a number, so every word has a total, and words with equal totals are taught to share a hidden connection.

## How the letters count

The [Hebrew alphabet](/learn/hebrew-alphabet) has 22 letters. The first nine are the numbers 1 to 9 (aleph is 1, bet is 2, and so on). The next nine are the tens (yud is 10, kaf is 20), and the last four letters are 100 to 400 (kuf is 100, tav is 400). Hebrew has no separate numerals, so letters have always served as numbers, and that is why many Jewish calendars and books number pages with letters.

To work out a word, add up its letters. The word for "life," chai (חַי), is chet (8) plus yud (10), which is 18.

## Famous examples

- **Chai = 18.** This is why Jews give gifts and donations in multiples of 18. See [the chai symbol](/learn/chai-symbol).
- **Sha-dai = 314.** The Divine Name Sha-dai (שַׁדַּי) is written on the back of the [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah) scroll, and it also stands for "Guardian of the doors of Israel."
- **The dreidel.** The letters nun, gimmel, hei and shin (נ ג ה ש) are 50 + 3 + 5 + 300 = 358, which equals the word Moshiach (מָשִׁיחַ). Chassidic teachers love noticing that the Chanukah top spins toward the Messiah. See the [dreidel](/learn/dreidel).

## Gematria in the Torah and in Chassidic teaching

Gematria is one of the ways the Sages and Rashi explain verses, and Chassidic teachers use it to reveal links between ideas. It is part of the wider study of Kabbalah and the inner dimensions of the Torah; see [Kabbalah](/learn/kabbalah) for an introduction.

## An important rule

In Jewish tradition gematria illustrates and enriches a teaching, but it does not create Jewish law and does not predict the future. Reading it as a code to foretell events is not the Jewish approach. Treat it as a way of seeing beauty in the language of the Torah. Ask the Rabbi if you wish to see how a particular example is used.

## Common questions

### What is gematria?
Gematria is the system of assigning numbers to Hebrew letters and finding connections between words with the same total.

### What is gematria used for?
It is used in Torah commentary, Chassidic teaching and Jewish folk custom to add depth to a word or idea, not to make legal rulings.

### Why is 18 lucky in Judaism?
Because 18 is the value of chai, "life." Many Jews give gifts in multiples of 18. Strictly, it is a symbol of blessing, not luck.

### What do the Hebrew letters stand for as numbers?
Aleph to tet are 1 to 9, yud to tzadi are 10 to 90, and kuf to tav are 100 to 400.

### Is gematria a way to predict the future?
No. Judaism sees it as a tool for understanding the Torah, not for fortune-telling. See [mazal and astrology](/learn/mazal-and-astrology).
