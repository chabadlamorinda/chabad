Every Shabbat at Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, after services end at noon, the whole community sits down together to a hot **Kiddush lunch**. Families from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda and beyond make Kiddush over wine, eat, sing and catch up. It is one of the warmest hours of the week. When you sponsor the Kiddush, you share your occasion with everyone at that table.

## Why sponsor a Kiddush?

Jewish tradition teaches that we mark the important moments of life by sharing them, and by doing a mitzvah in the merit of the person we love. A Kiddush does both. People in our community sponsor the Kiddush for:

- **A yahrzeit**, to honor a loved one's memory and elevate their soul
- **A birthday**, which is a personal Rosh Hashanah
- **An anniversary**, to give thanks for the years together
- **A new baby**, to welcome a new Jewish soul with joy, often on the Shabbat of a baby girl's naming
- **A bar or bat mitzvah**, to celebrate the milestone with the whole community
- **A recovery**, to pray for, or give thanks for, a complete recovery
- **In honor of someone**: a parent, a teacher, a friend

## How it works

1. **Go to the [Kiddush page](/kiddush/).** You can see the coming weeks, and show only the weeks that do not have a sponsor yet.
2. **Pick your Shabbat.** For a yahrzeit, choose the Shabbat before or after the date. Not sure of the Hebrew date? Use the [yahrzeit calculator](/tools/yahrzeit) or the [Hebrew birthday tool](/tools/hebrew-birthday).
3. **Write your dedication**, for example "In memory of..." or "In honor of...'s birthday."
4. **Complete your sponsorship online.**

Your name and dedication are shown on our Kiddush board, the dedication is announced, and the Rabbi mentions it. If you would like to come and say a few words, or say Kaddish for a yahrzeit at services, let us know.

Prefer to arrange it by phone, or have a question about a special date? Call 925-384-7242.

## Come and enjoy it

A Kiddush is most meaningful when you are there. Join us for Shabbat morning services from **10:00 AM to 12:00 PM** at 3477 Golden Gate Way, with the Torah reading, a short talk and children taking part, and then the Kiddush. Invite your family and friends. Everyone is welcome, with no membership needed. See the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).

## Other ways to mark an occasion

A Kiddush is one option among several. On our [sponsor page](/sponsor) you can also:

- **Sponsor a Shabbat dinner**, giving the gift of a Shabbat table to families, students and newcomers
- **Sponsor a Torah class**, dedicating a week of learning in someone's merit
- **Sponsor a children's program**, such as Hebrew School, CKids or a holiday program
- **Give a preschool scholarship** to help a child attend Lamorinda Jewish Preschool
- **Sponsor a holiday program**, from Chanukah to Passover
- **Give tzedakah** in their merit, any amount

For a loved one who has passed away, you can also arrange [Kaddish](/kaddish) and add their name to the [Yizkor booklet](/forms/yizkor).

## Common questions

### How do I sponsor a Kiddush at Chabad of Lamorinda?
Go to [chabadoflamorinda.com/kiddush/](/kiddush/), pick an open Shabbat, add your dedication and complete it online. Or call 925-384-7242.

### Can I sponsor a Kiddush for a yahrzeit?
Yes. Many people sponsor the Kiddush on the Shabbat near a yahrzeit. Your dedication honors their memory, and you can say Kaddish at services.

### Do I need to be a member to sponsor a Kiddush?
No. There is no membership at Chabad of Lamorinda. Anyone can sponsor, and everyone is welcome to come.

### Can I share a Kiddush sponsorship with another family?
Call 925-384-7242 and the Rabbi will be glad to help you arrange what works best.
