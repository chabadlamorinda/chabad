A farbrengen (פארברענגען, Yiddish for "to spend time joyfully together") is an informal Chassidic gathering where people share Torah thoughts, sing melodies and encourage one another to grow. L'chaim (לחיים) means "to life," the toast said when a small cup is raised.

## What happens at a farbrengen

Chabad.org describes a farbrengen as an informal gathering where people share Torah, sing melodies and enjoy light refreshments. It can mark a birthday, a life-cycle event or a holiday, or take place with no special occasion.

A typical farbrengen includes:

- **Niggunim.** Wordless melodies that stir the heart. See [niggun](/learn/niggun).
- **A teaching or a story.** Often from [Chassidus](/learn/chassidus).
- **Honest talk.** Friends gently encourage each other to take a small step forward.
- **A l'chaim.** A small toast, raised together.

## Why "to life"?

L'chaim reminds us that Judaism chooses life, joy and connection. At a farbrengen it is a way to open the heart, not to party. Chabad.org's guidance is to drink only when the group toasts and to drink sparingly, never reaching the point of acting foolishly. A non-alcoholic l'chaim, such as juice, is perfectly fine. If you would like to know more, see [kosher beer and spirits](/learn/kosher-beer-and-spirits).

## The Rebbe's farbrengens

The Lubavitcher Rebbe held farbrengens on Shabbat and holidays in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, where thousands gathered. Chabad.org tells of one where the Rebbe spoke for about three hours, and the Chassidim raised small cups of wine between talks. One message that day: build communities even before you have perfect individuals.

## What to expect as a guest

- No formal dress. Men cover their heads and women dress modestly.
- No assigned seats. Take any open spot.
- Eat the food. It is meant to be enjoyed.
- Men and women usually gather separately.
- Chassidic custom is that others pour your drink for you.
- Hum along. Leave during a lull in the conversation.

## Common questions

### What is a farbrengen?
A farbrengen is an informal Chassidic gathering of Torah, song, stories and friendly encouragement to grow.

### What does l'chaim mean?
L'chaim means "to life." It is the Jewish toast.

### Why do Jews say l'chaim?
To affirm life, joy and connection, usually when raising a small cup.

### Do I have to drink alcohol at a farbrengen?
No. Juice is fine, and moderation is always expected.

### What should I wear to a farbrengen?
Nothing formal. Men cover their heads and women dress modestly.
