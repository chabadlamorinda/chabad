Samuel the prophet (שְׁמוּאֵל הַנָּבִיא, Shmuel HaNavi) was the son of Hannah and Elkanah, the last of the judges of Israel, and the prophet who anointed both King Saul and King David. His name means "G-d heard," because he was the answer to a mother's prayer.

## Born from a prayer

Samuel was born in Ramah to Elkanah, a respected Levite, and to Hannah, who had long been childless. At the Sanctuary in Shiloh, Hannah prayed from the depths of her heart and vowed that if she had a son she would dedicate him to G-d. She named him Shmuel, "G-d heard." Her prayer is a model for all Jews. See [Hannah and her prayer](/learn/hannah-and-her-prayer) and [talking to G-d in your own words](/learn/talking-to-g-d-in-your-own-words).

## Growing up in Shiloh

When he was young, Hannah brought Samuel to Shiloh to learn Torah under Eli the High Priest. Chabad.org notes that Eli's sons did not follow the Torah, and that Shiloh was later destroyed by the Philistines. Samuel grew up in the Sanctuary, serving and learning.

## "Here I am"

G-d revealed Himself to Samuel at an early age, and Samuel understood that he carried a great responsibility. The Bible tells of a night in which he hears a voice calling him; at first he thinks it is Eli, and he answers, "Here I am" (*hineni*). The story teaches us to listen for G-d's call. See [Tanach](/learn/tanach).

## A leader of the people

Samuel gathered a group of prophets in Ramah to teach Torah and turn the people away from idolatry. He traveled from town to town, urging people to live by G-d's commandments. Under his leadership the tribes drew closer, idolatry declined, and the Jewish people drove back the Philistines and recovered lost towns. He took no payment for his work.

## Saul and David

The people demanded a king like other nations. Samuel was reluctant, because G-d is the true King, but he anointed Saul. Later Saul disobeyed G-d in the war against Amalek, and Samuel told him the kingdom was taken from him. G-d then directed Samuel to anoint David, son of Jesse. David became Samuel's disciple, continuing the chain of Torah tradition from Moses. See [King David](/learn/king-david).

## His yahrzeit

Samuel died in Ramah, where he was buried, and the whole nation mourned him. Chabad.org gives his yahrzeit as 28 Iyar.

## What we can learn

- **Prayer is heard.** The child who answers a mother's prayer reminds us that no sincere prayer is wasted.
- **Say "Here I am."** Be ready when a chance to do good appears.
- **Lead with integrity.** He took no payment, and the people trusted him.

## Common questions

### Who was Samuel the prophet?
Samuel was the son of Hannah, the last of the judges and the prophet who anointed Saul and David.

### What does the name Samuel mean?

After he was weaned, Hannah brought him to Shiloh to learn Torah with Eli the High Priest.

### Who did Samuel anoint?
He anointed Saul as the first king, and later David.
