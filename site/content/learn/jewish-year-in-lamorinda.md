The Jewish year is a journey, and in Lamorinda you do not have to travel it alone. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette celebrates every holiday with families from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez. There is no membership and no tickets, whatever your background. Here is what each season looks like here, with a link to each holiday page for dates and details.

## Fall: the High Holidays and Sukkot

**[Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah)** opens the year. Our services come with explanations and page announcements, so everyone can follow. We hear the shofar together, gather at the Lafayette Reservoir for **Shofar and Tashlich**, and sit down to a **community Rosh Hashanah dinner** in Lafayette. Kids make dot cakes and more.

**[Yom Kippur](/holidays/yom-kippur)** services are held at the **Lafayette Park Hotel**, from Kol Nidre to Neilah, with the Rabbi's explanations along the way. Add your loved ones' names to the [Yizkor booklet](/forms/yizkor), and stay for the break-fast. Save your seat for both holidays with the [High Holiday reservation form](/forms/high-holidays).

**[Sukkot](/holidays/sukkot)** brings a community dinner in the sukkah, Sushi in the Sukkah for families and Scotch and Steaks in the Sukkah for the [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club). [Order a lulav and etrog](/forms/lulav-etrog), or come shake one at our sukkah any day of the holiday. Then we dance with the Torah on **[Simchat Torah](/holidays/simchat-torah)**, with flags and treats for the children.

## Winter: lights and trees

On **[Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah)** we gather for a grand menorah lighting and community celebration with latkes, donuts and music, and kids make their own menorah. You can also be a Chanukah lamplighter by sponsoring a night of the community menorah. In the heart of winter, **[Tu BiShvat](/holidays/tu-bishvat)** is our family celebration of the New Year for Trees.

## Spring: Purim to Shavuot

**[Purim](/holidays/purim)** is the happiest day of the year: Megillah readings, a Purim party for the whole community and a kids' hamantashen bake. Costumes encouraged!

For **[Passover](/holidays/passover)**, reserve a place at the community Seder, or bring the Seder home with a Seder-to-go kit and handmade shmurah matzah (both on the [matzah order form](/forms/matzah)). [Sell your chametz](/forms/sell-chametz) online through the Rabbi, and bring the kids to the Model Matzah Bakery.

**[Lag BaOmer](/holidays/lag-baomer)** means a BBQ in Lafayette. Then on **[Shavuot](/holidays/shavuot)** the whole family comes to hear the Ten Commandments, followed by an ice cream and cheesecake party.

## Summer

**[Tisha B'Av](/holidays/tisha-bav)** is the fast day mourning the Holy Temples. Call the Rabbi to hear how we mark it this year.

## Every week of the year

Between the holidays there is Shabbat. Join us Saturday at 10 AM for services and a hot Kiddush lunch, or for a Friday night dinner with the Rabbi's family. See [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda) and the [holiday calendar](/holidays) for candle lighting times.

## Be part of it

Holidays in Lamorinda are built by the community. [Volunteer](/forms/volunteer) to set up the sukkah or help at an event, [sponsor a holiday program](/sponsor), or simply bring a friend. Every holiday is open to everyone.

## Common questions

### Where are High Holiday services in Lafayette?
Chabad of Lamorinda holds Rosh Hashanah services, Shofar and Tashlich at the Lafayette Reservoir, and Yom Kippur services at the Lafayette Park Hotel. No tickets or membership are needed. [Reserve here](/forms/high-holidays) to help us save you a seat.

### Is there a public menorah lighting in Lamorinda?
Yes. Our Chanukah celebration includes a grand menorah lighting. The date and place are posted on the [Chanukah page](/holidays/chanukah) and the [events calendar](/events).

### Can I join a Passover Seder near Moraga or Orinda?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda hosts a community Seder for the whole area, and Seder-to-go kits for your home. Reserve through the [Passover page](/holidays/passover).

### How do I hear about holiday events?
Sign up for the [weekly email](/forms/newsletter) or check the [events calendar](/events).
