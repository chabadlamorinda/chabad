What happens to a donor-advised fund after you die depends on the form you filled in with the fund: you can name a charity as the beneficiary of what remains, or name a successor who will keep recommending grants. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts grants from donor-advised funds now, and may also be named as a successor or beneficiary of the fund.

## Two choices to ask about

Chabad.org lists a donor-advised fund among the accounts for which you can name a beneficiary, using a "Change of Beneficiary" form from the fund manager. The [legacy page](/legacy) says Chabad of Lamorinda may be named as a successor or a beneficiary of a fund. Funds commonly offer two choices:

- **A beneficiary charity.** The remaining balance goes to the charity or charities you name.
- **A successor.** A person you name, such as a child, takes over the fund and recommends the grants.

Funds have their own rules and forms, so ask your fund which choices it offers. This guide is not legal or tax advice; please ask your attorney or tax advisor.

## Teaching children to give

If you name your children as successors, you also hand them a responsibility: deciding where tzedakah goes. Some families use this to talk about their values together. Jewish tradition teaches children about tzedakah from a young age; see [Children, Money and Tzedakah](/learn/children-money-and-tzedakah). Writing a letter to explain your hopes can help; see [An Ethical Will](/learn/ethical-will).

You may also name Chabad of Lamorinda for a share of what remains, with your children recommending the rest. These are ideas to talk over with your family and advisor.

## Common questions

### What happens to my donor-advised fund when I die?
It goes to the successor or the charities you named on the fund's form. If nothing is named, ask your fund what its rules say.

### Can I name Chabad of Lamorinda as a beneficiary of my donor-advised fund?
Yes. Contact Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski at 925-384-7242 or Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski at 925-384-7241.

### Does a donor-advised fund grant count as maaser?
Yes. Rabbi Labkowski has confirmed that a grant from a donor-advised fund counts as maaser. See [Donor-Advised Funds, Stock and Maaser](/learn/donor-advised-funds-and-tzedakah).

### Can I give from my fund while I am alive?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda accepts donor-advised fund grants now. Call the Rabbi to arrange one.

### Can my children be successors?
Funds offer different choices. Ask your fund.

### Where are the details my fund needs?
On the [legacy page](/legacy), including the tax ID.
