The Torah is G-d's teaching to the Jewish people, given to Moses at Mount Sinai over 3,300 years ago. In its narrowest sense it means the Five Books of Moses, and in its widest sense it includes all of Jewish teaching, from the Bible to the Talmud to the wisdom of today's sages.

## What does the word Torah mean?

Torah comes from the Hebrew root meaning "to teach" or "to guide." It is not just a history book or a book of laws. It is a guide for living, showing how to connect with G-d and make the world a better place.

## The Five Books of Moses

The Written Torah, also called the Chumash (from the Hebrew for "five"), contains:

1. **Bereishit (Genesis):** Creation, Adam and Eve, Noah, and the patriarchs and matriarchs.
2. **Shemot (Exodus):** slavery in Egypt, the Exodus, and the giving of the Torah at Sinai.
3. **Vayikra (Leviticus):** the laws of offerings, holiness and the priests.
4. **Bamidbar (Numbers):** the 40 years in the desert.
5. **Devarim (Deuteronomy):** Moses' final words to the people.

Together with the Prophets (Nevi'im) and Writings (Ketuvim), these form the Tanach, the Hebrew Bible.

## The Written and Oral Torah

At Sinai, G-d gave Moses both the Written Torah and an oral explanation of it. For example, the Written Torah says to bind "these words" on your arm and between your eyes, but only the Oral Torah explains that this means [tefillin](/learn/tefillin) and how to make them.

The Oral Torah was passed down by teachers for centuries and later written down in the Mishnah and the [Talmud](/learn/talmud). Both are essential, like a text and its key.

## The Torah scroll

A Sefer Torah is handwritten by a trained scribe (sofer) on parchment, using a quill and special ink. It contains 304,805 letters, and every one must be perfect. Writing a Torah can take about a year.

Torah scrolls are treated with deep respect. They are kept in the Ark of a synagogue, and we stand when the Torah is carried.

## The weekly Torah portion

The Torah is divided into 54 portions (parshiyot). One is read each Shabbat in synagogue, completing the entire Torah every year. The cycle ends and immediately begins again on Simchat Torah, a holiday of joyous dancing. Shorter readings are also done on Monday and Thursday mornings.

## Why study Torah?

Learning Torah is one of the greatest mitzvot. When you study Torah, you are connecting with G-d's own wisdom. The Rebbe encouraged every Jew, at every level, to set aside regular time for Torah study, even a few minutes a day.

## Common questions

### Who wrote the Torah?

Jewish belief is that the entire Torah was given by G-d to Moses, word for word. This is one of the foundations of Jewish faith.

### What language is the Torah written in?

The Torah is written in Hebrew. Translations exist in nearly every language, and many people study with an English translation side by side.

### Is the Torah the same as the Old Testament?

The Hebrew Bible (Tanach) contains the same books Christians call the Old Testament, though the order and interpretation differ. Jewish understanding of the text is guided by the Oral Torah.
