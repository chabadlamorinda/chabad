Choosing a preschool is one of the first big decisions a young family makes. If you live in Lafayette, Moraga or Orinda and want your child's first school to feel warm, joyful and Jewish, here is what you should know about **Lamorinda Jewish Preschool** in Lafayette, and the questions worth asking on any tour.

## Lamorinda Jewish Preschool at a glance

- **Ages:** 12 months to 4 years, in two classes: **12 to 24 months** and **2 to 4 years**.
- **Hours:** Monday to Thursday 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Friday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
- **Approach:** warm and play-based, inspired by Reggio Emilia. Children learn through hands-on exploration, and are seen as curious, creative and capable.
- **Values:** kindness, respect, compassion, confidence and responsibility. We follow the principle *Chanoch L'naar Al Pi Darko*: teach each child in their own way.
- **Tuition:** $17,000 for the school year, or 10 monthly payments of $1,700, including daily lunch, books, supplies and enrichment. Drop-in care is $22 per hour when space allows. Need-based scholarships are available.
- **Enrollment** for 2026 to 2027 is open.

Full details are on the [preschool page](/programs/jewish-preschool).

## Questions to ask on any preschool tour

Bring this list on every tour, here or anywhere:

1. **How do children spend the day?** Ask to see a daily schedule, and how much is free play, guided activity and time outdoors.
2. **What is the teacher to child ratio, and how long have the teachers been here?**
3. **How do you handle separation in the first weeks?** Good schools have a gentle plan for new families.
4. **What is included in tuition?** Meals, supplies and enrichment make a real difference to the true cost.
5. **How are Jewish values and traditions part of the day?** Ask about Shabbat, holidays, blessings and Hebrew words.
6. **How do you communicate with parents?**
7. **Is there flexibility,** such as drop-in care, scholarships or a summer program?

At Lamorinda Jewish Preschool, please ask any of these directly. The Rebbetzin will gladly answer each one on your tour.
