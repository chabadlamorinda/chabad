Maybe you have not been to a synagogue since your bar or bat mitzvah. Maybe you never had one. Maybe you are not sure what you believe, or you only know a few Hebrew words. You belong at Chabad of Lamorinda.

Jews of every background and level of observance come to us in Lafayette, from Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill, Martinez and beyond, just as they are. There are no dues, no membership and no test. Questions are always welcome.

## Why Chabad welcomes everyone

The Lubavitcher Rebbe taught that every Jew has a spark of G-d within, a spark that can never go out. It does not depend on how you were raised or how much you practice. A Jew is always a Jew. So at Chabad, we do not ask "how religious are you?" We say "welcome, we are glad you came."

Our motto is "Judaism with a smile." Our services are traditional, but the atmosphere is casual and joyful. On Simchat Torah, we dance with the Torah closed, because its joy belongs to everyone, whatever they know.

## What it feels like to walk in

- **No one is watching you.** People of every level sit side by side.
- **You can follow along.** The prayer book is in Hebrew and English, and the Rabbi announces the pages and explains as we go.
- **You will not be the only newcomer.** Many people at our services are attending for the first time.
- **Kids are welcome.** Children take part in the Saturday service.
- **There is food.** After Saturday services, everyone stays for a hot Kiddush lunch.

Nervous? Read about your [first time at synagogue](/learn/first-time-at-synagogue).

## First steps

You do not have to do everything. You do not have to do anything. But if you want to try one thing, here are some easy starts:

1. **Come for Shabbat dinner.** The Rabbi's family hosts a traditional Friday night meal with songs and warm conversation, any week of the year. [Let us know you are coming](/forms/shabbat-meal).
2. **Come on Saturday morning.** Services are 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM at 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette, followed by Kiddush lunch. See the [Shabbat page](/shabbat).
3. **Come to a holiday.** The menorah lighting on [Chanukah](/holidays/chanukah), the Megillah and party on [Purim](/holidays/purim), or the shofar at the Lafayette Reservoir on [Rosh Hashanah](/holidays/rosh-hashanah). High Holiday services need no tickets; [reserving](/forms/high-holidays) just helps us save you a seat.
4. **Try a class.** Sunday 9:00 PM in English, Tuesday 8:30 PM in Hebrew with a meal, or Tanya on Wednesday at 1:00 PM. Every level is welcome. See [adult classes](/programs/adult-education).
5. **Do one mitzvah.** Light Shabbat candles, put up a [mezuzah](/learn/mezuzah), or put on tefillin with the Rabbi. Every mitzvah counts. Read [why mitzvot](/learn/why-mitzvot).
6. **Talk to the Rabbi.** About anything, privately and at no charge. See [talk to the Rabbi](/help/talk-to-the-rabbi).

## Common questions

### Do I have to be religious to go to Chabad of Lamorinda?
Not at all. Jews of every background and level of observance come to Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette, just as they are. There are no dues and no test.

### I don't read Hebrew. Will I feel comfortable?
Yes. The prayer book is bilingual, the Rabbi announces the pages and explains, and many people are there for the first time.

### Is there a synagogue near Moraga or Orinda where I won't be judged?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette is about ten minutes from Moraga and a few minutes from Orinda. Everyone is welcome, with no membership and no judgment.

### Do I need to dress a certain way?
Come as you are. If you are unsure, call 925-384-7242 and ask. We are happy to help.

### What is the easiest first step?
A Friday night Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family. [RSVP here](/forms/shabbat-meal) or call 925-384-7242.
