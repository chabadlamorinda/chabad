Every week, from Friday at sunset until Saturday night, Shabbat turns the busy pace of Lamorinda into a day of rest, family and soul. Whether you live in Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill or Martinez, you have a Shabbat table and a shul at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way in Lafayette. No membership, no dues and no Hebrew needed.

## Friday night: Kabbalat Shabbat

We welcome Shabbat with joyful singing at **Kabbalat Shabbat**, Friday evening at **{kabbalat-shabbat}**. The time may change with the season, so check [this week's Shabbat times](/shabbat) before you come. Candle lighting times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda are posted there every week.

## Shabbat dinner with the Rabbi's family

Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Tzipora Labkowski and their family host Shabbat dinners: a traditional Friday night meal with Jewish songs, warm conversation and a festive table. It is the best way to get to know the Rabbi and Rebbetzin, and you are welcome any week of the year. Just let us know you are coming through the [Shabbat meal form](/forms/shabbat-meal) or at 925-384-7242.

The same form works the other way, too. New in town, a student, or just want company? We can match you with a host. Have room at your own table? Sign up to host guests, and we will make the match.

## Saturday morning: services and Kiddush lunch

Shabbat morning services run **Saturday 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM**: Torah reading, a short talk, and children taking part. The service is traditional, in a casual and joyful atmosphere, with a bilingual Hebrew and English prayer book. The Rabbi explains as we go and announces the pages, so first-timers feel at home. Read our guide to your [first time at synagogue](/learn/first-time-at-synagogue) if you are nervous.

After services everyone stays for a **hot Kiddush lunch**. It is where newcomers meet neighbors from all three towns.

## Getting here

Chabad of Lamorinda is close to downtown Lafayette and the Lafayette BART station. From Moraga it is about ten minutes up Moraga Road or St. Mary's Road. From Orinda it is a few minutes east on Highway 24. See [our area](/our-area) and your town page: [Lafayette](/jewish-lafayette), [Moraga](/jewish-moraga), [Orinda](/jewish-orinda).

## Common questions

### Where can I go for Shabbat services in Lafayette?
Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette. Saturday 10 AM to 12 PM with a hot Kiddush lunch, and Kabbalat Shabbat on Friday evening. Everyone is welcome.

### Is there a Shabbat dinner in Lamorinda for newcomers or students?
Yes. The Rabbi's family hosts Shabbat dinner any week of the year. Students from Saint Mary's College in Moraga and Diablo Valley College are always welcome. [RSVP here](/forms/shabbat-meal).

### When is candle lighting in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda?
It changes every week with sunset. See [this week's times](/shabbat) or the [zmanim tool](/tools/zmanim).

### I don't read Hebrew. Will I be comfortable?
Yes. The prayer book is bilingual, the Rabbi announces pages and explains, and questions are always welcome.

### Can I bring my kids?
Please do. Children are always welcome and take part in the Saturday service.
