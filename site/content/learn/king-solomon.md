King Solomon (Shlomo, שְׁלֹמֹה) was the third king of Israel, the son of King David and Batsheva, famed as the wisest of men and the builder of the First Holy Temple in Jerusalem. He ruled for forty years, an era of peace and prosperity, and is credited with three books of the Bible: Proverbs, Song of Songs and Ecclesiastes.

## A young king asks for wisdom

Solomon took the throne after his father [King David](/learn/king-david) and, according to Chabad.org, was only twelve. At Gibeon G-d appeared to him and asked what he wanted. Solomon did not ask for riches or long life or victory over enemies. He asked for an understanding heart to judge the people. G-d was pleased and gave him wisdom, and also wealth and long life, which he had not requested.

## The judgment of the two mothers

Two women lived in one house, each with a baby. One baby died in the night, and each woman claimed the living child. Solomon called for a sword and ordered the child divided in two. One woman cried out: give the baby to the other, only let him live. Solomon knew she was the true mother and gave her the child. The story is a model of how wisdom reads the heart.

## Building the Temple

Solomon began building the [Holy Temple](/learn/the-holy-temple) in the fourth year of his reign, and it took seven years. It was built of quarried stone and cedar, with no iron tools used on the Temple Mount. At its dedication, in the month of Tishrei, the celebration went on for fourteen days. Afterward he spent thirteen years building his own palace.

## Three books of the Bible

- **Proverbs (Mishlei):** short sayings on how to live well. Its closing poem, [Eishet Chayil](/learn/eishet-chayil), is sung on Friday night.
- **Song of Songs (Shir Hashirim):** a love song understood as G-d's love for the Jewish people, read on Passover.
- **Ecclesiastes (Kohelet):** a search for meaning, read on Sukkot.

## Lessons for today

- **Ask for wisdom first.** Solomon chose understanding over wealth. What we ask for shapes what we become.
- **Judge with the heart.** Justice is more than rules; it listens for what people truly care about.
- **Focus on the light.** Chabad.org cites the Rebbe: focus on good and light, and darkness will disappear on its own.
- **Beware of compromise.** The Bible tells that his many foreign wives drew his heart away, and the kingdom split after his death. Greatness needs vigilance. For the full picture and how our Sages understand it, ask the Rabbi.

## Common questions

### Who was King Solomon in the Bible?
King Solomon was the third king of Israel, son of David and Batsheva, and the builder of the First Temple.

### Why was King Solomon so wise?
He asked G-d for wisdom instead of riches or power, and G-d granted his request.

### What books did King Solomon write?
Tradition credits him with Proverbs, Song of Songs and Ecclesiastes.

### How long did it take Solomon to build the Temple?
Seven years, starting in the fourth year of his reign.

### What is the story of the two mothers?
Two women claimed one baby, and Solomon proposed dividing the child; the woman who gave up her claim to save the child was recognized as the mother.
