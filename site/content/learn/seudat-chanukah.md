Chanukah has no required festive meal the way Purim and the Seder do, but there is a long tradition of gathering for a joyful meal with songs of praise and words of Torah. Opinions differ on how much of a mitzvah the meal is, so ask the Rabbi about the details.

## Is there a special meal on Chanukah?

The only commandment of the holiday itself is lighting the menorah. Eating is a custom: Jewish tradition notes foods fried in oil and dairy dishes, and it encourages hosting a festive meal. Latkes, doughnuts and dairy are customs, not obligations. For the food ideas, see [Chanukah foods](/learn/chanukah-foods).

Still, Jewish teachers have long asked whether a Chanukah meal has religious meaning. The main views, which Jewish law discusses, are:

- **It is optional.** The Shulchan Aruch, the standard code of Jewish law, treats a Chanukah feast as a voluntary meal.
- **It is partly a mitzvah.** The Rema, a leading Ashkenazi authority, mentions a view that there is a partial mitzvah, recalling the dedication of the altar in the desert.
- **It becomes a mitzvah through its content.** Some teach that a meal made special for Chanukah, with praise of G-d and Torah, becomes a "seudat mitzvah" (סעודת מצווה), a meal that is itself a mitzvah.

Because these views differ, we do not say that you "must" have a feast. We say that a meal with songs and Torah is a beautiful way to keep the holiday. If you want to know how this is practiced in your family, ask the Rabbi.

## How to make the meal special

1. **Light the menorah first.** The lights come at the right time, then the meal. See [how to light the menorah](/learn/chanukah-menorah) and the [menorah page](/menorah).
2. **Sing.** chassidic teaching describes families singing after the lighting, from traditional hymns about the menorah and the miracles to lighter songs about the dreidel. See [Chanukah songs](/learn/chanukah-songs) and [Shabbat songs, zemirot](/learn/zemirot) for the idea of singing at the table.
3. **Share a thought.** Tell the Chanukah story ([the Chanukah story](/learn/chanukah-story)), ask each person to share a miracle in their life, or bring one idea from [the lessons of Chanukah](/learn/lessons-of-chanukah).
4. **Say the blessings.** Say the blessing before eating and Grace After Meals. Chanukah's Al HaNisim ("for the miracles") is added to the Grace, so ask the Rabbi or see [blessings after food](/learn/blessings-after-food) for how.
5. **Give gelt and play.** The tradition lists gelt and dreidel among the holiday's traditions. See [gelt](/learn/chanukah-gelt) and [dreidel](/learn/dreidel).

## A Chassidic gathering: the farbrengen

In Chassidic tradition, a joyful gathering of song, stories and honest talk is called a [farbrengen](/learn/lchaim-and-farbrengen). Chabad teaching encourages increasing joy on Chanukah, and many families and communities hold a gathering with song. A farbrengen is not a requirement of the holiday. ## Is there a Chanukah meal for everyone?

Yes: yours. A meal can be simple. Soup, latkes and a sing-along around the kitchen table count. What matters is that it is joyful, that the family is together and that it helps the children feel the holiday. If you have guests, see [hosting a Chanukah party](/learn/hosting-a-chanukah-party). It is a lovely mitzvah for families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda to invite a neighbor who might otherwise be alone.

## Common questions

### Is there a special meal on Chanukah?
Chanukah has no required festive meal. There is a custom to eat oily and dairy foods and to host a joyful meal, and some teach that a meal with songs and Torah becomes a seudat mitzvah.

### What is a seudat Chanukah?
It is a festive meal held in honor of Chanukah. Some authorities say it is only optional, and some see it as a partial mitzvah. Ask the Rabbi for guidance.

### Do I have to say Torah or sing at the Chanukah meal?
No one will check, but singing and a word of Torah are what give the meal its meaning. Even one song and one story are enough.

### Should the Chanukah meal be dairy?
Dairy is a custom recalling Yehudit, not a requirement. Meat is also fine; see [a Chanukah dinner menu](/learn/chanukah-dinner-menu).

### Can I eat before lighting the menorah?
It is best to light at the proper time and then eat. If you are unsure about timing, especially on Friday, ask the Rabbi.

A farbrengen is a Chassidic custom of gathering, and it can happen on any occasion. Call 925-384-7242 to hear what is planned.
