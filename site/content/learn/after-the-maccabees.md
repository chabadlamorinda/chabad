After the Maccabees rededicated the Temple, the Hasmonean family kept fighting for about 25 years until the Jews were fully free. Their family then ruled the Land of Israel for roughly a century, and the story ended badly: the Hasmoneans took the crown, which the Torah reserved for the house of David, and in the end Herod and the Romans took over, leading to the destruction of the Temple.

Most people stop the Chanukah story at the rededication. Here is the rest, and the lesson it leaves us.

## Twenty-five more years of war

Chanukah was not the end of the fighting. The Greek rulers sent more armies. According to Jewish tradition, the battles went on for some 25 years after the rededication, and most of them came after Antiochus had died. Judah the Maccabee, and then his brothers, fought on. Shimon, the last surviving son of Matityahu, finally cleared the remaining Hellenist strongholds, and Jewish independence was achieved. (For the king, see [Who Was Antiochus?](/learn/who-was-antiochus).)

## The Hasmonean dynasty

Shimon became leader of the Jewish people and founded a dynasty that Jewish teaching counts as lasting 103 years. The early rulers were righteous and did not take the title of king. Let us look at a few of them.

- **Yochanan Hyrcanus** was generally a righteous leader, who strengthened tithing. But he also forced the people of Edom to convert, and this is criticized, since Jewish law does not permit forced conversion.
- **Aristobulus**, his son, was the first of the family to call himself king.
- **Jannai** persecuted the Torah sages and killed many of them. Under him, the family was at its lowest.
- **Queen Shlomtzion (Salome Alexandra)**, Jannai's wife, was thoroughly righteous. Her reign brought peace and prosperity and is remembered as one of the best periods of the Second Temple. She returned the Sages to leadership. Among the achievements of the time, the sage Joshua ben Gamla set up a school system for children. This is a link to the theme of Chanukah and education, which you can read in [What Does Chanukah Mean?](/learn/what-does-chanukah-mean).

## The mistake of the crown

Why do we say the story ended badly? The Torah says the kingship belongs to descendants of David, of the tribe of Judah. The Hasmoneans were priests of the tribe of Levi. The tradition writes that taking the crown was a grievous mistake, and many Jewish authorities, among them the Ramban, saw the later fall of the family as the result. (Some sources defend the Hasmoneans, so if you would like to explore this, ask the Rabbi.)

## Herod and Rome

The dynasty ended when Herod, a descendant of converted Edomites, took the throne. Herod murdered the last Hasmonean king, Antigonus, and ruled brutally, killing many Torah sages. He also rebuilt and beautified the Second Temple; the Talmud says no one who had not seen it had seen a beautiful building. Under him Judea was a vassal of Rome.

When Judea later rebelled against Rome, the emperor sent Vespasian and then his son Titus. Jerusalem's walls fell on the 17th of Tammuz, and the Temple was destroyed three weeks later on the 9th of Av. Our Sages teach that the Second Temple fell because of baseless hatred among Jews. You can read about this day in [Tisha B'Av for Beginners](/learn/tisha-bav-for-beginners) and about the era in [The Second Temple Period](/learn/second-temple-period).

## The lesson

The Maccabees won a great victory, and their descendants lost much of what it was for. Victory alone does not protect Jewish life. Victory has to be followed by faithfulness, humility and unity, year after year. We celebrate Chanukah for the light of the Temple, not for the crown, and that is what the Maccabees began with.

## Common questions

### What happened after the Maccabees won?
The war continued for about 25 years, until Jewish independence was secure. Then the Hasmonean family ruled for roughly a century.

### Who were the Hasmoneans?
The Hasmoneans (*Chashmonaim*) were the priestly family of Matityahu, whose sons led the Maccabee revolt. Their descendants led and later ruled the Jewish people.

### Why is the Hasmonean kingship criticized?
They were priests, and the Torah says kings come from the family of David. Many Jewish authorities saw taking the crown as a mistake.

### How did the Hasmonean dynasty end?
Herod, a descendant of converted Edomites, took the throne and killed the last Hasmonean king. Judea later fell under direct Roman control.

### Did the Second Temple stand until the Romans?
Yes. The Second Temple was destroyed on the 9th of Av in the first century of the Common Era. Sources differ slightly on the exact year, so we do not state it.
