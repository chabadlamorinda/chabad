You do not need a background, a membership or perfect Hebrew to study Torah in Lamorinda. Chabad of Lamorinda in Lafayette offers weekly classes for adults of every level, from your very first class to deep Talmud and Kabbalah. Neighbors from Lafayette, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasant Hill and Martinez come to learn, ask questions and enjoy lively discussion. Questions are always welcome.

## The weekly schedule

| Day | Time | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | 9:00 to 10:00 PM | Weekly Torah class, in English |
| Tuesday | 8:30 to 9:30 PM | Torah class in Hebrew, with a meal |
| Wednesday | 1:00 to 2:00 PM | Tanya class, in English |

All classes are at Chabad of Lamorinda, 3477 Golden Gate Way, Lafayette. Check the [events calendar](/events) for any changes, and see the [adult education page](/programs/adult-education) for details.

## Sunday night: the weekly Torah portion

End the weekend with an hour on the weekly [parsha](/learn/weekly-parsha), the Torah portion read in synagogue that Shabbat. The Rabbi brings the ancient text into today's world in a modern, relevant way. Read ahead on our [parsha page](/parsha) if you like.

## Tuesday night: in Hebrew, with a meal

For Israelis and Hebrew speakers in Lamorinda, the Tuesday class is a warm evening of Torah in Hebrew, around a table with a meal. It is a wonderful way to meet other Hebrew-speaking families in Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## Wednesday afternoon: Tanya

The [Tanya](/learn/tanya) is the classic book of Chassidic philosophy, written by the first Rebbe of Chabad. It speaks about the soul, how to handle the struggles inside us, and how to live with joy. A midday hour on Wednesday fits well for retirees, people working from home and parents during school hours.

## Topics we study

Over the year, classes cover the weekly Torah portion, Tanya, Explore the Talmud and practical Jewish living. Past classes have included Kabbalah, a women's class in Hebrew, and community dinners that mix a good meal with Torah, such as Spaghetti, Meatballs and Torah.

## Learn one-on-one

Prefer to learn on your own schedule? Choose any topic and study with the Rabbi at your own pace, or ask him to set up a study partner. Many people start by learning to read Hebrew. See [Hebrew reading for adults](/learn/hebrew-reading-for-adults).

## Keep learning every day

A few minutes a day adds up. Our guide to [daily Torah study](/learn/daily-torah-study) suggests easy ways to start, and you can [ask the Rabbi](/ask) any question that comes up.

## Women's and men's learning

The [Jewish Women's Circle](/programs/womens-circle) brings women together for creative evenings and inspiration, and the [Men's Club](/programs/mens-club) mixes fellowship, learning and a l'chaim, with a chance to put on tefillin.

## Common questions

### Are there adult Jewish classes in Lafayette?
Yes. Chabad of Lamorinda holds weekly classes: Torah in English on Sunday at 9 PM, a Hebrew class with a meal on Tuesday at 8:30 PM, and Tanya on Wednesday at 1 PM.

### Is there a Torah class in Hebrew near Orinda or Moraga?
Yes. The Tuesday class at 8:30 PM in Lafayette is taught in Hebrew and includes a meal. It is a few minutes from Orinda and about ten minutes from Moraga.

### Do I need to know Hebrew or have a background?
No. Classes welcome every level, and the Sunday and Wednesday classes are in English.

### Do I need to sign up or pay?
There is no membership. Just come, or call 925-384-7242 to ask about a class.

### Can I study privately with the Rabbi?
Yes. Ask the Rabbi to set up one-on-one study on any topic, or a study partner.
