For Shabbat or synagogue, wear clean, nice clothes, a step up from your weekday outfit. A guest is always welcome as they are: no one at Chabad checks your outfit. Men cover their heads, and if you need a kippah, just ask when you arrive.

## Come as you are

If this is your first time, wear what feels respectful and comfortable. Business casual is fine. Many people dress up a little, and many do not. What matters is that you came. See [your first time at synagogue](/learn/first-time-at-synagogue) and the [Shabbat guest guide](/learn/shabbat-guest-guide).

## Why we dress up for Shabbat

The Torah calls Shabbat a day "to be declared holy." Our Sages taught that honoring it includes wearing clean, dignified clothing and enjoying good food. They said: "Your Shabbat garment should not be like your weekday garment."

Chabad.org explains that Shabbat clothes honor the day itself, not onlookers. So Jews change into them even when they are spending Shabbat alone. See [Shabbat alone](/learn/shabbat-alone).

## What it looks like in practice

- **Within your means.** Set aside nicer clothes for Shabbat if you can. A jacket or suit that is noticeably better than weekday wear is a nice standard. Clean clothes and polished shoes are a good start.
- **If you do not have special clothes,** wear your usual ones in a more dignified way.
- **Women** are encouraged to change into Shabbat clothes before lighting [Shabbat candles](/learn/shabbat-candles).
- **Men** cover their heads in synagogue; see [the kippah](/learn/kippah).
- **Modesty.** Chabad.org mentions modest tops, skirts or dresses for women at a chassidic gathering. When in doubt, ask.
- **Many people** keep Shabbat clothes on until after Havdalah.

## Common questions

### What should I wear to synagogue?
Wear clean, modest, respectful clothes. Business casual works well. Men cover their heads; ask for a kippah if you need one.

### What should I wear to a Shabbat dinner?
Dress nicely but comfortably. Hosts care about your presence, not your outfit.

### Why do Jews dress up for Shabbat?
To honor the holy day, as the Sages taught that Shabbat clothes should differ from weekday clothes.

### Do I need Shabbat clothes if I am alone?
Yes. They honor the day itself, not the people watching.

### Do I have to dress formally at Chabad?
No. Come as you are. Everyone is welcome.
