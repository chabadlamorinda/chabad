The eighth and last day of Chanukah has a name of its own: **Zot Chanukah**, "This is Chanukah." On the night before, all eight candles burn together, the brightest night of the year. The day itself is seen as the high point and the summary of the whole festival.

## Where the name comes from

Each morning of Chanukah, the Torah reading tells of the gifts the leaders of the twelve tribes brought when the Mishkan, the Sanctuary in the desert, was dedicated. On the eighth day the reading ends with the words "**Zot chanukat hamizbe'ach**," "This was the dedication of the altar." The word Chanukah itself means dedication, and so the day became known by these words.

## The eighth day: beyond nature

Seven stands for the natural world, the seven days of creation. Eight stands for what is above nature. The eighth candle, lit on the eighth night, brings the light of Chanukah to its fullest, a reminder that the Jewish people live not only by the rules of nature but by G-d's miracles, then and now.

## A last chance from the High Holidays

Chassidic and Kabbalistic teaching explains that the judgment of Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur is fully sealed only on Zot Chanukah. The days of Chanukah, and Zot Chanukah most of all, are a time when a prayer, a good resolution or an act of kindness carries special weight. Many people use the day to pray for their families and for anyone who needs a blessing.

## How to make the day count

- **Light all eight candles** the night before, and sit by them for a little while with the family.
- **Say Al Hanissim and Hallel** as on every day of Chanukah. See [Al Hanissim and Hallel](/learn/al-hanissim-and-hallel).
- **Give tzedakah** and add a mitzvah. Chanukah gelt for the children, given with a smile, is part of the joy.
- **Pray for someone who needs it.** It is a good day to ask for blessings.

## Common questions

### When is Zot Chanukah?
It is the eighth day of Chanukah, the day after the eighth night's candles are lit. See this year's dates in our [Jewish holidays calendar](/calendar).

### Do we light candles on the night after Zot Chanukah?
No. Chanukah ends at nightfall on the eighth day, so there is no ninth lighting.

### Is Zot Chanukah a separate holiday?
It is part of Chanukah, the last of its eight days, with no extra prohibitions. It is simply the fullest and brightest day of the festival.
