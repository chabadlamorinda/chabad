Kedushah (קְדֻשָּׁה, "holiness") is a passage in the public prayer service in which the leader and the congregation take turns reciting the angels' praise of G-d, "Kadosh, kadosh, kadosh." It is said in the third blessing of the leader's repetition of the Amidah.

## Where it comes in

The [Amidah](/learn/amidah) is first prayed silently. Then the leader repeats it aloud, and in the third blessing the congregation joins for Kedushah. Chabad.org describes it as a passage recited responsively by the leader and the congregation. It is part of prayer with a [minyan](/learn/minyan). Alone, you do not say it as Kedushah.

## The angels' song

The words come from the visions of the prophets Isaiah and Ezekiel, who saw angels praising G-d: "Holy, holy, holy is the L-rd of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory." Kedushah is our way of joining that song. Read more about [angels](/learn/angels).

## What to do

- **Feet together.** Chabad.org notes that we stand with feet together, like the angels.
- **Rising.** The custom is to rise on the toes at each "Kadosh." Chabad.org explains that this expresses reaching toward G-d while acknowledging that He cannot be fully grasped.
- **Answer.** The congregation says each verse in turn with the leader. If you are not sure where to say it, follow along in a siddur with transliteration.
- **Listen.** At other parts of the Kedushah, listen quietly and respond as the leader prays.

## The three verses

1. "Kadosh, kadosh, kadosh Adonai tzevaot, melo chol ha'aretz kevodo."
2. "Baruch kevod Adonai mimkomo."
3. "Yimloch Adonai le'olam, Elokayich Tziyon, ledor vador, Halleluyah."

Some people rise on their toes at the later verses too. Customs vary, so ask the Rabbi.

## Common questions

### What is Kedushah?
Kedushah is the holiness prayer said during the leader's repetition of the Amidah.

### What does "Kadosh, kadosh, kadosh" mean?
"Holy, holy, holy," the angels' praise of G-d from Isaiah and Ezekiel.

### Why do we rise on our toes during Kedushah?
To imitate the angels, reaching toward G-d while knowing we cannot fully reach Him.

### Why do we keep our feet together?
In imitation of the angels in the prophets' visions.

### Does Kedushah need a minyan?
Yes, it is said with a minyan.
