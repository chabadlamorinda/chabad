Judaism is the faith and way of life of the Jewish people, based on the belief in one invisible G-d and on the Torah and its 613 commandments, the mitzvot. Jews are born into it through a Jewish mother or join it by conversion, and Judaism asks non-Jews only to follow the seven Noahide laws.

## What Jews believe

- **One G-d.** Judaism teaches one invisible Creator without a body. Chabad.org notes that G-d is also called "Hashem," "The Name."
- **The Torah.** The Five Books of Moses, with the Oral Torah in the Midrash and Talmud, are the foundation of Jewish belief and practice.
- **The mitzvot.** The word mitzvah means "command," not just "good deed." The Torah has 613 commandments. Every mitzvah has value, and people can take on more step by step.

## Common myths

**"You can be half-Jewish."** Chabad.org explains that Jewishness passes through the mother. A child of a Jewish father and non-Jewish mother can become Jewish through conversion. Read [who is a Jew](/learn/who-is-a-jew).

**"Judaism is a race."** Jews come from every racial and ethnic background. They are united by covenant and shared responsibility.

**"Jews, Hebrews and Israelites are different peoples."** They are the same people at different times. See [Jews, Hebrews, Israelites](/learn/jews-hebrews-israelites).

**"Only the Ten Commandments matter."** The Ten are fundamental, but all 613 commandments come from G-d.

**"Kosher is a health diet."** Kosher is a Divine command, observed because G-d commanded it. See [kosher](/learn/jewish-foods).

**"Judaism is all or nothing."** Every mitzvah counts, and you can add one at a time. If you do not feel religious, [you are welcome](/learn/not-religious-welcome).

**"Jews are all rich," or "Jewish women are oppressed."** Both are untrue. Jews, like everyone, vary in wealth, and Judaism gives women a central role in passing on Jewish life.

## Judaism and the rest of humanity

Judaism does not seek converts. It asks all people to live by the seven Noahide laws, moral basics such as not murdering or stealing, and Chabad.org teaches that those who do so have a share in the World to Come. Read about the [Noahide laws](/learn/noahide-laws). Judaism welcomes every Jew home, whatever their background.

## Shabbat and the Jewish week

Jews rest from creative work each week on Shabbat, with prayer, festive meals and the remembrance of creation. See [Shabbat in Lamorinda](/learn/shabbat-in-lamorinda) and the [Shabbat times](/shabbat-times) for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda.

## Common questions

### What do Jews believe?
Jews believe in one invisible G-d who created the world, gave the Torah, and asks us to live by its commandments.

### Is Judaism a religion or an ethnicity?
Judaism is both a faith and a people. Jews belong through a Jewish mother or conversion, and come from every background.

### Do Jews try to convert others?
No. Judaism does not seek converts. It welcomes sincere seekers who ask, and asks non-Jews to keep the seven Noahide laws.

### Does Judaism believe in an afterlife?
Yes. The Talmud and Midrash describe it, and resurrection is one of Maimonides' 13 principles.

### Do Jews have to keep all 613 mitzvot?
Many apply only to the Temple, or to certain people. Each mitzvah has value, and you can grow step by step. Ask the Rabbi what applies to you.

### Who is Jewish?
Anyone born to a Jewish mother, or who converted through a rabbinical court.
