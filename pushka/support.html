Common questions

Where is my receipt? Stripe emails a receipt after every gift. Check spam, or tap “Receipt” next to the gift in History.

How do I cancel a monthly gift? More → Profile → Recurring → Cancel.

How do I remove my card or my data? More → Profile → Remove (card), or Delete account.
