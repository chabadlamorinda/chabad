# 30 new ideas, November 1, 2026

The season ahead: Thanksgiving (Nov 26), Giving Tuesday (Dec 1), Chanukah (begins the evening of Dec 4), year-end giving. New on the site this week: step-by-step series of guides, the quiet giving form, lists sorted by what people open, emails to the Rabbi for everything.
[Claude] = Claude can build it with no input from the Rabbi.

## Being found
1. **Each series as its own page** (/learn/series/shabbat...): "how to keep Shabbat step by step" is a common search; one page listing the parts ranks better than ten separate guides. [Claude]
2. **"When is Chanukah 2026?" quick answer** at the top of the Chanukah page, with the dates computed automatically and FAQ markup, so Google shows our page in the answer box. [Claude]
3. **A free candle-lighting widget** other local websites can paste in (PTAs, realtors, Jewish businesses): this week's times for Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda, with a link back to us. [Claude]
4. **A Chanukah article for Lamorinda Weekly**: Claude writes "Chanukah in Lamorinda" (history, how to light, where to celebrate); the Rabbi sends it to the editor.
5. **Russian-speaking Bay Area groups**: the Russian holiday guides are ready to share in Russian Jewish Facebook and WhatsApp groups. Needs the Rabbi (or a volunteer) to post.
6. **Israeli Bay Area groups**: the same with the Hebrew guides, Chanukah first. Needs someone to post.

## Advertising
7. **A Thanksgiving "Jewish thank-you" picture** to share on WhatsApp and Facebook: Modeh Ani and a line on gratitude, with the link to the guide. [Claude]
8. **A window poster for Chanukah**: families print "We light here" with a QR code to /menorah and put it in their window. [Claude]
9. **Library displays in December**: a ready display kit (poster, booklist from the guides, QR code) for the Lafayette, Moraga and Orinda libraries. Needs the Rabbi to ask the libraries.
10. **Ad Grants for the series**: free search ads for "how to sit shiva", "how to keep kosher", "how to start keeping Shabbat", landing on the series. [Claude]

## Sign-ups
11. **"Send me the next part"**: on every guide in a series, readers can get the next part by email once a week, which brings them onto the list. [Claude]
12. **A waiting list when an event is full**: people join it in one tap and get an email the moment a place opens. [Claude]
13. **A family Chanukah blessing card**: type your family's name, get a lovely printable card with the blessings; it can be emailed to you. [Claude]
14. **A parents' monthly email from the kids' corner**: holiday crafts, the next holiday for children and kids' events. [Claude]
15. **"Remind me next year"** after sponsoring a Kiddush in memory: a yahrzeit reminder saved in one tap. [Claude]

## Coming back
16. **Series progress on this phone**: "You've read 3 of 11 Shabbat guides", with the next one ready, on /learn and the home page (kept in the browser only). [Claude]
17. **"Ask about this guide"**: the chat opens already knowing which guide the reader is on, for follow-up questions. [Claude]
18. **A monthly "your Jewish month" email per family**: their yahrzeits, Hebrew birthdays, Rosh Chodesh and the holidays ahead, in one note. [Claude; needs email sending]
19. **Menorah photo wall during Chanukah**: families send a photo of their menorah; the Rabbi approves; it appears on /menorah. Needs the Rabbi's OK.
20. **Thanksgiving table talk**: a printable page of three questions about gratitude from Jewish teaching, for the family table. [Claude]

## Giving
21. **Your giving receipt in My account**: donors download their 2026 year-end letter themselves in January. [Claude]
22. **Tap-to-give page for events**: a QR code at the door opens a phone page with big Apple Pay and Google Pay amounts. [Claude]
23. **IRA gifts at year-end**: a short, plain explanation on /legacy of giving from a retirement account (for people over 70½), with "talk to your adviser". [Claude]
24. **"This holiday was made possible by..."**: holiday-fund donors who agree are thanked by name on the holiday page. Needs the Rabbi's OK.
25. **Monthly Kiddush partners**: a monthly gift that keeps the Kiddush going every week, thanked on the Kiddush board. Needs the Rabbi to set the amount.

## Running it
26. **Where readers stop in each series**: the Rabbi area shows which part of each series people leave at, so we know which guide to improve. [Claude]
27. **Ready replies** in the Kaddish and requests list: one tap fills a warm reply ("I received your request for Kaddish for..."). [Claude]
28. **Keep the site awake**: a free service checks the site every 10 minutes so visitors never wait for Render's free plan to wake up. Needs a free account (cron-job.org), 5 minutes at a computer.
29. **A monthly safety copy of everything**: one download of all RSVPs, forms, donors and subscribers, emailed to the Rabbi on the 1st. [Claude; needs email sending]
30. **A spam guard on the forms**: a hidden field and a time check that stop robots filling in forms, without any puzzle for people. [Claude]
