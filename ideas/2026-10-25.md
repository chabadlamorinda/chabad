# 30 ideas for the website: Sunday, October 25, 2026

Say "do idea 7" (or any number) and Claude will build it.

## Being found first (Google and AI)
1. **Redirect every old chabadoflamorinda.com address** (/3186196, /4970020...) to its new page on launch day, so no Google ranking is lost.
2. **Town pages**: "Jewish Lafayette", "Jewish Moraga", "Jewish Orinda", each with real local content (schools, parks where we celebrate, distances), not copies.
3. **"Near me" FAQ answers**: "Where can I hear the shofar near Walnut Creek?", "Kosher food in Contra Costa", "Bar mitzvah tutor Lafayette". Each answer names the place, day and time.
4. **Weekly Torah thought page** (/parsha) written from the Rabbi's Shabbat talk: fresh content every week is what Google and AI reward most.
5. **Google Ad Grants application**: $10,000 a month of free search ads for 501(c)(3)s. Claude can draft the application and the first ad campaigns.
6. **Google Business Profile posts** for each event, written automatically from events.json.
7. **Reviews page and prompt**: after an event, the RSVP thank-you email asks happy guests for a Google review.
8. **Holiday landing pages for each year** ("Chanukah 2026 in Lafayette") with that year's events, so they rank for the dated searches.

## One account, everything connected
9. **Yahrzeit reminder emails** a week and a day before, with "sponsor a Kiddush in memory" and "would you like an aliyah?" buttons.
10. **Hebrew birthday greetings**: an automatic email from the Rabbi on each family member's Hebrew birthday, with that day's custom (give charity, learn, have an aliyah).
11. **Bar/Bat Mitzvah countdown**: families with a child turning 12 or 13 within 18 months get an invitation to the program.
12. **Family profile photo and "our family" page** in My account, so the Rabbi can greet children by name.
13. **One-tap Shabbat dinner RSVP every week**, with the family's count and food preferences remembered.
14. **Dietary and allergy notes** in the family profile, used automatically for meals and kids' programs.
15. **Pay for events in the RSVP** (Stripe is already connected): seat reservations for High Holidays, Seder and dinners.

## Things every Jewish home needs
16. **Order center**: lulav and etrog, shmurah matzah, Chanukah menorahs, mezuzah checking, all ordered and paid online with pickup or delivery.
17. **Sell your chametz online form**, signed electronically, with the Rabbi's list in the admin area.
18. **Kaddish request form** and a memorial wall (yahrzeit plaques online) for loved ones.
19. **Mezuzah and tefillin checking reminders** (every 3.5 years for a mezuzah), stored per family.
20. **Jewish life-event guides**: "I just had a baby: what now?", "Someone passed away: first steps", "Planning a Jewish wedding", each with a contact button.

## Modern and easy
21. **"Ask the Rabbi" AI helper on the site** that answers from the website's own content (times, events, programs) and hands real questions to the Rabbi.
22. **WhatsApp community link and weekly WhatsApp broadcast** generated from the coming week's events.
23. **Hebrew and Russian versions** of key pages (the community has Israeli families; the Hebrew class is popular).
24. **Weekly email newsletter** built automatically every Thursday from events, Shabbat times and the parsha thought.
25. **Event photo albums**: after each event, its Facebook album appears on the event page.
26. **Live-stream page** for classes (the old site's Zoom link is broken), with recordings of past classes.
27. **Volunteer sign-ups** (set up the sukkah, bake challah, drive a senior) with the same one-tap RSVP.

## Growth and giving
28. **Building Campaign page** with a live progress bar and dedication opportunities.
29. **Sponsor-a-program pages** (sponsor a Shabbat dinner, a Hebrew School day, a holiday), paid online and thanked on the site.
30. **Welcome flow for newcomers**: a "new to Lamorinda?" page plus a 3-email welcome series and an invitation to Shabbat dinner.
