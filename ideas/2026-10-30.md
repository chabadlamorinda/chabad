# 30 new ideas — October 30, 2026

Season ahead: Thanksgiving (Nov 26), Giving Tuesday (Dec 1), Chanukah (first candle Friday Dec 4), year-end giving.
New on the site this week: a photo on every guide, guide cards on events and holidays, the Chanukah quiz, check-in at the door, printable flyers.
**[Claude]** = Claude can build it with no input from the Rabbi.

## Being found
1. **"Start here: Judaism 101"**: a path of 10 guides in order (Shabbat, prayer, kosher, holidays...) with "you've read 3 of 10" on the reader's phone. Google loves it, and so do newcomers. [Claude]
2. **The Rabbi's name on every guide** ("Reviewed by Rabbi Yisroel Labkowski") with a short bio: Google trusts pages with a real expert behind them. Needs the Rabbi's OK and a short bio.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: send the sitemap to Bing, which also feeds ChatGPT and Copilot search. Needs one verification click. [Claude prepares]
4. **"Kids ask"**: a page of real children's questions ("Why do we light 8 candles?") with short answers. Parents share it. [Claude]
5. **Pinterest boards** from the new guide photos (Chanukah, challah, Shabbat table). Free; needs an account.

## Advertising

10. **Table cards for coffee shops and the library** with a QR code to /menorah and the quiz in December. Printing costs a little.

## Sign-ups
