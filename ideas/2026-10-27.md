## Being found (needs a few minutes from the Rabbi)
1. **Google reviews.** Reviews are the #1 factor for "synagogue near me" and Google Maps. Send the Google review link of the Business Profile, and the thank-you email after each RSVP will ask happy guests for a review.
2. **Nextdoor.** Lamorinda is very active on Nextdoor. Every event now has a ready "Copy & open Nextdoor" post; posting each event (and a Chanukah greeting to neighbors) reaches thousands of local families.
3. **Local papers' calendars.** Lamorinda Weekly, Patch and city calendars list community events for free. Each event has a "Copy for local calendars" listing; Claude can draft a short monthly column ("The Rabbi's corner") too, if a paper accepts one.
4. **A 60-second weekly video** (the Rabbi's Shabbat thought, filmed on a phone). Claude writes it up as text for the website and the weekly email; video is what Google, Facebook and Instagram show most.
5. **Partners:** Saint Mary's College (in Moraga) Jewish students, the local senior communities, schools' parent groups, and realtors (a welcome packet with a free mezuzah offer for Jewish families who just bought a home).

## Helping and interacting with more people
6. **"New homeowner" mezuzah offer:** a page and form for families who just moved in: a free mezuzah check or a mezuzah at cost, put up by the Rabbi. Needs: what you want to offer.
7. **Shabbat meal matching, every week:** hosts and guests sign up; the site suggests matches for the Rabbi to approve.
8. **Hospital and care-home visit requests:** a simple form "Please visit my mother at ..." that goes straight to the Rabbi's phone.
9. **Holiday kits to order** (Chanukah menorah kit, Purim baskets, shmurah matzah): ordered and paid online. Needs: what is offered and prices.

12. **Memorial pages** for loved ones, with yahrzeit reminders for the family (needs your OK on approving each page).
13. **Live Zoom classes:** a page with the link and recordings (needs the Zoom link).
14. **"Ask the Rabbi" weekly:** each week publish one real (anonymous) question from the chat with your answer; it becomes a page that Google and AI assistants quote.
